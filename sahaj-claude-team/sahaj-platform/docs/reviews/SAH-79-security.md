## Controls confirmed
1. **Tenant isolation:** every query read binds `tenantId` from `ClinicUser`; no `tenantId` in any body. The new 1720 and 1730 objects read under RLS, have no SECURITY DEFINER, use `search_path = pg_catalog`, and have execute revoked from public and `sahaj_app`. The ledger, payable entries, client requests and decisions are insert-only. Composite tenant foreign keys on the payable tables, the debit note and the ledger.
2. **Authorisation:** every handler goes through the gate, the path ids, the lazy body, then the store check (purchase capability plus Central Store only for PO, receipt and debit note; ADJUST_STOCK; READ_STOCK; RECEIVE_SCHEDULE_X for second signers). The approver read path is limited to a current approver of a PENDING request. In decisions: no self-approval (also in the database), no deciding both levels, the version and level must be current, and the inbox is limited to owners, HODs and delegates.
3. **Schedule X:** pharmacist only; the PIN is checked outside any transaction (`StaffPinService` refuses otherwise); the signer is checked again inside the transaction (active, pharmacist or owner, not the caller); the signer is kept on the Schedule X lines only; the database refuses a signer equal to the actor and, since 1720 and 1730, a person's Schedule X line without a signer. Rate limits: 5 wrong tries per caller per 15 minutes, 10 per day, and 5 per caller-colleague pair. The PIN is never stored or audited; the `SecondSignerInput` and `HandOver` `toString` hide it; the PIN fields are `type="password"`, `autocomplete="off"` and cleared on success and on error.
4. **Money paths:** payables are written once per receipt with an INVOICE entry; a credit is applied only with an entry and cannot exceed the payable (`ck_vendor_payable_credit_within`; 1720 tightens `settled`). Amounts are whole paise, at most 99,999,999,900. Half-up rounding only. Posting refuses negative stock (409, all or nothing).
5. **Availability:** lock order store, request, document, PO, number, batches, balances; balances locked per store in uuid-text order; the expiry sweep uses SKIP LOCKED; deadlocks, lock timeouts and statement timeouts answer 503 `database-busy` (`lock_timeout` 10 s, `statement_timeout` 30 s); paging capped at 50 or 100.
6. **Privacy and logging:** pharmacy logs carry ids, counts and statuses only. Audit rows hold ids, quantities, totals and reason keys. The representative's name is in the audit and the debit-note row, never in a log. No patient data in the SAH-79 tables read.
7. **Input and frontend:** closed bodies and a lazy read after the gate; bind parameters only; line caps of 200, quantity caps and anchored checks; print pages are blob pages with the CSP meta and every value escaped; no `innerHTML`, `bypassSecurityTrust*`, storage or console in `inventory/` or `purchase/`.
