## How the money is tested
Expected values are written in the test from the story and the default rates (General bed ₹800, doctor visit ₹500, nursing ₹300, so ₹1,600 a bill day), never read back from the application. Bill days above 1 use `IpdBillingSupport.backdate` (`NO FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY`, one update, `FORCE` again, in one transaction as `sahaj_migrator`). The three balances use a ₹10,000 deposit: day 10 gives ₹16,000 (collect ₹6,000 = 600000 paise), day 1 gives ₹1,600 (refund ₹8,400 = -840000 paise), day 6 plus a ₹400 dressing gives exactly ₹10,000 (settled). A sign-off dated yesterday is inserted as the table owner (`signed_at` is not writable by `sahaj_app`). Tests that depend on the IST day call `assumeTrue(sameIstDay)` after reading and before asserting.

## Findings (for the owners; no application code was changed by QA)
- **F-27-1 (tech lead decision): sign-off writes one `patient.record_access` row.** The test was written to fail until the java-developer's change. It passes now: the change is already in the code.
- **F-27-2 (CLOSED): the balance the cashier reads could differ from the balance settled.** The request now carries `expectedDepositsPaise`; the server answers 400 on a missing, negative or too large value (field `expectedDepositsPaise`, "Review the final bill and try again") and 409 `bill-changed` when the total or the deposits differ. Tests: `DischargeIT.aDepositAddedBetween...`, `DischargeChecksIT.aDifferenceInTheDepositsAlone...` (the deposits check comes before the refund rule and the mode), `DischargeConcurrencyIT.aDepositRacing...`, e2e `error: 409 bill-changed after a deposit was added meanwhile...`.
- **F-27-3 (CLOSED): `opd.follow_up` accepted another clinic's patient or doctor.** `V2026_11_21_1000__opd_follow_up_tenant_fks.sql` replaced the single-column keys with tenant-composite ones; `FollowUpPort.createForDischarge` in clinic B's context with clinic A's patient or doctor now fails with SQLState 23503 and writes nothing (`DischargeFollowUpIT.thePortCannotReachAnotherClinicsPatientOrDoctorAndWritesNothing` checks the SQLState). The same pattern may exist in other `opd` tables (not checked).
- **Security review M3 (CLOSED in code, tests follow it):** opening the Discharge sheet now writes one `patient.record_access` row (name and diagnosis are shown).
