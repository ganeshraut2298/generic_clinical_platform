## Answer (human, chat, 12 Nov 2026)
1. **Scope: the mockup wins; build everything.** All 17 mockup screens and tools are in scope, in the human's 5-phase order, including the items the BRD marks out of scope for release 1 (body map, 3D dental view, Gynae/Physio/Oncology/Surgery screens with ANC, chemo order sheet and OT booking, treatment courses with part and advance payments, form builder). The brief's line "the BRD wins" is overridden for these items.
2. **SAH-44: rework now** for departments, the full section library, part and order editing, field editing, draft/publish versions and the form builder. SAH-43 (masters) continues unless the rework needs it to change.
   Consequence for the database: the SAH-44 migrations V2026_12_08_1020 (template seed) and V2026_12_08_1030 (tenant tables) are not merged but are already applied in `sahaj_test`. Rework means either editing those unmerged files (then `sahaj_test` must be reset or Flyway-repaired by the human; agents never clean databases) or adding a migration that drops or alters those tables (a destructive change). Either path is a destructive database step and comes back to the human with a concrete plan from the architect and db-engineer before it runs.
3. **Clinical, legal and billing rules: build as drawn in the mockup.** The mockup's values become requirements as shown (chemo BSA dosing and levels, cumulative anthracycline limit, CTCAE grading and the suggested-decision engine, MLC auto-flag and wording, PCPNDT Form F, drug-allergy warnings, child dose by weight, ANC thresholds, growth centiles, part/advance payments, deposits, package rates, MJPJAY/PM-JAY, TPA pre-authorisation). The tech lead recorded that it advised against this for patient-safety rules; every such rule is tagged in the stories and code docs as "value from the EMR mockup, accepted by the human in DEC-039, not clinically reviewed", so it can be found and reviewed later.
4. **WhatsApp, ABHA/ABDM sharing and Marathi/Hindi labels: include now.** This is outside phase 1 as written in CLAUDE.md ("Not in phase 1: integration gateway (ABDM, HL7, payments), notifications (WhatsApp/SMS)") and needs new external services. Opened as DEC-040 (architecture: modules, vendors, credentials, consent, CLAUDE.md change by the human). Marathi/Hindi labels go with the stories; WhatsApp and ABHA stories wait for DEC-040.
