## Problem
A clinic or hospital that wants to run a pharmacy and a store on Sahaj has nowhere to say what its stores are, who works in which store, which medicines and supplies it stocks, who it buys from, who must approve what, how documents are numbered, and what stock it starts with. Nothing in Phase 2 (Inventory) or Phase 3 (Pharmacy) can run until these masters exist: "Nothing else can run without these" (BRD section 1).

Today Sahaj has two separate drug lists that are not a stock master: `opd.medicine` (about 50 common medicines for OPD prescribing, SAH-7) and `ipd.drug` (14 medicines for IPD orders, SAH-31). Neither has strengths per pack, units, GST, vendors or stores. DEC-061 Q3 decided that Pharmacy & Inventory owns its own product master and that the two lists stay unchanged until Phase 3.

## Who uses it (roles) and in which setting (clinic, hospital ward, front desk)
Settings: the clinic or hospital office (setup), the central store, the pharmacy counter and wards (only for choosing a working store in this release).

Role names. The BRD uses eight P&I roles. Sahaj already has eight team roles (owner, doctor, visiting doctor, nurse, front desk, billing, pharmacist, counsellor; `MembershipRole`, SAH-6). A P&I role is the Sahaj team role plus a store role chosen per store. Proposed mapping (OQ-78-6, OQ-78-7):

| BRD role | In Sahaj | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Unit admin | owner | The only person who edits masters in this release. Sahaj has no separate admin role inside a clinic. |
| Owner | owner | Approvals above limits and reports (Phase 2). |
| Pharmacist | pharmacist (existing, DEC-017, DEC-049) | May receive Schedule X (mockup 16 shows "Yes · incl. Schedule X"). Sale and registers come in Phase 3. |
| Cashier | billing (proposed) | Phase 3. Mapped to a Pharmacy store only. |
| Store user | any active team member, store role "Store user" | Nurse, store assistant, pharmacist. |
| Approver (HOD) | owner, doctor, nurse or pharmacist, store role "Approver" | One per store (mockup 16 field "Approver (HOD)"). |
| Delegate approver | same people as Approver, store role "Delegate", with an end date | One per store at a time. |
| Central Store user | any active team member, store role "Central Store user", only at the Central Store | Store keeper. Sahaj has no store keeper or store assistant role: OQ-78-7. |

Who sees what in this release (OQ-78-8): the owner reads and changes every master. Pharmacist and Central Store user can read stores, products and vendors but cannot change them. Everyone else sees no "Inventory setup" tab; they still get the working-store switch if mapped to a store. Front desk, counsellor and visiting doctor get no access unless they are mapped to a store.

## Business rules (numbered)
Scope, tenant, access
- BR-1. One Sahaj clinic account is one P&I unit (OQ-78-9). The unit comes from the signed-in user's session on every call, never from the request. Another unit's data answers 404. The global product catalogue has no unit, is shared, and no unit can change it.
- BR-2. Only the owner creates, edits or switches off a master (stores, mappings, own products, adoption, local settings, vendors, approval rules, numbering, reasons, print templates, unit settings). Pharmacist and Central Store user may read stores, products and vendors (OQ-78-8). Every call checks the role from the clinic membership on the server, not from the screen and not from the token alone.
- BR-3. The P&I entry and its API are available only when the clinic has the Pharmacy & Inventory module on (OQ-78-10). Off: the entry is hidden (not greyed) and the API answers 403 `module-off`.
- BR-4. Nothing in Masters is hard-deleted. Stores, mappings, own products, adoptions, vendors, reasons and numbering series are switched off (inactive) and then cannot be chosen for new work; old records keep their names. Un-adopting or switching off a product that has approved opening stock is refused until Phase 2 gives a way to clear stock (OQ-78-38).
- BR-5. Every create, change and switch-off writes an audit event (who, when, what, before and after values) through the platform audit. Masters hold no patient data, so audit text may include names of stores, products and vendors, but never a patient name, mobile number or ABHA number.
- BR-6. A person whose team access is switched off (SAH-6) loses P&I access at once in the same way (at most 60 seconds), and their store mappings stop counting. Their past records keep their name.

Unit settings
- BR-7. Unit settings hold: unit name (taken from the clinic profile, not re-typed), GSTIN, drug licence number and expiry, Integration mode (Integrated or Standalone), FEFO, and negative stock blocked. Integration mode is a single unit-level switch. Changing it deletes nothing and needs a confirmation that says what will be hidden or shown. Integrated shows OPD and IPD features of P&I when those modules are on; Standalone hides them (hidden, not greyed). Every master works in both modes.
- BR-8. FEFO (first expiry, first out) is on and negative stock is blocked, and neither can be switched off in this release (OQ-78-11), because the BRD states "FEFO everywhere" and "stock never goes negative" as non-negotiable while also listing them as settings.

Stores and staff
- BR-9. A store has a name, a code, a type (Central Store, Pharmacy, Sub-store, Ward store), an issues-from store, "Sale to patients" Yes or No, "Consumption entry" Yes or No, an approver (HOD) and a delegate approver. Name and code are each unique in the unit, ignoring capital letters. A unit has exactly one active Central Store (OQ-78-12); it is the only store that buys from vendors and it has no issues-from store. Every other store issues from the Central Store.
- BR-10. HOD and delegate are active team members mapped to that store. Choosing a HOD or delegate who is not yet mapped maps them with the matching store role. A delegate has an end date (and a start date, OQ-78-15), is a different person from the HOD, and one delegate is active per store at a time. After the end date the delegate no longer approves; the history shows the dates. A store with no active HOD shows "No approver set" and, when the chosen approval rules need an HOD, a warning.
- BR-11. Access is given per store, not per user. A person can be mapped to many stores with a store role in each (Store user, Central Store user, Approver, Delegate), once per store. What a person may do (raise indent, approve, receive, consume or return) is worked out by the server from the store role, the store's flags and the person's Sahaj role, and is shown read-only in the mapping table (mockup 16 columns). Only a pharmacist shows "incl. Schedule X" for receive. Delegate approval powers show only while the delegate dates are current.
- BR-12. A person mapped to several stores picks a working store after sign-in (and may switch at any time). The server checks that the person is mapped to the working store on every store-bound call; a store the person is not mapped to is refused (403), whatever the screen shows.

Product master
- BR-13. The product master has two sources: the global catalogue (read-only, shared by all units, code `G-nnnnnn`) and the unit's own products (code `U-<unit code>-nnnn`). A unit adopts a global product and sets only local fields; global fields (name, salt, strength, form, manufacturer, HSN, GST %, schedule, units, conversion) stay read-only for the unit.
- BR-14. Before an own product is saved, the server looks for a global product (first) and then an own product with the same salt, strength and form (OQ-78-17) and, if found, the screen offers it ("Use global product" or "Create anyway"). The choice is audited. Creating anyway is allowed (mockup 16).
- BR-15. Every product has a purchase unit, an issue unit and a conversion: how many issue units are in one purchase unit (a whole number, 1 or more; "Box of 10 → vial" is 10; "Kit → kit" is 1). All transactions after receiving stock use the issue unit. Once a product has any approved opening stock (and later any stock movement) its units and conversion are locked, because changing them would silently change every quantity.
- BR-16. An own product needs: name, salt or generic name, strength, form, purchase unit, issue unit, conversion, HSN, GST %, and an explicit schedule choice (None, H, H1, X) with no default. Manufacturer is required unless the owner marks the product "made in-house". Values for HSN, GST % and schedule are the owner's responsibility and are never defaulted by Sahaj (OQ-78-19).
- BR-17. Local settings per adopted or own product: reorder level per store (issue units, whole number 0 or more), rack per store (up to 30 characters), preferred vendor (an active vendor of the unit), alias (what staff call it), barcode (unique among the unit's active products), returnable Yes or No. The BRD's "MRP / selling rule" is not built (OQ-78-20).
- BR-18. Search in the product master looks at global and own products together, ignores capital letters, and matches name, salt, alias, barcode and code. An inactive product is not offered for new work.
- BR-19. Opening: no global product exists for a unit until it adopts it; adoption is per unit and can be repeated safely (adopting twice changes nothing).

Vendors
- BR-20. A vendor has name, GSTIN, drug licence number and expiry (or "not required", for consumables), contact, credit days, return policy (days before expiry, credit %) and the stores it supplies to. Vendor name is unique in the unit, ignoring capital letters.
- BR-21. A vendor whose drug licence has expired is shown "Blocked · licence expired" and must not get a new purchase order (AC-M5 of the BRD; enforced when POs exist in SAH-79, and exposed now as a status the PO story must read). The licence is valid through its expiry date and blocks from the next day. A vendor marked "not required" is never blocked for licence. Existing documents of a blocked vendor are untouched.

Approval rules
- BR-22. Approval rules are data, not code. For each transaction type an owner sets: number of levels (0, 1 or 2) and the role of each level; an optional condition (store type, amount in ₹, item flag Schedule X or high value, priority); ₹ limits (PO, write-off, discount, sales return, stock adjustment); escalation after N hours; and the delegate rule (a delegate, set per store in BR-10). Changing a rule applies to new transactions only and never to ones already waiting.
- BR-23. Three presets ship: Lean (no HOD step), Standard (one level, HOD), Strict (HOD, then owner above a limit). Applying a preset fills the rules and can be changed afterwards. Sahaj supplies no ₹ amounts: a preset that needs a limit asks the owner for it (OQ-78-26, OQ-78-28).
- BR-24. Three rules are fixed and shown but cannot be switched off by any setting: nobody approves their own request; Schedule X needs a pharmacist and a second signature; the stock ledger cannot be edited (corrections are reversing lines). Phase 1 shows and stores nothing for them beyond displaying them; Phase 2 and 3 enforce them.

Numbering, reasons, templates
- BR-25. Each document type (PO, GRN, indent, transfer, bill) has a numbering series: prefix, sequence length, and whether the sequence restarts at the start of the financial year (1 April). Numbers are unique, never reused and never edited. A format change applies to new numbers only. The format rules for the bill are a legal matter (OQ-78-31).
- BR-26. Reason lists exist for store return, sales return, damage and stock adjustment (OQ-78-32), each seeded from the mockup and shown to users as chips. The owner can add a reason and switch one off; a switched-off reason stays on old records. Seeded reasons cannot be renamed or deleted.
- BR-27. Print templates exist for invoice, issue slip and debit note. The owner sets header lines, footer note and paper size; legally required fields (such as GSTIN, drug licence, HSN, batch, expiry on an invoice) cannot be hidden (OQ-78-33). Printing uses the browser print view.

Opening stock
- BR-28. Opening stock is loaded from a CSV file in a Sahaj template with the columns store, product, batch, expiry, MRP, purchase rate and quantity (BRD M-section "Opening stock"). The server checks every row and shows all problems before anything is kept. A file with any error is not imported; nothing is partly imported.
- BR-29. An import is not final until the owner approves it, and the owner who approves must be a different person from the one who uploaded it (the fixed rule "nobody approves their own request", OQ-78-36). An approved import cannot be edited or cancelled; corrections are made in Phase 2 with stock adjustments. Opening stock is allowed once per store (one approved import per store, OQ-78-37). The file rules are in OQ-78-35.
- BR-30. Opening stock posts as an "opening stock" line in the stock ledger (BRD). The ledger is created in Phase 2, so when the lines are posted depends on the architect's design (OQ-78-34). Until posted, no screen shows the quantity as usable stock.

Formats
- BR-31. UI text is plain English in sentence case. Money is ₹ with Indian grouping (₹ 1,25,000). Dates are 24 Oct 2026; times 11:05 AM IST. The mockup's "till Mar 2028" for a licence expiry is shown as the full date entered, "till 31 Mar 2028" (deviation from the mockup text, for sign-off with OQ-78-23).

## Patient-safety and privacy points
- No patient data is entered or stored in Masters. No patient name, mobile or ABHA number may appear in a master, a log or an audit value. Vendor and staff contacts are business data and are never logged.
- The product master decides what the pharmacy may later sell and dispense, so its safety fields matter: schedule (H, H1, X), the issue unit and conversion, and the duplicate check. A wrong schedule flag could let a Schedule X or H1 drug be sold without its register or second signature in Phase 3; a wrong conversion makes every quantity wrong. Hence: schedule has no default and must be chosen (BR-16); global fields are read-only (BR-13); units and conversion lock once stock exists (BR-15); duplicates are warned and the choice is audited (BR-14).
- Duplicate products split stock and break first-expiry-first-out. The duplicate check and the unique barcode rule reduce that.
- Negative stock and non-FEFO selling are not switchable (BR-8).
- The fixed rules (no self-approval, Schedule X two signatures, append-only ledger) cannot be turned off by any setting (BR-24).
- Expired vendor licences block new orders (BR-21): buying medicines from an unlicensed supplier is a legal and safety risk. The licence number and validity are the owner's entry; Sahaj does not verify them with any authority (OQ-78-23).
- Access is per store and checked on the server on every call (BR-11, BR-12): a user cannot see or act on a store they are not mapped to by changing the screen or the request.
- Seed and test data for the global catalogue, vendors and licences is sample data and clearly marked; no real licence numbers, GSTINs or patient data (OQ-78-16).

- OQ-78-23 (Human, legal). Drug licence and GSTIN. Recommended: licence number is free text up to 40 characters, licence expiry is a date, GSTIN is checked for the 15-character format only (no check digit, no online lookup); which vendors need a licence is the owner's choice per vendor ("Drug licence required" Yes or No; consumables suppliers can be No). Licence blocking starts the day after expiry, no grace days. Sahaj does not verify licences with any authority. The human confirms whether a licence number format must be checked for the state.
- OQ-78-24 (BRD section 10). Sales return window and refund modes: per unit or per organization? Recommended: per unit (there is no organization). They belong to the Pharmacy phase (SAH-80, mockup 04 says "return rules set in Setup"); no field is built in SAH-78 except the per-product "returnable Yes or No" (BR-17).

### Approval rules
- OQ-78-25 (Human). Transaction types. Recommended set, from mockup 17 and BRD section 2: Purchase order, Receive stock (rate rise above limit), Vendor return, Indent, Stock transfer, Write-off (damaged or expired), Stock adjustment (audit), Discount at sale, Sales return. Phase 1 stores the rules; Phase 2 and 3 enforce them as those screens appear. Role choices per level: store approver (HOD or active delegate), or owner. Confirm the list and whether pharmacist-in-charge (mockup 04: "Returns above ₹1,000 need pharmacist-in-charge approval") is an approval role.
- OQ-78-26 (Human, pricing). The ₹ limits (PO, write-off, discount, sales return, stock adjustment) and the "high value" item threshold: Sahaj ships no amounts. Mockups quote ₹1,000 and 10% only as examples. Recommended: empty until the owner enters them; the presets that need a limit ask for it when applied.
- OQ-78-27 (Human). Discount limit unit. The BRD says "₹ limit for ... discount"; mockup 05 shows "Discount above 10%". Recommended: percent for discount, rupees for the others.
- OQ-78-28. Presets. Recommended: Lean = level 0 for indent, transfer, write-off, stock adjustment, discount and sales return, owner above limit for PO; Standard = level 1 (HOD) for indent, transfer, write-off; owner above limit for PO, stock adjustment, discount and sales return; Strict = HOD then owner above the limit for all. Please confirm these meanings; they are a compliance choice for the human.
- OQ-78-29 (BRD section 10). Escalation times. Recommended: accept the BRD's suggestion as defaults: indent 2 hours (urgent 30 minutes) and transfer 24 hours, editable. The BRD lists "escalation timers" as Later for the Approvals inbox (I9), so Phase 1 stores the numbers and Phase 2 does not act on them until a timer story exists; the screen says so.
- OQ-78-30 (Human, legal and clinical). Schedule X "second signature": who is the second signer (a second pharmacist, the owner, a doctor)? Recommended: leave open until SAH-80; Phase 1 only displays the fixed rule.

### Numbering, reasons and print templates
- OQ-78-31 (Human, legal). Document numbering. The bill is a tax invoice, and GST rules on invoice numbers (unique, consecutive within a financial year, limited length) are a legal matter. Recommended: prefix up to 6 characters, a financial-year part (for example 26-27), a sequence of 4 to 8 digits that restarts on 1 April, a total of 16 characters or fewer, one series per document type per unit (bills per store if the owner chooses), never reused. The human or the clinic's accountant confirms before the bill series is released.
- OQ-78-32. Reason lists. Recommended: four editable lists seeded from the mockup: Store return (Excess stock, Near expiry, Not needed (order stopped); mockup 11), Sales return (Doctor changed medicine, Excess bought, Wrong item given, Adverse reaction; mockup 04), Damage (Damaged / leaking, Expired, Cold-chain break; mockup 11), Stock adjustment (Consumption not entered, Breakage, Unknown; mockup 13). The BRD names three lists (return, damage, adjustment); splitting return in two follows the mockup. The indent rejection reasons (damaged, wrong item, near expiry or expired, excess, short or not received) are fixed by the BRD and belong to SAH-79; the consumption purposes of mockup 11 also belong there.
- OQ-78-33 (Human, legal). Print templates. Recommended settings: header lines, footer note, paper size A4 or A5, and for the invoice the fixed fields GSTIN, drug licence number, HSN, batch, expiry, MRP and GST amounts; the human confirms the legally required fields of a pharmacy tax invoice, an issue slip and a debit note.

### Opening stock
- OQ-78-34 (Architect and human). The ledger does not exist until Phase 2, so "posts as an opening-stock line in the ledger" cannot happen in Phase 1 as it stands. Recommended: SAH-78 builds upload, checking, review and owner approval, and keeps approved lines as "approved opening stock"; the first Phase 2 story creates the ledger and posts them. The architect may instead create the ledger now; the stories do not change (they say "approved, then posted").
- OQ-78-35. Opening-stock rules. Recommended: one approved import per store; a row with an expiry date in the past is an error (expired stock is not loaded as usable); MRP and purchase rate are per issue unit in rupees with up to 2 decimals (confirm: pricing); quantity is a whole number of issue units above 0; a purchase rate above MRP is a warning shown at approval, not an error; batch up to 20 characters; at most 5,000 rows per file; file saved as CSV (UTF-8); product found by its product code and store by its store code. The same batch twice for the same store and product in one file is an error.
- OQ-78-36 (Human). Self-approval. "Nobody approves their own request" is fixed, but a clinic with one owner would never be able to approve its opening stock if the owner uploaded it. Recommended: store staff (Central Store user or pharmacist mapped to the store) upload; the owner approves; if the owner uploads, a different owner must approve. Confirm.
- OQ-78-37 (Human). Is a second approved import for the same store allowed (forgotten products)? Recommended: no; later additions go through Phase 2 receipts or adjustments, because the ledger cannot be edited.
- OQ-78-38. Switching off an adopted or own product that has approved opening stock: recommended refused until Phase 2 can clear stock (BR-4).
