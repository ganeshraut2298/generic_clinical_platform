# SAH-75 · EMR design alignment: bring the built EMR to the 17-screen design package (audit of 14 Nov 2026, DEC-052)
Module: opd (the EMR and its configuration). Reads platform (doctor, roles), patient, ipd and billing only through their `api` packages or domain events (BR45-1). Embedded treatment courses use the existing course backend (opd `/emr/courses`, billing `/billing/courses`). No new module.
Mockup screen: Queue (sidebar → Queue) (EMR mockup #DoctorGeneral, #GeneralTreatment, #DoctorDental, #DoctorGynae, #DoctorPaeds, #DoctorPhysio, #DoctorSurgery, #DoctorOncology, #OncologyTreatment, #TreatmentCourse, #BodyMap, #EmrList, #VisitHistory, #CasePaper). Configuration screens in the same design: Clinic setup → Specialty template, Case paper and Rx (EMR mockup #Main, #AddDepartment, #FormBuilder). Design package: `docs/design/emr-config/emr-config-design-handoff/emr-config/` (SCREENS.md, `screens/NN-Name.png`, `source/Name.dc.html`; its full HTML is identical to `docs/design/mockup/EMR-Configuration-Smart-Clinic.html`).
Source: Human approval in DEC-052 (`docs/decisions/DEC-052-emr-design-alignment.md`, 14 Nov 2026: "approved from my side need to make necessary changes [FE, BE, DB level]"), from the audit `docs/reviews/EMR-design-audit-2026-11-14.md` (17 screens, a design line and a code line for every gap). Written by the business-analyst on 14 Nov 2026.

## Problem
All 17 screens of the design package were built (SAH-45 to SAH-73), but the audit of 14 Nov 2026 found that the built screens differ from the drawn ones. Eight themes carry the Major gaps: (1) the doctor screen is not 50/50 and the left section nav, Quick tools tab, step subtitles, badges and ready footers differ from each department's drawing; (2) the treatment course is not shown on the Dental, Gynae (ANC), Physiotherapy, Surgery and Oncology screens and can be opened only from the Dental plan; (3) the body map is missing in General Medicine, Surgery, Oncology and Physiotherapy; (4) the starter "required" flags follow the Main configurator tables, not the doctor screens, and "Prescription print format" is required everywhere and blocks a visit; (5) the six Dental assessment cards are not built; (6) the Oncology cancer-journey strip and watch chips are not built; (7) Paediatrics "Mark all due as given" skips the overdue vaccine; (8) about 80 Minor differences in labels, copy and small behaviours. The human decided in DEC-052 to align all of it (frontend, backend, database), with the doctor screen winning where it and the Main tables disagree.

## Who uses it (roles) and in which setting
Sahaj roles: owner, doctor, visiting doctor, nurse, front desk, billing, pharmacist (the platform also has counsellor). The design word "Therapist" (Physiotherapy) means a doctor or visiting doctor with the Physiotherapy department (no new role); "Medical director" means owner (OQ-45-13).
- **Doctor, visiting doctor, owner with doctor details (consultation room, ward, day care):** open and write the EMR on the doctor screens; start a course and change its sittings; use Quick tools. Doctor and visiting doctor: own patients; owner: all patients (DEC-038).
- **Owner (Clinic setup):** configurator, Add department, Form builder (owner only writes; other roles read, OQ-45-12). The required-flag changes in SAH-75-4 reach clinics only as new starter defaults; the owner can still change any flag.
- **Billing, owner (and front desk where SAH-10 allows):** collect payments on a course (SAH-58-3). A doctor sees the money but cannot collect.
- **Nurse, front desk, billing, pharmacist, counsellor:** never open the EMR (BR45-16). Nurse and pharmacist cannot read a course (403).
- Setting: OPD clinic first; IPD ward round and Emergency use the same frame (SAH-52, SAH-67).

## Business rules (numbered)
BR75-1. **The design package is the design; where the doctor screen and the Main configurator disagree, the doctor screen wins** (DEC-052). Every criterion in the stories quotes the design text (labels as written, sentence case) and names the screenshot and source file. A difference not covered by DEC-052 or an accepted decision stops and goes to a human (open questions below).
BR75-2. **One configuration for all visit types stays** (OQ-45-18). Part, order, Show and Required come from the published layout; the new defaults in SAH-75-4 change only the global starter layouts.
BR75-3. **Not changed (accepted decisions):** DEC-039 (scope, owner-only edit, locks for Allergies and PCPNDT Form F, "Medical director" is owner, "Who can fill" stored and shown only), DEC-041 (Back button instead of the design's top bar, "Copy an existing department" disabled until a saved department exists, ABHA tag hidden), DEC-042 (preview save), DEC-043 (Chips and Table views in the EMR list, details closed at start in Past visits, other accepted gaps), DEC-044, DEC-048, DEC-049 (Share on the case paper switched off, WhatsApp and ABDM Later), DEC-051, OQ-45-27 ("Speak" reads "Voice", SMS Later), SO-45-9 (the "+ Add field" button and the field drag handle in the configurator are not built; fields are edited in the Form builder), SO-45-16 (no doctor-screen top bar), SAH-48 (shared prescription editor), added safety features (leave guard, optimistic locking, owner-only edit, hide-lock for Allergies and PCPNDT Form F, BR45-10 "Required cannot be on for a hidden section", allergy alert and addendum extras, "Courses of this patient" switcher).
BR75-4. **Seed only, forward only.** Changes to starter layouts (required flags, shown sections, the new General Medicine "Local examination" section) are made by new forward Flyway migrations to the global starter data. No migration rewrites a clinic's published layout, a clinic's draft, a saved visit or any patient data. A visit keeps the layout it started with (BR45-18). A clinic that already published a layout keeps it; the owner can change flags in the configurator.
BR75-5. **Clinical and legal values are built as drawn and tagged** "value from the EMR mockup, not clinically reviewed" (same meaning as the tag of BR45-4, DEC-039). This applies to the vaccine schedule and the overdue rule, the doxorubicin lifetime limit 450 mg/m², the pre-eclampsia, anaemia and fetal-heart thresholds, ROM normal values, ECOG, lab limits, MLC causes, consent lists and the pre-op test list. Sample amounts in the design are never used as prices; prices come from the clinic tariff (SAH-7, SAH-18) and money values come from the server.
BR75-6. **Embedded treatment course (SAH-75-3).** The course panel (tabs "Visits", "Table", "Payments", four stat tiles, step strip, legend) appears inside the Dental, Gynae (ANC), Physiotherapy, Surgery and Oncology screens, with the unit word of each kind (Sitting, Visit, Session, Step, Cycle) and its own course name. When the patient has no course of that kind, a start entry is shown. The full course page stays reachable from the panel.
BR75-7. **Course actions (SAH-75-10)** are exactly those drawn: "Mark <unit> done", "Patient did not come", "Reschedule: <date>", "Move to day care / IPD", "Admit now", "Remove", "Rebook", and the "Add to this course:" options of the kind. They change status and plan only; charges are added by the server when a sitting is Done and never by the browser. A sitting that is Done cannot be removed or edited except by an addendum on its visit (SAH-58-2).
BR75-8. **Body map (SAH-75-5)** is the shared body-map component (SAH-57) with its rules (MLC flag for Road accident, Assault and Burn, findings register). Its findings are saved with the visit and appear on the case paper and in "Check before completing".
BR75-9. **Required means required only where the section is shown.** A section hidden by the visit mode (Gynae mode hides the ANC section, ANC mode hides menstrual history) is not counted as missing for the other mode (BR45-10 applied to the visit mode).
BR75-10. **Department accent colours** (General Medicine #1D5FA8, Dental #0B6E69, Obstetrics and Gynaecology #A3366B, Paediatrics #B4471F, Physiotherapy #6D3FC0, Surgery #1E40AF, Oncology #9D174D) are applied to the EMR list, the case paper and the course. Other departments keep the app colour.
BR75-11. **No patient data in logs** (BR45-5): no name, mobile number, ABHA number or clinical note in any log, audit value or analytics event. New audit events (course actions, defaults migration) hold ids, keys, status words and setting values only.
BR75-12. **Tap targets** stay 44 px (OQ-45-30) although the design draws some 32 px chips.

## Patient-safety and privacy points
- **Overdue vaccines (SAH-75-9):** "Mark all due as given" will also record the overdue vaccine as given today. This is a clinical rule from the mockup, not clinically reviewed (BR75-5); only vaccines that are Due today or Overdue are marked, never those not yet due, and every vaccine can be unmarked one by one as drawn.
- **Required flags (SAH-75-4):** making "General examination", "Menstrual history" (Gynae mode), "Pre-op work-up", "Surgery plan and OT booking", "Consent" and "Medical history review" required raises the bar for completing a visit in those departments; making "Chief complaints" optional in Oncology and dropping "Prescription print format" lowers it. Both are the doctor screens' own rules.
- **Dental medical history and alert banners (SAH-75-6):** allergy and risk banners (penicillin, tobacco, bleeding risk, diabetes, BP / heart, pregnant, bisphosphonates) are patient-safety prompts built as drawn and tagged. They warn; they do not replace the drug-allergy block of SAH-48.
- **MLC banner text:** "Photos are kept with the record." is drawn, but photo upload is Later; see OQ-75-6.
- **Money:** course amounts and receipts come from the server; the browser never computes a charge. A "Patient did not come" or "Remove" never deletes a receipt or an advance.
- **Privacy:** the audit and the log hold ids and status words only (BR75-11). The patient's name appears on the screen only for the doctor who opened the visit.
- **Multi-tenancy:** every call takes the clinic from the logged-in user, never from the request body; another clinic's course, visit or patient answers 404; starter data stays global and read-only to the app.

## Plan or entitlement
The EMR is part of OPD (always on, SAH-19). IPD options in the course ("Move to day care / IPD", "Admit now", "IPD admission", "Day-care admission") need the IPD entitlement and are not offered when the clinic's IPD is off (SAH-58-2). No new plan flag. Permissions: `clinic.consultation.write` for the doctor screens and course changes, `clinic.emr_setup.edit` (owner) for the configurator; payment collection keeps its billing permission (SAH-58-3). The architect names any new permission.

## Open questions for the human
Each question has a default from DEC-052 or the accepted decisions; the stories follow the default and nothing is blocked. The human may change any default.

- **OQ-75-1 · General Medicine "Local examination · body map".** The doctor screen draws it (DoctorGeneral.dc.html:225-227, as card "Local examination · body map" after Systemic examination, no required star); the Main General Medicine list does not have it (the design contradicts itself). **Default (DEC-052 item 5 and "doctor screen wins"):** add a Body map section "Local examination" to the General Medicine starter, shown, optional, in part 1 Assessment after "Systemic examination", by a forward seed migration. Clinics that already published a General Medicine layout do not get it automatically (BR75-4); the owner shows it from the configurator (the architect confirms the configurator offers it). Affects SAH-75-4, SAH-75-5.
- **OQ-75-2 · Surgery: is "Prescription" still required?** The Surgery screen draws no prescription card and its footer reads "Still needed: complaints, diagnosis, OT booking" (DoctorSurgery.dc.html:264). The starter requires complaints, diagnosis, prescription and print format. **Default:** required = Chief complaints, Pre-op work-up, Diagnosis, Surgery plan and OT booking, Consent; Prescription becomes optional (not drawn on the screen); Prescription print format not required. The part badges in the design count 2 of 2 per part; see SAH-75-2. Affects SAH-75-4.
- **OQ-75-3 · Physiotherapy "Pain site": how is it filled?** The design draws a small body outline with the right knee marked and the caption "Right knee, medial side · worse on stairs, better with rest" (DoctorPhysio.dc.html:83-96) and does not say how the site is chosen; the screen's "Uses" list does not include BodyMap. **Default (DEC-052 item 5):** the pain site is a single marker on the shared body figure (front view, "R" and "L" labels as drawn), set by tapping the body, plus a one-line note under the figure; no findings register, no MLC. Affects SAH-75-5.
- **OQ-75-4 · "Reminder sent to rebook" and other messages the design shows as sent.** The drawn toasts "Marked missed. Reminder sent to rebook." and "<amount> received by <mode> · receipt <no> sent on WhatsApp" promise a message. **Default (DEC-049):** the status changes and the receipt prints; the toast does not claim a message was sent: "Marked missed." The send part stays Later. Affects SAH-75-10.
- **OQ-75-5 · "Admit now" and "Move to day care / IPD".** DEC-052 lists "Move to day care / IPD" but not "Admit now" (drawn on a planned IPD sitting). **Default:** both are built as in SAH-58-2: "Move to day care / IPD" creates an admission request through the ipd `api`; "Admit now" opens the existing admission sheet (SAH-23) and follows OQ-45-31 (the owner, front desk and nurse admit; a doctor who is not the owner sends the pre-filled request instead and the button reads "Request admission"). Affects SAH-75-10.
- **OQ-75-6 · MLC banner sentence "Photos are kept with the record."** The design draws it (BodyMap.dc.html:111); photo upload is Later, so the sentence would state something that is not true. DEC-052 item 10 aligns Minor gaps; the business-analyst advises holding this one sentence. **Default:** the sentence is added only when the documents service exists; until then the banner reads "MLC: record time of examination, who brought the patient, and inform the police as per hospital policy." **Human to confirm or order the sentence now.** Affects SAH-75-8.
- **OQ-75-7 · Oncology journey strip: where do stage, biology, intent and board date come from?** The strip draws "Ca left breast · IDC G2", "Stage IIIA · cT2 cN2 M0", "ER+ PR+ HER2−", "Ki-67 30% · BRCA pending", "Curative · neoadjuvant", "1st line · board 26 Jul" (DoctorOncology.dc.html:50-59) and the design does not draw how they are entered. **Default:** diagnosis comes from the latest Diagnosis entries; the plan chips and "Cycle n of m · day 1" come from the patient's chemotherapy course; response comes from the lesion tracker (SAH-65-2); stage, biology, intent, line and board date are fields of a small "Cancer journey" record per patient that the doctor edits from the strip, and until the record exists those cells show "Not recorded". **The edit form is not drawn; the human must supply or approve a layout before it is built.** Affects SAH-75-7.
- **OQ-75-8 · Oncology watch chips: where do "Doxorubicin 180 / 450 mg/m²" and "MJPJAY · ₹ 38,000 left" come from?** **Default:** the lifetime doxorubicin chip is the sum of doxorubicin doses (mg/m²) of the chemotherapy orders recorded in Sahaj, against the limit 450 mg/m² (rule C-8, tagged); the scheme chip is the balance of the scheme or insurance amount recorded in the course ledger (SAH-58-4) and is shown only when a scheme payment exists; the chip is hidden when there is no value. Affects SAH-75-7.
- **OQ-75-9 · Departments without a drawn part tabs.** Gynae, Paediatrics and Physiotherapy are drawn as one page without the "1 Assessment / 2 Treatment" tabs and without a left nav, while the Main configurator gives them parts. **Default (doctor screen wins):** these three show one scrolling page, sections in the published order (Assessment sections first), no part tabs and no "n of m" badges; the part setting still saves in the configurator and still decides the order. Dental, Surgery and Oncology keep the part tabs as drawn. Affects SAH-75-1, SAH-75-2.
- **OQ-75-10 · Departments with no drawn doctor screen (8 generic departments and clinic-made departments) and the left nav.** DEC-052 says the left nav only on General Medicine "as drawn"; the generic departments have no drawn screen. **Default:** they keep the left section nav as built (a 20-section page needs it); only the drawn departments lose it. Affects SAH-75-1.
- **OQ-75-11 · Width below which the 50/50 layout stacks.** The design is drawn at 1920 px and gives no small-screen rule. **Default:** 50/50 from 1280 px; below 1280 px the right panel sits under the left panel with its tabs kept sticky. Affects SAH-75-1.
- **OQ-75-12 · Start-a-course entry: wording and rule.** The design draws the course panel with an existing sample course and no "start" button (TreatmentCourse.dc.html). DEC-052 asks for a start entry "where drawn". **Default:** when the patient has no open course of that kind, the panel shows the course title, the sentence "No course started yet." and one button, "Start course" (the Dental screen keeps its existing button); the doctor chooses the unit and first dates in the existing course-start flow (SAH-58-1). **The wording and layout of this empty state are not drawn; the human confirms.** Affects SAH-75-3.
- **OQ-75-13 · "Batch numbers from stock" in "4 vaccines recorded · batch numbers from stock".** Vaccine stock has no module. **Default:** the toast reads "4 vaccines recorded" (the number is the number marked); batch numbers are Later. Affects SAH-75-9.
- **OQ-75-14 · Form Builder field type "Tooth chart".** Still shown as "Later" (DEC-043 item 12) although the dental chart now exists; the design shows it as available (FormBuilder.dc.html:157,330). **Default:** unchanged until the human decides, because a tooth chart inside a clinic form needs a design of its own. Affects SAH-75-8 (listed under "not changed").
- **OQ-75-15 · Wording the design gives for the Configurator breadcrumb and Clinic select.** The design says "Settings / Clinical setup / EMR configuration" (Main.dc.html:23); the app calls the area "Clinic setup" with page "Specialty template". **Default:** "Clinic setup / Specialty template / EMR configuration" (the app's own names); the "Clinic" control is a select that shows the clinic name and is disabled while the user has one clinic. Affects SAH-75-8.

- **OQ-75-16 · Dental allergy chips and the patient's allergy record.** Do the chips chosen under "Allergies" in the Dental card "Medical history and alerts" also update the patient's allergy record (SAH-8, free text), or stay with this visit? **Default:** they are saved with the visit, shown in the header tags and used by the drug-allergy check of SAH-48 for this visit; they do not write to the patient record in this requirement (the write-back belongs to SAH-68-1). A penicillin allergy that is not in the patient record would then be lost for the next visit, so the human should decide. Affects SAH-75-6.
