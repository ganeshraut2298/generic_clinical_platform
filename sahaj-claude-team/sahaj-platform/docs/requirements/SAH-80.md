## Problem
After Phase 2 a unit has stores, staff, products, vendors, rules and real stock in four states with a ledger that cannot be edited, but nothing can be sold, dispensed, returned by a patient or shown to an inspector. "Pharmacy sells from the stock that Inventory creates" (BRD section 1). Today Sahaj has no counter sale, no GST invoice, no prescription queue for a pharmacy, no Schedule H1 or Schedule X register, no sales return, no pharmacy command centre (feasibility report, sections 3 and 6). A pharmacy at the hospital works on paper registers and a separate billing tool, which causes the problems the BRD targets: stock sold from expired batches, Schedule H1 registers filled by hand days later, a medicine given to a patient with a recorded allergy, IPD medicines issued but never charged to the running bill, returns accepted against the wrong batch, and no trail of who overrode what.

Phase 3 delivers the five screens of the mockup's Pharmacy tab at the "Basic features in this release" level (BRD P1 to P5) and the Pharmacy rules of BRD section 5: counter sale with FEFO batches and a GST invoice, OPD prescription dispensing, IPD order dispensing, ward returns, sales return against the original bill and batch, the H1 register filled from sales, the Schedule X register with two signatures, licences, and the command centre. Offline billing, analytics, refill reminders, interaction checks, WhatsApp and every "Later" item stay out.

Three kinds of work are mixed in this phase and the stories keep them apart:
1. **Pharmacy-only work** (counter sale, returns, registers, command centre, reports, day close): new code in the `pharmacy` module only.
2. **Touch points that change what OPD, IPD or billing already do** (T2, T4, T5, T7, T8 and the T6 reader port): each is its own story or its own clearly marked part, built only after the human approves that touch point (ADR-028 OQ-PHI-10, decided in DEC-063: each Phase 3 touch point gets its own story). Until approved, the story's feature is hidden and the rest of Phase 3 works.
3. **Items that need a rule from a human** (tax, legal, clinical, pricing): written as OPEN questions with a Recommended answer. The stories build on the recommendation and say so.

Tablets and phones matter for the counter and for approvals; the BRD says "approvals, receiving, stock count and ward consumption must work on a tablet or phone" (section 7) and the mockup counter is laid out for a wide screen. See OQ-80-3 on what the stories require of a phone.

## Business rules (numbered)
Numbers restart in this file; "SAH-79 BR-n" and "SAH-78 BR-n" name rules of those files. Anything the BRD or the mockup does not say, and every tax, legal, clinical or pricing point, is written as "recommended" with its open question (OQ-80-n).

Scope, tenant, access
- BR-1. The same tenancy and access rules as SAH-78 and SAH-79 apply to every call (SAH-79 BR-1): the clinic (unit) comes from the signed-in user, never from the request; another clinic's record answers 404; every store-bound call has `{storeId}` in its path; a store of this clinic to which the caller is not mapped answers 404; a mapped person without the capability gets 403 `store-permission-denied` (audited); the store check runs before any filter, sort or paging. A sale, hold, return or dispensing in a store whose "Sale allowed" is No is refused with 409 `sale-not-allowed` (the screen hides the Pharmacy screens for such a store).
- BR-2. The module switch `pharmacy_on` Off (the default) answers 403 `module-off`; with it Off every existing screen, API and test behaves exactly as before (SAH-78 BR-3). The Pharmacy tab is shown only when at least one Phase 3 screen is released and the person has a Phase 3 capability.
- BR-3. Integrated and Standalone (BRD section 6; ADR-028 D5). Every Phase 3 screen works in both modes; only the source of patient, prescription, allergy, orders, charges and bill link changes. The server answers 409 `integration-off` to an Integrated-only call in Standalone mode; the screen never shows what the capabilities list does not contain. IPD Off or read-only removes T4 to T7 even when the unit is Integrated. If a port call fails, the sale continues in Standalone style, the bill is marked "Link to patient pending", and a "Link to patient" action completes it later (feasibility section 5). There is no retry job. The table below is the one place that says what changes:

| Touch point (BRD section 6) | Integrated mode | Standalone mode | Stories |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T1 Patient | Pick a registered patient by UHID, name or mobile (read through `patient.api`) | Walk-in name and phone, or the pharmacy's own customer list | 80-2 |
| T2 OPD prescription | Signed e-Rx appears in the OPD queue and loads into the bill | Rx typed (or "Paper Rx / walk-in"); doctor name and registration typed | 80-11, 80-12 |
| T3 Allergy | Read from the patient record at sale; match blocks the line | Not checked; an allergy note field on the customer card, shown only | 80-8 |
| T4 IPD order | New, changed, stopped orders in the IPD queue with priority | Not used; ward supply by indent only (SAH-79) | 80-13, 80-14 |
| T5 IPD issue and return | Issue posts a charge to the IPD running bill; ward return posts a credit | Not used | 80-16 |
| T6 Ward indent and consumption | Wards are stores; patient-wise consumption (SAH-79-16) | Wards are stores; general consumption | carry-over in 80-16 |
| T7 Discharge | Pharmacy clearance and take-home medicines shown | Not used | 80-17 |
| T8 OPD billing | Pharmacy bill stands alone (default) or is added to the OPD bill when the unit chooses | Pharmacy bill stands alone | 80-23 |

- BR-4. Access is by store mapping plus the store flags (SAH-79 "Who uses it"). Phase 3 adds the store role CASHIER (additive to the SAH-78 mapping rules, OQ-80-36) and these capabilities, derived on the server on every call and never sent by a client: Sell (store "Sale allowed" Yes and the person is a mapped pharmacist, a mapped cashier, or the owner), Dispense (pharmacist mapped to the store, or the owner for non-scheduled items), Accept ward returns (pharmacist), Sales return (cashier or pharmacist starts it), Registers and compliance (owner, pharmacist; the store's HOD reads), Cancel bill (owner; pharmacist the same day), Approve discount or sales return (the approver the rule names). Pharmacist-only actions are never available to the owner (ADR-028 D4).

Counter sale
- BR-5. A sale takes only Available, unexpired stock of the selling store, by FEFO (earliest expiry, then earliest received); the user cannot choose another batch (FEFO is fixed On, SAH-78 BR-8). A line asking for more than the first batch holds is split into one bill line per batch. An expired batch is never offered, never picked and never sold; it is already in Quarantine (SAH-79 BR-8) and the line shows "<batch> expired · blocked" as the mockup does. Stock never goes negative: a sale that would take a balance below zero is refused with 409 `insufficient-stock` and the available quantity, also when two counters sell the last units at once (SAH-79 BR-6). Saving a bill is one all-or-nothing step: it takes the bill number, writes the bill, its lines and payments, writes the ledger lines "Counter sale" (reference = bill number, never a name), writes the H1 and Schedule X register rows, and writes the audit event. A failed save leaves no number used, no stock moved and nothing half-saved.
- BR-6. Selling price. A sale is at the batch MRP per issue unit; MRP comes from the batch (SAH-78 OQ-78-20, DECIDED: selling is at the batch MRP in Phase 3). The mockup shows MRP including GST ("CGST 6% (incl.)", "SGST 6% (incl.)"). Recommended (OQ-80-6, accountant): line amount = quantity times MRP, rounded half up to the paisa; GST is worked inside the amount at the product's GST % (CGST and SGST halves) and shown as in the mockup; the bill total is rounded to the nearest rupee with a separate "Round off" line, as the mockup shows ("Round off − ₹0.38", "Net payable ₹532.00"). No selling above MRP and no price change at the counter (OQ-80-9).
- BR-7. Discount. A bill discount is a percent of the MRP total entered by the seller (the mockup's "Discount" line). Within the unit's limit it is applied at once; above the limit the bill waits for the approval named by the unit's rule for SALE_DISCOUNT (SAH-78-9: percent limit, OQ-78-27 recommended percent for discounts). The limit is the owner's entry; Sahaj ships none (SAH-78 G-4). The discount lowers the taxable value in proportion (OQ-80-6). The mockup's "chronic plan 5%" is a patient discount plan that no master defines: not built (OQ-80-8).
- BR-8. Payments are recorded, never collected by Sahaj: cash, UPI, card, udhar (credit), or a split between them (BRD P2). The payments must add up to the net payable before the bill can be saved. UPI and card carry no gateway, no QR and no reference check in this release (DEC-061 default). Udhar needs a named customer and stays within that customer's credit limit (BR-13). A bill with udhar shows the amount as "On credit" until paid.
- BR-9. The invoice shows, as the mockup says: the unit's name, drug licence number and GSTIN (SAH-78-1), HSN, batch, expiry, MRP, GST and amounts per line, the customer or patient, the bill number and date, the pharmacist's name and registration number where the bill has a pharmacist (BR-28), and a signature line. It prints through the browser print view with the invoice template of SAH-78-10; legally required fields cannot be hidden (SAH-78 BR-27, G-8). A saved bill cannot be edited. A mistake is corrected by cancelling the bill (BR-12) or by a sales return (BR-26).
- BR-10. Numbering. Bill numbers come from the BILL series (SAH-78-10). R2-12 (DEC-064) carried "bill series per store" to this phase: recommended, a series per selling store with its own prefix (up to 6 characters, so that the whole number stays within 16 characters, SAH-78 OQ-78-31), and the unit's BILL series for a store that has none. A number is taken in the same step that saves the bill; saved bills have no gaps; a cancelled bill keeps its number; a held bill and a discarded draft have no number. A sales return has its own credit note number from its own series (OQ-80-7); both belong to the accountant's confirmation (SAH-78 G-7, new G-22).
- BR-11. Hold. "Hold" (F8) stores the cart without a number, without stock movement and without payment, so that the counter can serve the next person. No stock is reserved: prices, batches and availability are checked again when the held bill is resumed and saved (OQ-80-13). A held bill belongs to the store and its holder; it can be resumed or discarded; the discard is audited.
- BR-12. Cancel. A saved bill can be cancelled: by the owner at any time and by a pharmacist the same day (IST) only; never by a cashier (mockup 05). Cancelling writes new reversing ledger lines (never edits the old ones), returns the units to Available only if the person confirms they are back on the shelf (else Quarantine), writes reversing register rows, keeps the bill number and shows "Cancelled" with who, when and the reason chip. What happens to the money is OQ-80-13. "Edit saved bill" is cancel and make a new bill; there is no edit.
- BR-13. Customers and credit. In Integrated mode the patient is read from registration (T1); in Standalone mode the customer is a walk-in name and phone, or an entry in the pharmacy's own customer list, with a note field for allergy (shown, not checked). A customer account holds the credit limit (set by the owner or pharmacist, OQ-80-11), the udhar due and, where built, a credit note balance. Payments received against udhar reduce the due. Pharmacy owns this; the billing module has no customer credit (OQ-80-11). A customer's name and phone are patient data in the `pharmacy` schema: tenant isolated, never logged (feasibility T1).

Clinical safety and registers
- BR-14. Substitution. Only an exact equivalent may replace a product: same salt, same strength, same dosage form (BRD section 5; the duplicate key of SAH-78 D3: salt, strength and form). The product prescribed or asked for stays on the bill line next to the one supplied; the reason (a chip) and the patient's agreement are saved on the bill line (mockup: "Substitution reason + patient consent stored on the bill"). Only a pharmacist substitutes (OQ-80-23).
- BR-15. Allergy (T3, Integrated mode only). At sale the server reads the patient's allergy record through `patient.api.PatientClinicalPort`. If the allergy text matches the salt of a product on the bill, that line is blocked ("Blocked: <product> — patient has a <allergy> allergy recorded in EMR."). A pharmacist may override with a reason chip and a note; the owner and a cashier may not. The override, the reason and the person are saved with the bill line and in the audit. The match rule on free text is OQ-80-22 (clinical). When the allergy record cannot be read, the sale continues with the notice "Allergy not checked" (OQ-80-22).
- BR-16. Schedule H1 (BRD section 5; AC-P5). When a bill is saved, every line whose product has schedule H1 writes one append-only register row in the same step: date, bill number, patient name and age as typed or read, prescriber name and registration number, drug, batch, quantity, and the prescription (the e-Rx link in Integrated mode; the typed prescription details in Standalone, no image, OQ-80-17). No manual register. A line with no prescriber name or registration number is blocked; a pharmacist may override with a reason, and the register row says so. A correction is a new reversing row, never an edit (BR-12, BR-26).
- BR-17. Schedule X (fixed rule 2 of BRD section 2; SAH-79 BR-32). Selling or issuing a Schedule X item needs a person with the team role pharmacist who is mapped to the store (never the owner, never a cashier) and a second signature by a different active person through the colleague PIN check; both names are stored on the bill line and on the register row. The prescription details are required. The Schedule X balance register shows, per product and store: opening, received, issued, book and physical (BR-27). Who may be the second signer, and NDPS items, are OQ-80-18 (G-6, carried from SAH-78 OQ-78-30 and SAH-79 OQ-79-8). The mechanism is the one SAH-79 built for receiving; SAH-80 adds sale and return.
- BR-18. Who may complete a line of schedule H, H1 or X. BRD roles: Cashier "Sale and payment", Pharmacist "Sale, dispensing, H1/X registers". Recommended: a cashier may bill only non-scheduled items; a bill that has a schedule H, H1 or X line can be saved only by a pharmacist (OQ-80-20, legal and pharmacist). The screen tells a cashier "A pharmacist must complete this bill." (NEW).

Dispensing (Integrated mode unless stated)
- BR-19. OPD queue and Rx-linked sale (T2, AC-P2). Signed e-Rx appear in the pharmacy OPD queue; the pharmacist or cashier picks one, checks it, picks the medicines by rack, bills and hands over. The e-Rx line is linked to a pharmacy product through a pharmacy-owned link table (DEC-061 Q3; BR-25); a line with no link shows "Not mapped" and is mapped once by a pharmacist. The prescription carries no quantity to dispense: the pharmacist types it (auto supply quantity is Later, OQ-80-24). The pharmacy keeps its own "dispensed" state per prescription line; nothing is written back to OPD in this release (OQ-80-29). A prescription can be supplied in part: the rest stays open in the queue as "Part supplied". Standalone: no queue; the Rx is typed on the sale screen.
- BR-20. IPD queue and issue to ward (T4, AC-P3). New, changed and stopped orders for admitted patients, read through a port, appear in the IPD queue by priority STAT, Urgent, Routine and Discharge (the Discharge lane is OQ-80-33). The IPD pharmacist check is built in IPD (SAH-31, SAH-76) and is read, not repeated (OQ-80-25). An issue takes Available stock of the pharmacy store by FEFO, writes the ledger lines "IPD issue" (reference = admission number, never a name), and posts a charge through T5; until T5 is approved the issue is saved as "Charge pending" (BR-22). Quantity is typed by the pharmacist (OQ-80-24). Issues are "Sent to ward"; whether a nurse confirms receipt at the ward is OQ-80-25.
- BR-21. Ward returns. A ward returns unused issued medicine to the pharmacy. Only the same batch that was issued can be returned. The pharmacist accepts each item: sealed goes back to Available in the store, doubtful or opened goes to Quarantine (and may be recorded as wastage); a Schedule X or cold-chain item needs the pharmacist's decision. Accepting posts a credit through T5 (or "Credit pending", BR-22). Details and the wastage path are OQ-80-26.
- BR-22. IPD charge and credit (T5, touch point). Pharmacy writes only charges, credits and dispense status (BRD section 6). A charge or credit is a synchronous port call inside the pharmacy transaction, so stock and charge commit together or not at all (ADR-028 D5). The port, the pharmacy service key and the credit line in the IPD running bill are changes to built IPD behaviour (the closed list of 10 service keys, quantity 1 to 50, no credit line in `ipd.admission_charge`; feasibility section 4 T5) and need the human's approval (OQ-80-31). The price charged is OQ-80-15. Consumption entries saved as "Charge pending" by SAH-79-16 are posted by the same story when T5 is approved, so none is lost (OQ-79-23).
- BR-23. Discharge clearance (T7, touch point). Recommended default: information only. The Pharmacy screen shows, for an admission whose discharge has started, "Pharmacy clearance: pending or cleared", where pending means unaccepted ward returns or an open discharge supply; the pharmacist marks it cleared. IPD discharge is not changed and nothing blocks the final bill unless the human approves a gate (OQ-80-33).
- BR-24. OPD bill (T8, touch point). The pharmacy bill stands alone by default (DEC-063: BRD section 10 Q1 accepted; ADR-028 OQ-PHI-12). A unit setting "Add pharmacy bill to the OPD bill" is built as an option through a port in `billing.api` that billing implements; it is off until the human approves the billing change (OQ-80-16).
- BR-25. Product links. `opd.medicine` and `ipd.drug` stay unchanged. Pharmacy keeps link tables from them to its own products (opaque ids, no foreign key). A prescription or order line with no link shows "Not mapped"; the pharmacist maps it once, and the mapping is audited (OQ-80-34).

Sales return, compliance, overview
- BR-26. Sales return (AC-P4). A return is only against an original bill and the same batch. The return quantity cannot exceed sold minus already returned. It must be within the unit's return window; the product must be returnable (SAH-78 BR-17); the condition is recorded; reason from the sales-return list; refund mode as the unit allows; stock goes back to Available (sealed) or to Quarantine (doubtful). Above the unit's ₹ limit the return waits for approval (SALES_RETURN rule). Money is recorded, not paid by Sahaj. The return has a credit note number. The window, modes and limit are the owner's entries; Sahaj ships none (OQ-80-12).
- BR-27. Registers. The H1 register lists the register rows of BR-16 by date, bill, patient, prescriber and registration, drug, batch, quantity and prescription; the Schedule X / NDPS balance register is computed from the ledger: opening (balance at the start of the period), received, issued, book, and physical from the latest stock count (SAH-79-17), with "review" where physical and book differ. Registers are read-only and append-only; retention periods are the human's (G-11; OQ-80-17).
- BR-28. Licences and pharmacist registration. The "Licences & renewals" list shows the unit's retail drug licence number and expiry (SAH-78-1), the Schedule X licence number and expiry (NEW fields, OQ-78-41), the GST registration, and each registered pharmacist with registration number and valid-till date (pharmacy-owned record, ADR-028 OQ-PHI-20 decided: Phase 3). Status is "Valid", "<n> days" or "Expired". Sahaj verifies nothing with any authority (SAH-78 BR-21). The days threshold for "<n> days" is OQ-80-19. No reminder is sent (no notifications).
- BR-29. Role read-out and audit log. The roles and permissions card of mockup 05 is a read-only read-out of what the server enforces (the unit's approval rules plus the fixed rules and capabilities), not an editor (ADR-028 C6 resolution: the screen-05 matrix becomes a Phase 3 read-out of the same rules). The audit log lists pharmacy actions with who, what, when, and the reason chip, from the platform audit; it never holds a patient name, mobile number, ABHA number or clinical text (OQ-80-21, OQ-80-35).
- BR-30. Command centre. Today's figures are for the signed-in person's working store (the owner may pick all stores), for the current calendar day in IST. The figures and what they count are in the requirement of story 80-20 and OQ-80-4. No figure is shown that cannot be worked out from built data; nothing is estimated.
- BR-31. Reports and export. The "Sales & compliance" catalogue group adds Sales and margin, Sales returns, GST sales register, Schedule H1 and X registers, and User activity and audit log to the SAH-79 reports (SAH-79-18, same filters, CSV, browser print, row cap). Exports never include a patient name, mobile number or ABHA number, except the H1 and X registers, where the legal content includes the patient (OQ-80-21).
- BR-32. Day close (NEW; BRD section 5 "Day close: cash tally by payment mode at shift end"; not drawn in the mockup). At the end of a shift a cashier or pharmacist closes their counter: the sheet shows, per payment mode, the amount the system expects from bills and returns of the shift, the person enters what was counted, the difference is shown and saved with a reason chip, and the day is locked for edits. The sheet's layout is NEW (OQ-80-14).

## Patient-safety and privacy points
- Wrong stock is a patient risk. Expired batches are never sold (BR-5): the batch is already quarantined by Phase 2 and the sale can only pick Available, unexpired batches by FEFO. Stock never goes negative; two counters cannot sell the same last unit.
- Allergy: a recorded allergy that matches the salt blocks the line, and only a pharmacist can override with a reason that is kept (BR-15). Allergy text is free text in the patient record, so a match can be missed and can be wrong; the block is the safe direction, and a record that cannot be read is shown as "Allergy not checked", never as "no allergy" (OQ-80-22).
- Substitution is a clinical decision. Only an exact salt, strength and form equivalent is offered, only a pharmacist substitutes, and the reason and the patient's agreement are on the bill (BR-14, OQ-80-23).
- Controlled medicines: Schedule X needs a pharmacist and a second signer who is a different person, both stored, and never the owner or a cashier (BR-17). Schedule H1 needs a prescriber name and registration or a pharmacist's override with a reason (BR-16). A wrong schedule flag in the product master would let such an item be sold without these checks, which is why the schedule has no default there (SAH-78 BR-17).
- A wrong quantity on an IPD supply is a clinical risk: the pharmacist types the quantity (no auto supply quantity, which is Later), sees the order text next to the box, and the issue is blocked when stock is short (BR-20).
- A ward return must be the same batch that was issued, and a doubtful item goes to Quarantine, so an opened strip cannot go back on the shelf (BR-21, BR-26).
- Registers are append-only: a correction is a new reversing row (BR-16, BR-27). Nobody approves their own request; the owner cannot do pharmacist-only actions.
- Privacy. The ledger holds bill numbers and admission numbers only. Logs, audit text and reports never hold a patient name, mobile number, ABHA number, Rx text or clinical note (BR-34). The mobile number is shown masked on screen ("+91 98XXX XX214", as the mockup shows) and read through the patient module's explicit call. The ABHA number is not stored in Sahaj (feasibility 3), so the mockup's "ABHA linked" chip is shown only if the patient port gives a yes or no (OQ-80-28). Walk-in names and phones in the `pharmacy` schema are patient data: tenant isolated by RLS, never logged (OQ-80-21). The H1 and Schedule X registers hold the patient's name because the law needs it (OQ-80-17); reading them is a record-access entry.
- Offline: no half-saved bill. A failed or slow save keeps what was typed and says so; nothing is queued silently (OQ-80-39).
- Data stays in India; seed data is marked "Sample".

### E. Explicitly NOT in this release
- **Day close (story 80-22).** The screen, the tally and the lock are not built; there is no cash tally by payment mode (code review 2 m8; DEC-093 item 4; CL-8).
- **WP-O (story 80-23, T8).** "Add pharmacy bill to the OPD bill" is not built (DEC-074).
- **Pharmacist registration form.** The entry of each pharmacist's registration number and valid-till date is not built. Check what the invoice and the licence list print before use (S3). Open question for the human: confirm this is acceptable for go-live, because BR-9 and BR-28 print the registered pharmacist's name and number.
- **OPD, IPD and patient-search integrations.** The OPD prescription source (TP-2), IPD order feed (TP-4), IPD charge and credit (TP-5) and admitted-patients reader (TP-6) are no-op ports. In Integrated mode the OPD and IPD queues answer 409 `not-connected`; setting a product link answers 409 `not-connected`; patient search answers 503 `patients-unreachable` (the sale falls back to a walk-in); `abhaLinked` is null; the allergy check, IPD issue, ward returns and discharge clearance cannot work until the human and the IPD session approve each adapter (DEC-072 Q2; DEC-088 item 7; DEC-089 item 1). Standalone mode is complete.
- **Ward consumption charging (the charging half of story 80-16).** Entries stay "Charge pending"; the charge columns are an unused surface. Do not report 80-15 or 80-16 charging as done (code review 2 m9; DEC-093 item 4).
- **Gross margin card** and the sale-margin grouping by anything but day and store (DEC-089 item 2).
- **"Export GST" and "Download inspection pack" buttons.** Not shown; the GST sales register and the H1 and X registers are CSV and print only (OQ-80-2).
- Also out (unchanged from the Out of scope list): offline billing, WhatsApp and SMS, drug-class allergy matching, interaction checks, refill reminders, barcode pick check, the discharge gate, multi-branch, and a change of MRP.
