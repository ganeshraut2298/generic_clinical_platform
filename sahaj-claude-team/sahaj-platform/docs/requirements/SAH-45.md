## Problem
The BRD design gives four specialty templates and lets the owner only show or hide a section and make it required. The human's mockup shows a much larger design: a configurator with 15 departments and a library of 100 sections that the owner can show, require, move between the two parts, reorder, publish as versions and reset; an Add department wizard; a form builder; and, for the doctor, seven specialty EMR screens plus five shared components. The human wants the EMR button on the existing Queue card to open the right screen for OPD, IPD and Emergency patients.

The two instructions that conflicted ("build the mockup" and "the BRD wins") are settled: the mockup wins (decision 1 above). The programme is therefore the whole mockup, delivered in five phases; phases 4 and 5 are now written in full (12 Nov 2026, later), with new ids SAH-69 to SAH-71 for the oversized stories.

## Who uses it (roles) and in which setting
Sahaj roles: owner, doctor, visiting doctor, nurse, front desk, billing, pharmacist (the platform also has counsellor). The mockup uses other words; the map is: Doctor = doctor, visiting doctor, or an owner who holds doctor details; Nurse / assistant = nurse; Reception = front desk; "Therapist" (Physiotherapy screen) = a doctor or visiting doctor with the Physiotherapy department (no new role); "Medical director" (Form builder approval) = owner (OQ-45-13, decided); "admin" in the human's brief = owner (OQ-45-12, decided); Patient (a Form builder "who can fill" option) is not a user of the product.
- **Owner: Clinic setup.** Configures departments, sections, versions and forms. Owner only writes; every other clinic role can read the page (OQ-45-12). `clinic.emr_setup.edit` is fixed in code (DEC-038).
- **Doctor, visiting doctor, owner with doctor details: consultation room, ward round, ER bay.** Open the EMR from the Queue card and write it. Doctor and visiting doctor: own patients; owner: all patients (BRD, DEC-038 `clinic.consultation.write`).
- **Nurse: vitals step, ward.** Fills vitals in the existing vitals step; never opens the EMR. The EMR does not pre-fill from the nurse's vitals in phase 2 (human's decision, DEC-039 addendum; separate future story, placeholder SAH-72). The mockup's "Who can fill" setting is stored and shown only; the doctor can edit every section (OQ-45-14).
- **Front desk, billing, pharmacist, counsellor:** see outcomes only (bill lines, follow-ups, case paper reprint, receipts).
- **Setting:** OPD clinic first; IPD ward and Emergency use the same frame (SAH-52, SAH-67).

## Business rules (numbered)
Programme rules (all phases):
BR45-1. **The EMR and its configuration belong to module `opd`** and use other modules only through their `api` packages or domain events. Reuse, do not rebuild: Queue and workflow (SAH-9, SAH-12), vitals, billing (SAH-10), follow-ups (SAH-14), IPD admission (SAH-23), ER (SAH-28), IPD safety checks (SAH-31), patient record (SAH-8).
BR45-2. **The mockup is the design.** Each story names its mockup screen as `EMR mockup #<ScreenName>`. Where the mockup differs from the BRD, the old repo mockup or what is built, the mockup wins (DEC-039 answer 1) and the difference is listed as decided in the sign-off list (SO-45-n). Where the mockup does not show something (the Add department steps 2 and 3, IPD and Emergency frames), the story says "not drawn in the mockup" and follows the human's brief or the BRD table the human approved in DEC-036.
BR45-3. **Global and tenant data are kept apart.** The section library and the 15 starter department templates are global reference data (no `tenant_id`, read-only to the app, changed by migration). A clinic's departments, drafts, published versions, section settings and form definitions are tenant data (`tenant_id`, row-level security, tenant from the logged-in user, never the request body).
BR45-4. **Clinical, legal and billing rules are built as drawn and tagged.** Numbers, lists and wording that the mockup shows for dosing, vitals limits, ANC thresholds, growth centiles, MLC, PCPNDT Form F, consent, drug-allergy warnings, chemotherapy, payments, deposits, package rates, schemes and pre-authorisation become requirements exactly as shown. Every such rule in a story carries the tag "value from the EMR mockup, accepted by the human in DEC-039, not clinically reviewed". The tech lead advised against this for patient-safety rules (DEC-039 answer 3); the tag is the way to find and review them later. Real prices still come from the clinic tariff (SAH-7, SAH-18), not from sample rates.
BR45-5. **Privacy.** No patient name, mobile number, ABHA number or clinical note in any log, audit value or analytics event. ABHA is shown, linked or shared only with the patient's consent (and those actions are Later, DEC-049; the consent record is kept in-app).
BR45-6. **Shared components are built once** (EMR list, Treatment course, Body map, Patient history, Case paper, Prescription and services) and used by every specialty screen and by the generic renderer.

Configuration rules (phase 1, SAH-53, SAH-45, SAH-54):
BR45-7. **A section has** a stable key, a name, a kind (Common, Specialty or Compliance), a description and a list of fields (label, type, meta, required). The library holds 100 sections (counts above).
BR45-8. **A department is a clinic's own configuration** that starts from a global starter template (or blank, or a copy of another department of the same clinic). It has a name, initials, a status (Template, Draft, Live) and a version number.
BR45-9. **Per department and section the owner sets** Show in EMR, Required to close visit, Print on prescription, Copy from last visit, Collapsed by default and Who can fill (Doctor, Nurse / assistant, Reception), plus the part (1 Assessment or 2 Treatment) and the position in the order.
BR45-10. **A hidden section is never required.** The count "x of y on" and "required" count shown sections only. **Only Allergies cannot be hidden**; every other section can be switched off, as in the mockup (OQ-45-6, answered explicitly). A department with every section hidden, or with nothing required, may be published; there is no rule (OQ-45-24). Exception to check: OQ-45-29.
BR45-11. **Moving and ordering:** a section moves between the two parts with one action; Up and Down swap it with the next section of the same part. Order is per department.
BR45-12. **Draft and publish:** Save draft stores the draft and leaves what doctors see unchanged ("Doctors still see the published layout"). Publish creates the next version number for that department ("New visits use it from now"). A visit keeps the version it was opened with ("Visits already in progress keep the layout they started with"). All published versions are kept for ever, because signed visits point to them (OQ-45-21).
BR45-13. **Reset to template** returns the selected department's settings, parts and order to the starter template's defaults and can be undone from the message that follows it, until the page is left or the next change (OQ-45-8).
BR45-14. **Unsaved changes** are counted as distinct differences from the last saved draft (a switch turned off and on again counts 0) and shown ("n unsaved change(s)" or "All changes published") (OQ-45-15).
BR45-15. **Only the owner changes the configuration;** every other clinic role reads it without write controls. Every save draft, publish, reset and add-department writes an audit event (user, time, department, version, before and after of setting values; no patient data). Two owners editing the same department get optimistic locking (409 for the second). The sections page needs no approval step (OQ-45-13).

EMR rules (phase 2, SAH-46, SAH-47, SAH-52):
BR45-16. **The EMR opens from an "Open EMR" button on the Queue card** for OPD (Queue), IPD (IPD → Admitted) and Emergency (IPD → Emergency) patients, for doctor, visiting doctor and owner with doctor details only; nurse, front desk, billing, pharmacist and counsellor never see it (OQ-45-22).
BR45-17. **Template resolution:** doctor specialty, then the doctor's department, then the clinic default (the department matching the clinic's specialty, else General Medicine). A fixed, case-insensitive list of names maps a specialty text to one of the 15 starter departments (OQ-45-9, OQ-45-11).
BR45-18. **Snapshot:** at the first open a visit stores the department, the published version number and the ordered list of shown sections with their settings. Later publishes, resets or setting changes never alter that visit (signed or draft).
BR45-19. **Only published, shown sections appear, in the published order, with the required flag.** The frame is one for all departments: header with allergy and alert tags; part tabs 1 Assessment and 2 Treatment with done counts; section list with status; entry on the left; Past visits and Case paper on the right (Past visits first, as in the mockup); a sticky footer with the "Still needed" text, Save draft and Complete.
BR45-20. **Done and Still needed:** a part's badge reads "n of m required"; a tick replaces the number when every required section of the part is done; the footer lists what is missing; Save draft never validates. A generic section is Done when every required field is filled, or, with no required field, when at least one field is filled (OQ-45-25). A section with Copy from last visit is Done only after the doctor opens and confirms or edits the copied values (OQ-45-26). Moving between parts is never blocked; only Complete is. How Complete behaves is SAH-51 (phase 5).
BR45-21. **Encounter differences** (BRD FR-02, not drawn in the mockup): opens as Consultation, Daily progress note or Emergency assessment; the visit-type control, Past visits scope, Admit to IPD button and finish behaviour differ for OPD, IPD, Emergency.

Shared-component rules (phase 3, SAH-56, SAH-48, SAH-50, SAH-55, SAH-57, SAH-58, SAH-59):
BR45-22. **EMR list (SAH-56).** One list component serves five kinds: complaints, diagnosis, investigations, advice, procedures and services. Each row has a name, an optional code (diagnosis: ICD-10) and two attributes per kind. "Your most used" quick items are ranked by the doctor's use (SAH-43). A typed item that is not in the master stays on that patient's record only, is tagged "New to list", and is never ranked or shared (OQ-45-17).
BR45-23. **Prescription and services (SAH-48).** Medicines are chosen by generic name first, with frequency, days and a "when" instruction; favourites, "Repeat last Rx" and "Clear all" help routine visits; a drug that clashes with a recorded allergy blocks Complete unless the doctor removes it or confirms the alert; ticked services become bill lines through the billing `api`; advice can be printed in English, Marathi or Hindi.
BR45-24. **Patient history (SAH-50).** Past visits (OPD and IPD) with a department-specific key-facts box, search, All / OPD / IPD filter, expandable rows and actions that copy medicines or repeat tests into today's visit. IPD data respects ward scope and break-glass (SAH-34).
BR45-25. **Case paper (SAH-55).** A live paper of ten headings filled as the doctor types, A4 or A5, English, Marathi or Hindi headings, "Hide empty", Print; Share on WhatsApp / ABHA is Later (DEC-049); the button is shown switched off.
BR45-26. **Body map (SAH-57).** Pins on a body drawing (Male, Female, Other; three skin tones; Front and Back; zoom) with where, finding, cause, severity, size and note; a findings register; MLC flag set automatically for the causes Road accident, Assault and Burn.
BR45-27. **Treatment course (SAH-58).** One running account for a multi-visit treatment (sitting, visit, cycle, step or session) with OPD visits and IPD or day-care admissions linked to it; advances and part payments carry across visits and are used first.
BR45-28. **Form builder (SAH-59).** The owner builds a clinic form from 23 field types, with show-if logic, scores and bands, versions and approval; it appears as a custom section in the departments chosen in its settings.
BR45-29. **Later (DEC-049; was "Waiting for DEC-040").** A story criterion that needs a WhatsApp or ABHA / ABDM send, link or share is marked "Later (DEC-049)" (older wording "Waits for DEC-040" means the same) and is not built until the Meta account, the ABDM sandbox registration and the legal opinion exist. The in-app part (button, consent capture, message preview, messages that say sending is not available) stays as built with the send control switched off. The sending work is held by SAH-68-4, SAH-68-5 and SAH-68-6.

## Patient-safety and privacy points
- **Hiding a section is a safety decision.** The mockup lets any section be switched off. Decided: only Allergies cannot be hidden (OQ-45-6). Prescription, Medical alerts and History are no longer locked as they were in SAH-44 (withdrawn); Physiotherapy starts with Prescription off, as drawn.
- **Compliance sections** (PCPNDT Form F, consents, surgical safety checklist, pre-authorisation): the owner may switch them off as drawn, except PCPNDT Form F in Obstetrics & Gynaecology, which cannot be hidden (OQ-45-7 default). Whether that lock still stands next to "only Allergies" is OQ-45-29.
- **Drug-allergy warnings** are built as drawn (SAH-48-2, tagged): a drug flagged with an allergy class clashes with a recorded allergy of that class; the doctor removes the drug or ticks "I have reviewed the alert and want to prescribe anyway" before Complete. IPD already has checks (SAH-31); the EMR must not create a second, unchecked path for IPD medicines (OQ-43-24).
- **Out-of-range colours** use the limits of the mockup (SpO2 below 95, temperature 99.5 °F or above), tagged; adult limits apply to a child in Paediatrics until the Paediatrics story says otherwise (OQ-43-23).
- **"No known allergy" versus "not asked"**: the patient record holds free text (SAH-8). The header must not say "No known allergy" when nothing was asked (OQ-43-22 stays open; OQ-45-23 is the same point).
- **Medico-legal (MLC), PCPNDT Form F, consent, ABDM wording** are built as drawn and tagged. They are legal duties; the tag marks them for the later legal review.
- **Free-typed EMR list items** stay on that patient's record only (OQ-45-17). The mockup's hint "It is saved to your list for next time" is therefore not shown; the hint says the item stays on this patient's record.
- **Copied values** from the last visit are marked "Copied from <date>" and need the doctor's confirmation (OQ-45-26).
- **Offline draft, photos, handwriting** keep clinical data on a device or in a service not in phase 1 (OQ-43-28, OQ-45-28).
- Never log patient names, mobile numbers, ABHA numbers or clinical notes. Audit holds ids, keys and setting values, not clinical text.

## Rules built as drawn, tagged "value from the EMR mockup, accepted by the human in DEC-039, not clinically reviewed"
These are the exact rules found in the mockup source. They are built as shown (DEC-039 answer 3). Each carries the tag; the stories repeat it on the criterion. No owner has signed them and no story waits for one. Numbers are quoted from the example data and logic in the mockup, not from any clinical source.

### Clinical rules (C)
- **C-1 Vitals flags (General Medicine):** blood pressure systolic 140 or above, or diastolic 90 or above "High", systolic below 90 "Low"; pulse above 100 "High", below 60 "Low"; SpO2 below 95 "Low"; temperature 99.5 °F or above "Fever", below 96 "Low"; respiratory rate above 20 "High". (The BRD said SpO2 below 94 and temperature 100 °F or above; the mockup values are used.) In Oncology: systolic 160 or above, or below 90, "Check again"; fever is 38 °C or above.
- **C-2 Drug-allergy warning:** a drug flagged sulfa clashes with a sulfa allergy; amoxicillin clashes with a penicillin allergy; the doctor must remove the drug or tick "I have reviewed the alert and want to prescribe anyway" before completing.
- **C-3 Dental suggestions:** caries → composite filling or root canal; root canal done → root canal (phase 1) then PFM crown (phase 2); filled → replace filling; crown → re-cementation; missing → implant with crown (phase 3) or bridge (phase 2); extract → extraction; implant → implant review. Root canal as 3 sittings; next sitting after 3 days, 7 days, 14 days or 1 month.
- **C-4 Obstetrics:** blood pressure 140 systolic or 90 diastolic and above = "Possible pre-eclampsia" with a PIH work-up order (urine protein, LFT, platelets, uric acid); Hb below 11 mild, below 10 moderate, below 7 severe anaemia, with advice text ("Continue oral iron", "Consider IV iron", "Needs urgent evaluation"); fundal height differing by more than 2 weeks from gestational age; fetal heart rate outside 110 to 160; "High risk: previous LSCS"; scan schedule (dating 6 to 9 w, anomaly 18 to 22 w, growth 28 to 32 w, Doppler or NST from 36 w); OGTT at 24 to 28 w; Td dose; expected date from LMP.
- **C-5 Paediatrics:** dose by weight (paracetamol 15 mg/kg, at most 500 mg a dose, syrups of 120 mg and 250 mg per 5 ml; ibuprofen 10 mg/kg, at most 400 mg; frequency text such as every 6 hours, at most 4 doses a day); weight-for-age centile notes and length "~40th centile"; WHO 0–5 y growth chart; IAP immunization schedule with due and overdue; milestones list for 18 months.
- **C-6 Physiotherapy:** NPRS 0 to 10 pain scale; range of motion normal values (for example knee flexion); manual muscle testing 0 to 5 with right and left; special tests positive or negative; package of 10 sessions.
- **C-7 Surgery:** pre-op work-up tests and their states; fitness for surgery; consent checklist; WHO surgical safety checklist; stay length per procedure (Day care, 1 day, 1 to 2 days, 4 to 5 days).
- **C-8 Oncology:** body surface area = square root of (height × weight / 3600), optionally capped at 2.0 m²; protocol doses per m² from an approved library (AC: doxorubicin 60 and cyclophosphamide 600 mg/m², every 21 days); dose levels and a mandatory reason for reduction; rounding to the nearest vial size within 5% of calculated; doses recalculate if weight changes by 10% or more; lifetime anthracycline limit (doxorubicin under 450 mg/m²); lab gates (Hb 8 g/dL, neutrophils 1,500 /µL, platelets 100 ×10³/µL, creatinine 1.5 mg/dL, bilirubin 1.5 mg/dL, ALT 100 U/L, LVEF 50%) and labs drawn within 72 hours; side effects graded 0 to 4 for nine symptoms; ECOG 0 to 4 (fit for chemo at 2 or lower); response category from the change in the sum of lesion diameters (complete response, partial response, stable, progressive); "suggested decision" engine (stop, delay for failed labs or fever, change regimen for progression or low LVEF, reduce for grade 3 or fever with low counts, else proceed) with a mandatory reason when the doctor differs; independent second check by someone other than the prescriber (the checker confirms with their own sign-in or colleague PIN before the order is sent: OQ-45-33, SO-45-42).
- **C-9 Form builder scales:** Glasgow Coma Scale, Morse fall risk scale, pre-anaesthetic (ASA grade) and diabetic foot risk score are starter forms with scores and bands; band cut-offs and normal ranges are those of the mockup's starter forms.
- **C-10 Clinical lists:** all quick-option, chip and field content (complaints, systems, signs, history chips, development, feeding, EMR list starter items per department) is a starter list as drawn (OQ-43-8 clinician review still advised before real use).

### New questions (few; each has a default, nothing is blocked)
All questions below, OQ-45-27 to OQ-45-32 and OQ-45-34, are **answered: default accepted (DEC-039 addendum "database path, remaining defaults, chemo second check", 12 Nov 2026)**, as is OQ-43-27 (one EMR record per IPD day and per ER case). OQ-45-33 is answered differently from its default (see below). The "Default" wording in each item is now the decided rule.

- **OQ-45-27 · SMS, handwriting recognition, voice input and teleconsult.** *Answered: default accepted (DEC-039 addendum).* The human said nothing explicit before. The mockup shows SMS reminders for follow-ups, "Tap and write" handwriting recognition and voice dictation (General Medicine), a handwriting pad (Form builder) and a Teleconsult visit type. **Default: Later, shown disabled with a "Later" note.** Affects SAH-45-6, SAH-47, SAH-59-1, SAH-60, SAH-51.
- **OQ-45-28 · Registers, schedules and services that no module has.** *Answered: default accepted (DEC-039 addendum).* Lab results and orders, Pharmacy and chemo pharmacy, documents service (photos, file upload, signature images), ANC register, vaccination register, OT schedule, ward patient list, tumour board, packages and exercise library lists, day-care chairs. **Default: Later, shown disabled with a "Later" note; what the doctor types in the EMR is kept; photo, file and signature-image fields accept no file until the documents service exists.** Affects SAH-47, SAH-57, SAH-59, SAH-63 to SAH-66.
- **OQ-45-29 · PCPNDT lock against "only Allergies".** *Answered: default accepted (DEC-039 addendum).* OQ-45-6 (explicit) says only Allergies cannot be hidden; OQ-45-7 (default accepted) says PCPNDT Form F cannot be hidden in Obstetrics & Gynaecology. **Default: both apply (Allergies everywhere, PCPNDT Form F in Obstetrics & Gynaecology) until the human says the second lock is dropped.** Affects SAH-45-2, SAH-53-2, SAH-61.
- **OQ-45-30 · Tap target size.** *Answered: default accepted (DEC-039 addendum).* The mockup chips are 32 px; the BRD and SAH-43 (C-1) use 44 px. DEC-039 says the mockup wins for differences. **Default: 44 px for every tap target (touch use on tablets); the look otherwise follows the mockup.** Affects all screens.

New with the phase 4 and 5 stories (12 Nov 2026, later; each has a default and nothing is blocked):
- **OQ-45-31 · Can a doctor admit from the EMR?** *Answered: default accepted (DEC-039 addendum).* The admit roles of SAH-23 and SAH-28 are owner, front desk and nurse; a doctor who is not the owner cannot create an admission. The Admit to IPD button and the "Admit to IPD" ER outcome need a path. **Default: the doctor sends a pre-filled admission request; owner, front desk and nurse complete it (bed, deposit, consent); the owner can admit directly.** Affects SAH-67-2, SAH-67-3, SAH-64-3, SAH-52-4.
- **OQ-45-32 · Reference data and wording the mockup does not give.** *Answered: default accepted (DEC-039 addendum).* (a) WHO growth centile tables and milestone lists for ages other than the 18 months drawn; (b) Marathi and Hindi words for the vaccination card, home exercise programme and chemo calendar headings. **Default: (a) centile note and milestone list show "not available / not set up for this age" and nothing is guessed; the chart point is still plotted; (b) English headings until reviewed wording is supplied; case paper words (SAH-55) are reused where the word is the same.** Affects SAH-62-1, SAH-62-2, SAH-62-4, SAH-63-4, SAH-70-1.
- **OQ-45-33 · How is the chemotherapy independent second check confirmed?** *Answered by the human (DEC-039 addendum): the checker confirms with their own sign-in or the colleague PIN mechanism built in SAH-38, before the order can be sent to pharmacy. This differs from the mockup, which only lets the prescriber pick the checker's name; the difference is the human's decision (SO-45-42).* The mockup lets the prescriber pick the verifier's name (Sr. nurse, Pharmacist, another doctor) on the same screen; nurse and pharmacist do not use the EMR. **Decided rule: the named checker (nurse, pharmacist or resident doctor, never the prescriber) confirms with their own sign-in, or with their colleague PIN typed on the prescriber's device (SAH-38 mechanism, including its wrong-try limits and lock); the name, role, way of confirming and time are recorded; "Sign and send to pharmacy" stays disabled until the confirmation is done.** The earlier default (name picked only, no confirmation by the checker) is withdrawn. Affects SAH-66-2. Built in SAH-66-2 and the new SAH-66-3. Open design point for the architect (not a human question): the colleague PIN of SAH-32 and SAH-38 was built for a second nurse; every role may set a PIN (ADR-010), so the architect decides how it is reused for a pharmacist or resident doctor, and the layout of the checker's small confirmation page (not drawn in the mockup).
- **OQ-45-34 · "Print Rx & call next patient".** *Answered: default accepted (DEC-039 addendum).* The Queue has no "call next patient" function. **Default: the label stays as drawn; the confirm prints and returns to the Queue where the next waiting patient is at the top; no announcement or display call is built.** Affects SAH-51-1, SAH-51-2.
