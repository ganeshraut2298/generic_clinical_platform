## Problem
After Masters, a unit has stores, staff, products, vendors, approval rules and an approved opening-stock file, but no stock. Nothing can be bought, received, issued to a ward, transferred, counted or reported, and Phase 3 (Pharmacy) has nothing to sell: "Pharmacy sells from the stock that Inventory creates" (BRD section 1).

Today Sahaj has no stock, batch, vendor, purchase or ledger code anywhere (feasibility report section 4). The hospital runs these on paper or in spreadsheets, which causes the problems the BRD targets: expired medicines found on the shelf, ward stores that run out because indents are verbal, rejected or missing items that nobody owns, and no trail of who moved what.

Phase 2 delivers the ten Inventory screens of the mockup at the "basic features in this release" level (BRD I1 to I10): every stock movement works, is approved where the unit's rules say so, and is written once to a ledger that cannot be edited. Analytics and the "Later" column stay out.

## Who uses it (roles) and in which setting (clinic, hospital ward, front desk)
Settings: the Central Store (receiving goods, dispatching), sub-stores and ward stores (indents, receiving, consumption, counting), the office (owner approvals and reports). The front desk and the billing role do not use this phase.

Roles are the SAH-78 roles (SAH-78 "Who uses it"): owner; pharmacist (store role Pharmacist); Central Store user (store role, only at the Central Store); store user; HOD (store role Approver, or the delegate while the delegate dates are current); Store staff team role (store keepers and assistants, mapped as Store user or Central Store user). A person's powers come from the store role plus the store's flags, worked out on the server (SAH-78-4).

| Action in Phase 2 | Who may do it |
| --- | --- |

Front desk, counsellor, visiting doctor and a pharmacist with no store mapping get no Pharmacy & Inventory entry (ADR-028 D11).

## Business rules (numbered)
Scope, tenant, access
- BR-1. The same tenancy and access rules as SAH-78 apply to every call (SAH-78 BR-1, BR-2, BR-11, BR-12). The clinic (unit) comes from the signed-in user, never from the request. Another clinic's record answers 404. Every store-bound call has `{storeId}` in its path; a store of this clinic to which the caller is not mapped answers 404; a mapped person without the capability gets 403 `store-permission-denied` (audited); bad input is 400; the store check runs before any filter, sort or paging. The owner reaches every store and has every store capability except pharmacist-only actions.
- BR-2. The module switch `pharmacy_on` Off (the default) answers 403 `module-off`, and with it Off every existing screen, API and test behaves exactly as before (SAH-78 BR-3, BR-32).
- BR-3. Every Inventory screen works in both Integration modes. Only patient-wise ward consumption needs Integrated mode and IPD On; in Standalone it is hidden, and the server answers 409 `integration-off` (BRD section 6, AC-I6; ADR-028 D5).

Stock and ledger
- BR-4. Stock is held per store, product and batch, in four states: Available (on the shelf), In transit (dispatched to this store, not yet received), Pending return (rejected or returned, waiting for the other side to accept), Quarantine (damaged, expired or on hold). Only Available stock may be issued, dispatched, transferred, consumed or sold (BRD section 8).
- BR-5. Every movement writes ledger lines in the same step as the document that causes it. A line holds: date and time (IST), transaction, reference (document number), store, state, product, batch, quantity in, quantity out, balance after, user and reason. A move between stores or states writes two lines (out of the source, in at the destination). The ledger is append-only: no edit, no delete, enforced in code and in the database. A correction is a new reversing or adjustment line that points to the line it corrects, never a change (ADR-028 D6; fixed rule 3 of BRD section 2).
- BR-6. Stock never goes negative. A movement that would take a balance below zero is refused with nothing posted (409 `insufficient-stock` with the available quantity), also when two users take the last units at the same moment. The database also holds this rule (ADR-028 D6).
- BR-7. FEFO everywhere: any issue, dispatch, transfer or consumption picks Available, unexpired batches by earliest expiry first, then earliest received. The user cannot choose another batch. FEFO is fixed On (SAH-78 BR-8). Batch-specific actions (vendor return, adjustment, count, damage report, return to Central Store) name the batch because the stock is already identified.
- BR-8. An expired batch is never usable. A batch whose expiry date has passed is moved from Available to Quarantine (reason "Expired", ledger lines, user shown as "System") by the first screen or action that finds it, with no scheduled job (ADR-028 D6). An expired batch can never be received into, put back into or released to Available. A batch is valid through its expiry date and expired from the next day (IST). Expiry is entered as month and year and counts to the last day of that month (OQ-79-7).
- BR-9. Quantities are whole numbers of the issue unit. Purchase quantities are converted at receiving (purchase quantity times the product conversion, SAH-78 BR-15). Document amounts are held in whole paise; unit rates after conversion are held to 4 decimals; a line amount rounds half up to the paisa; GST is worked per line on the taxable value (ADR-028 D10). Money is shown as ₹ 1,25,000 or ₹ 1,25,000.50.
- BR-10. A batch is a product, a batch number and an expiry. Receiving the same batch again adds to it when the MRP is the same; the same batch number and expiry with a different MRP is refused (an MRP is printed per batch). MRP and purchase rate are kept on the batch and on each receipt line (OQ-79-6). The MRP of an existing batch is fixed. A wrong MRP is corrected by emptying the batch by a stock adjustment and receiving a new batch (go-live check for the pharmacist, OQ-D-2 of `docs/database/SAH-79-inventory.md`, accepted in `docs/handoffs/SAH-78.md`). Batch numbers that differ only in case are one batch (OQ-79-37, DECIDED).
- BR-11. Opening stock approved in SAH-78-7 is posted to the ledger as "Opening stock" lines in the Available state, once, as one all-or-nothing step; until posted it is not usable stock (SAH-78 BR-30; ADR-028 OQ-PHI-9, decided). Lines whose expiry passed between approval and posting are posted straight to Quarantine (OQ-79-30).

Approvals (the runtime of the rules stored in SAH-78-9)
- BR-12. Approval is configured, not coded. When a document is submitted the server reads the unit's rule for that transaction type (levels 0, 1 or 2, the role of each level, conditions, ₹ limits) and keeps a copy of the rule used. Level 0: no approval, the document proceeds at once. A rule change applies to documents submitted afterwards only; documents already waiting keep the rule they started with.
- BR-13. Fixed rule: nobody approves their own request. Enforced in the service and by a database check; no setting switches it off. If the only eligible approver is the requester, the item goes to the next approver and shows "Needs another approver" (OQ-79-28).
- BR-14. "Store approver" means the HOD of the store the rule names for that transaction, or the delegate while the delegate dates are current (IST, SAH-78 BR-10): the requesting store for an indent; the sending store for a transfer; the store that reports for a write-off; the store counted for an adjustment or audit; the Central Store for a purchase order, a rate rise and a vendor return. "Owner" means any owner. A store with no HOD and no current delegate shows its waiting items to the owner.
- BR-15. Every decision is saved with who, in what capacity (HOD, delegate, owner, escalated), when, the decision and the reason. A rejection needs a reason. A decision is final; two people deciding at once: the second gets 409.
- BR-16. Escalation: an item that has waited longer than its rule's time (defaults: indent 2 hours, urgent indent 30 minutes, transfer 24 hours, set in SAH-78-9) also shows to the next approver and is marked "Escalated". This is worked out when the inbox or a list is read; there is no timer job and no notification (ADR-028 D7, OQ-79-29).
- BR-17. The amount that a rule's "above ₹" condition looks at is: the purchase order total including GST; for every other transaction, the quantity times the purchase rate per issue unit of the batches involved, without GST (OQ-79-36).

Purchasing
- BR-18. Purchase orders, receiving stock, vendor returns and debit notes belong to the Central Store, the only store that buys from vendors (SAH-78 BR-9). A purchase order needs an active vendor that supplies the Central Store and is not blocked; an expired drug licence blocks a new purchase order (AC-M5; SAH-78 BR-21). Existing purchase orders of a vendor that becomes blocked are untouched and can still be received (OQ-79-9d). A blocked vendor stays on the vendor list with the mark "Blocked · licence expired" but is not offered on the purchase order, direct receipt and vendor return forms (OQ-79-39). The credit days of a purchase order are filled from the vendor when the field is left empty.
- BR-19. A purchase order has statuses Draft, Waiting for approval, Approved, Sent to vendor, Partly received, Received (or "Received · n short" when closed short), Cancelled, plus the mark "Overdue" when the expected delivery date has passed with goods outstanding. "Sent" means the user printed it and marked it sent (OQ-79-32). A document gets its number from its series when first submitted; a draft has no number.
- BR-20. Receiving stock (GRN) is done manually (no OCR, no e-invoice), against a purchase order or direct. Each line has batch, expiry, quantity received, quantity billed, free quantity, rate, MRP, discount and GST. Short supply against the purchase order and a rate above the purchase order rate are flagged on the line. A rate rise above the unit's limit holds the receipt for approval and posts nothing until approved (OQ-79-10). The same vendor invoice number cannot be received twice for a vendor.
- BR-21. Posting a receipt adds the received and free quantities to the Central Store as Available, creates or tops up the batches, and creates a vendor payable entry for the invoice amount with a due date of invoice date plus credit days. The payable is a pharmacy-owned record; the billing module has no supplier or payable concept (OQ-79-5). Posting is all-or-nothing.
- BR-22. A vendor return and debit note picks the vendor's batches that are in Quarantine at the Central Store or near expiry inside the vendor's return policy, plus short supply from a receipt. Credit is worked from the vendor's policy (OQ-79-11). Selected units are held so nobody can use them and leave the stock when handed to the vendor (OQ-79-12). The debit note is tracked to the vendor's credit note, which reduces the vendor payable.

Indents (kept from the validated requirement, BRD section 4)
- BR-23. An indent is raised by a store user for their store, to the store it issues from (the Central Store in this release), with priority Routine or Urgent. Approval stays with the HOD or active delegate; receiving is open to every person mapped to the receiving store. The HOD can approve, reject or change a line quantity; a remark is required when rejecting or reducing. An approval with every approved quantity 0 is refused ("Reject the indent with a reason instead"). The form shows the issuing store from the start, taken from the store the indents of the requesting store go to.
- BR-24. The Central Store can change the product or the quantity of a line with a reason, dispatches by FEFO, and chooses for each unmet balance "Back-order" (stays open on the same indent for a follow-up dispatch) or "Close balance". One indent can have several dispatches; each dispatch has its own receipt (DECIDED, DEC-063 Q1 A, ADR-028 OQ-PHI-16). The requesting store sees every product or quantity change and its reason before it receives.
- BR-25. Receipt per line: accepted plus rejected equals dispatched. A reason is required for any rejected quantity: damaged in transit, wrong item, near expiry or expired, excess quantity, short or not received. Accepted quantity enters the receiving store as Available at once. Rejected quantity waits as Pending return in the receiving store and enters the Central Store only when the Central Store accepts it, into saleable stock or quarantine. Short or not received is a shortage for the Central Store to investigate, never a physical return, and never enters the receiving store's Available or Pending return stock (OQ-79-18). Each line is received once (DEC-075 Q-1; replaces "received once, in full"). A person who is not a pharmacist can receive the lines that are not Schedule X; the Schedule X lines stay in transit until a pharmacist receives them with a second signature (BR-32), and the dispatch is "Partly received" until then; the indent has no "Partly received" status and stays "Partly dispatched" with the chip "· Schedule X lines waiting for a pharmacist". A re-send of the receipt for a partly received dispatch carries only the lines not yet received. On the indent, the chips "· n rejected, waiting for CS" (the quantity the Central Store has not yet accepted) and "· n short, CS investigating" (the shortage quantity not yet settled) win over the label "Received · <product> back-order open". Schedule X units are never Available while they wait.
- BR-26. Every step of an indent, transfer, return, damage report, adjustment and audit is saved with user, store, time, before and after quantity and reason, and is shown on the document's history.

Other movements
- BR-27. A transfer moves stock from one store to another store of the unit. It works like an indent: requested, approved by the sending store's HOD (mockup 12 and 17, OQ-79-20), dispatched by FEFO, received by any mapped person at the receiving store with accept or reject and reason; rejected units go back to the sending store as Pending return. One transfer not received within the transfer time (default 24 hours) shows "Not received" and appears for both store HODs, computed when read.
- BR-28. Ward consumption takes Available stock of the ward or store by FEFO, either against an admitted patient (Integrated mode) or as general ward use with a purpose (both modes). Store-bound capability "Consume / return" and the store's flag "Consumption entry = Yes" are both needed. Patient-wise entries keep the admission only (never a patient name) and show "Charge pending" until SAH-80 posts the IPD charge (OQ-79-23).
- BR-29. A store can return Available stock to the Central Store without an indent (reason chip); the units wait as Pending return until the Central Store accepts them. Damaged or expired stock is reported with a reason chip, moves to Quarantine at once so it cannot be used, and waits for the HOD, who approves either a write-off at the store or sending it to the Central Store.
- BR-30. A stock audit is a blind count: the counter never receives the system quantity (the book quantity is not shown to the counter; a person who can review the count sees it). While a store has an open audit, the stock screens, lists, reports, the stock ledger and error messages of that store show no quantity, for the products in the audit, to a person who can count but cannot review the count; the quantity fields are empty and the screen shows "Counting in progress" (NEW; the mockup `13` has no wording for it); the rule ends when the audit is Closed (DEC-080 L12; story rule C-9). After the count is submitted the HOD sees system quantity, counted quantity and difference for lines with a difference, gives each a reason chip, can send a line for recount, and approves; approved differences are posted as ledger adjustment lines. The book quantity is the quantity at the time the count is submitted; a batch that moved since then must be recounted (OQ-79-26).
- BR-31. A manual stock adjustment (book to physical, reason required) follows the approval rule for stock adjustment, including the Schedule X flag, and posts as ledger lines.

Schedule X
- BR-32. Receiving, issuing, dispatching, counting or adjusting a Schedule X item needs a person with store role Pharmacist (never the owner) and a second signature by a different active person through the colleague PIN check; both names are stored (fixed rule 2 of BRD section 2; ADR-028 D7). The second signer is chosen from the other active pharmacists and owners of the unit; the list comes from the server and never contains the person signing first (OQ-79-8; SAH-78 G-6 stays a go-live check). A stock movement of a Schedule X product by a person is not accepted by the server without the second signer, and the stock ledger refuses it too (a database trigger on the person-driven transaction types of ADR-029 P7, which the architect lists; DEC-080 L14; story rule C-10). Movements by the system need no signer. NDPS is not modelled in SAH-78 and is a question in OQ-79-8. Posting approved opening stock that contains Schedule X items needs no second signature: the poster is a person who may post, and the file was approved by another owner (ADR-029 A-8, DECIDED, DEC-075 item 4; go-live check for the pharmacist, G-15).

Documents, reasons, print, export
- BR-33. Documents use the numbering series of SAH-78-10: one number per document type, taken in the same step that saves the document, no gaps for saved documents, a cancelled document keeps its number, nothing is hard-deleted. Document types the mockup needs beyond SAH-78-10's five (debit note, stock audit, return to Central Store, damage report, stock adjustment) are OQ-79-14.
- BR-34. Reasons are chips from the reason lists of SAH-78-10, not free text. An optional note may be added; the reason "Other" needs a note of at least 4 characters. Lists the mockup needs that SAH-78-10 does not seed are OQ-79-15.
- BR-35. Printing (purchase order, issue slip, pick list, debit note, count sheet) uses the browser print view with the print-template settings of SAH-78-10. Reports are shown on screen and exported as CSV (UTF-8); "Save as PDF" is the browser's (OQ-79-4).
- BR-36. UI text: plain English, sentence case; "Receive stock", not "GRN" alone (the one exception is the receipt button, which reads "Post GRN & update stock" as the mockup `08` does); money ₹ 1,25,000; dates 24 Oct 2026; times 11:05 AM IST; batch expiry as the mockup shows it, month and year, "Mar 2028" (OQ-79-7). Approvals, receiving, stock counting and ward consumption must work on a tablet or phone (BRD section 7). Hide what is not configured; never grey it out.
- BR-37. Every create, change, decision and posting writes an audit event through the platform audit (who, when, what, before and after). The ledger and the approval decisions are the audit trail for stock and approvals. Logs and audit text carry ids, counts and outcome codes only: never a patient name, mobile number, ABHA number or clinical note, and not batch quantities in application logs.

## Patient-safety and privacy points
- Wrong stock quantity is a patient risk: a ward that thinks it has a vial it does not have delays treatment. Hence never-negative stock, one ledger line per movement, accepted plus rejected equals dispatched, and rejected stock that cannot vanish between stores (it sits as Pending return until someone accepts it).
- Expired and near-expiry medicines: expired batches are blocked from every issue and are quarantined automatically (BR-8); near-expiry lists let staff act first; FEFO keeps old stock moving.
- Substitution at dispatch (a different product than the one the ward asked for) is a clinical decision. The Central Store's choice of a replacement and the rule that limits it are OQ-79-16; the requesting store always sees the change with its reason before it receives, and may reject it as "Wrong item".
- Schedule X and similar controlled items: pharmacist-only handling and two signatures are fixed rules (BR-32). A wrong schedule flag in the product master (SAH-78) would let such an item be handled without them, which is why the schedule has no default there.
- Blind counts stop counters from writing the number they expect to see; the system quantity is not sent to the counter's screen at all (BR-30).
- Patient-wise consumption holds the admission link, which identifies a patient. It stores the admission only, shows the patient's name on screen to the nurse who needs to verify the right patient, and reads the patient through the patient and IPD ports with a record-access entry (ADR-028 D9). Reports and the ledger show the admission number, not the name. Nothing patient-related is logged. Patient data never leaves India (all data hosted in India).
- Vendor, licence, GSTIN and invoice data are business data, never logged; they may be in audit.
- Store-scoped access is on the server for every call (BR-1): a ward nurse cannot see or act on another store by changing the address.
- No real patient data in test data. Seed vendors, batches and licences are marked "Sample".

### Stock, pricing and accounting
- OQ-79-5 · OPEN (Human; billing and accounting). Vendor payable. BRD I3 says a receipt "posts to ... vendor payable". Finding: the billing module (`billing.bill`, `bill_item`, `payment`, `receipt_counter`, course payment and credit transfer) is patient receivables only; there is no vendor, supplier, payable or credit-note concept anywhere in Sahaj. Options: (A) pharmacy-owned payable record in the `pharmacy` schema: one entry per posted receipt (vendor, invoice number and date, amount, due date from credit days), reduced by vendor credit notes (79-8), shown on the receipt and the debit-note tracking, in the "Debit notes pending" report and the purchase register; no payment recording; (B) add payables to the billing module (a new concept there: schema change in a module the human says should not change, and a bigger design). Recommended: A, because it keeps billing untouched (DEC-061 additive rule) and the BRD only asks for the receipt to "post" to payable and the credit note to adjust it. Recording vendor payments, ageing and an accounts export are not built; a later accounts story decides where they live.
- OQ-79-6 · OPEN (Human; pricing, accountant). Landed cost, margin and stock value. The mockup shows PTR, landed per unit, margin and "stock value ₹18.6 L (landed)". Landed spreads "free quantity, scheme discount, GST credit and freight". The mockup's numbers include GST in the landed value (for example 22.36 for a rate of 22.40 with 2% discount, 4 free on 40 and 12% GST) although its text says GST credit is spread. Recommended: purchase rate per issue unit = rate after line discount, spread over paid plus free units, without GST (input GST credit is claimed separately by the unit's accountant); landed = that rate; margin = (MRP − landed) ÷ MRP; stock value = quantity times landed. Freight is not captured (no freight field in the mockup grid). A repeat receipt of the same batch at a different rate keeps each receipt's rate on its line and the batch shows the latest. Build on this recommendation, label columns plainly, and confirm with an accountant before real use (go-live item G-13).
- OQ-79-9 · OPEN. Receiving stock details the mockup draws without rules. (a) Billed quantity versus received quantity: the "3-way match" card (USP chip) and the debit note for "2 short boxes" need both; the grid has only one Qty. (b) Invoice total: recommended the user types the total as printed on the paper invoice; the screen shows the difference from the computed total; posting is blocked when the difference is more than ₹ 1 (rounding allowance, a placeholder for the accountant). (c) Debit note for a rate difference: the mockup auto-drafts a debit note for "2 short boxes + rate difference ₹253.06".
