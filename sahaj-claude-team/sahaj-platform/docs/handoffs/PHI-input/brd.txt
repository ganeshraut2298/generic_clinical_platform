1. Purpose, scope and build order

Sahaj gets one Pharmacy & Inventory module, built in three phases (Masters → Inventory
→ Pharmacy), that runs inside Sahaj with OPD and IPD or on its own for a standalone
pharmacy or store. This BRD follows the 18-screen mockup (Sahaj Pharmacy & Inventory –
USP Mockup) but keeps each feature at a basic, buildable level.

In scope: masters and configuration, purchase to stock, store-to-store movement, ward
consumption, counter sale, OPD/IPD dispensing, returns, registers, approvals and reports.

Out of scope for this release: invoice OCR, distributor B2B connect, ABC/XYZ analytics,
cold-chain sensors, refill reminders, AI reorder, multi-branch HO purchasing. They stay in
the mockup as later USPs.

Build order

  1. Phase 1 – Masters (configuration): unit, stores, staff–store mapping, product master,
     vendors, approval rules, numbering. Nothing else can run without these.

  2. Phase 2 – Inventory: purchase order, GRN, vendor return, batch stock, indents,
     transfers, ward consumption, returns/damaged, stock audit, approvals inbox, reports.

  3. Phase 3 – Pharmacy: command center, POS billing, OPD/IPD dispensing, sales return,
     registers and compliance.

Each phase is released and tested before the next starts, because Pharmacy sells from
the stock that Inventory creates, and Inventory needs the stores and products that
Masters define.

Configurable approval levels (basic)

Approvals are set per transaction type in Masters, not coded:

Setting    Values
Levels
Condition  0, 1 or 2, each tied to a role
           Store type, amount (₹), item flag (Schedule X, high
Absence    value), priority
Limits
           Delegate with dates; escalate after N hours
           ₹ limit for PO, write-off, discount, sales return,
           stock adjustment

Ship three presets: Lean (no HOD step), Standard (one level, HOD) and Strict (HOD then
owner above a limit). Fixed rules that no setting can switch off: nobody approves their
own request, Schedule X needs a pharmacist and a second signature, and the stock ledger
cannot be edited.

Product master rules

     A unit adopts a global product and sets only local fields; global fields stay read-only.
     A unit can create its own product. Before saving, the system checks salt + strength +
     form + manufacturer and offers the matching global product first.
     Every product has a purchase-to-issue unit conversion (Box of 10 → vial). All
     transactions after GRN use the issue unit.
     In Integrated mode, the same product master feeds OPD/IPD prescribing so doctors
     pick what pharmacy stocks.

Opening stock

A one-time import (Excel template) loads store, product, batch, expiry, MRP, purchase
rate and quantity. It posts as an opening-stock line in the ledger and needs owner
approval.

Indent rules (kept from the validated requirement)

     Approval stays with the HOD or active delegate; receiving is open to every user
     mapped to the receiving store.
     Receipt per line: accepted + rejected = dispatched. Reason is required for any
     rejected quantity: damaged, wrong item, near expiry/expired, excess, short/not
     received.
     Accepted quantity enters the receiving store at once. Rejected quantity waits as
     Pending return and enters Central Store only when CS accepts it, into saleable stock
     or quarantine.
     Short / not received is a shortage for CS to investigate, never a physical return.
     Every step is saved with user, store, time, before/after quantity and reason.

Pharmacy rules

     Substitution: only same salt, strength and dosage form; reason saved on the bill.
     Schedule H1: doctor name and registration, patient and quantity are written to the
     register at sale; no manual register.
     Allergy (Integrated mode only): if the EMR has an allergy for a drug's salt, the sale is
     blocked; a pharmacist override needs a reason.
     Offline billing (basic): a counter keeps selling during an internet outage and syncs
     later; stock conflicts go to the pharmacist on sync.
     Day close: cash tally by payment mode at shift end.

Integration rules

     Pharmacy & Inventory owns its own tables (stock, ledger, bills). It reads patient, Rx,
     order and admission data, and writes only charges, credits and dispense status back.
     Integration goes through one adapter layer (service or events). Switching Integration
     mode to Standalone turns the adapter off; no screen code changes.
     If OPD/IPD is unreachable, pharmacy keeps working in standalone style and links the
     bill to the patient later.

7. UI/UX rules

The existing Sahaj shell stays exactly as it is; only the main content area follows the
mockup.

     Sidebar and header: use the current Sahaj sidebar, top bar, logo, user menu and
     theme as they are in the product today. Do not copy the mockup's sidebar styling.
     Module entry: add one sidebar entry, Pharmacy & Inventory. Inside it, three tabs in
     this order: Inventory setup, Inventory, Pharmacy, matching the build order. Each tab
     lists only its own screens.
     Main content: follow the mockup's layout per screen (header, queue on the left with
     detail on the right, tables, one main action button), using Sahaj's existing components,
     fonts, colours and spacing.
     Speed at the counter: keyboard shortcuts on POS and dispensing (search, hold,
     save), barcode first, one primary button per screen.
     Mobile and tablet: approvals, receiving, stock count and ward consumption must
     work on a tablet or phone.
     Plain words: "Receive stock", not "GRN" alone; reasons as chips, not free text.
     Hide what is not configured: if Integration mode is Standalone, OPD/IPD queues and
     patient-bill links are hidden, not greyed out.

Stock state  Meaning                                         Usable
Quarantine   Damaged, expired or on hold                     No

     FEFO everywhere; expired batches blocked; stock never goes negative.
     Ledger line: date/time, transaction, reference, store, state, batch, in, out, balance, user,
     reason. Corrections are reversing lines.

Non-functional

     Multi-tenant: organization → unit isolation; global product master shared read-only.
     POS search under 300 ms; bill save under 1 s.
     Role and store based access on every API, not only on screens.
     No hard delete of transactions; full audit trail.
     Data hosted in India; encryption in transit and at rest; daily backup; full export.
