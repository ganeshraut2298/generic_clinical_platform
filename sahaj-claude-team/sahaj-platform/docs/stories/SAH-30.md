# SAH-30 · Nursing vitals, nursing notes and early warning score (IPD, part 9)
Mockup screen: Nursing sheet (card → Nursing) — the "Vitals and notes" tab of the Nursing sheet (`pcNursing`, `pcvsave`), the early warning score (`pcEws`), the escalation raise (`sfRaise`, `sfLog`, the wrapped `ACT.pcvsave` of the Safety layer)
Module: ipd

## 1. Story
As a nurse on a ward, I want to record a patient's five vital signs and a short note, see the early warning score at once, and have a medium or high score raise an escalation for the doctor, so that a patient who is getting worse is noticed and seen within a set time.

Scope note: this story records vitals and notes, calculates the score on the server, and raises or updates the escalation record. What the doctor does with an escalation (Acknowledge, Record response, "Call senior" and the banner and counters of the Safety tab) is the Safety board story, which is not part of this story; until it exists a raised escalation is stored and audited but no screen lets a doctor act on it (OQ-30-2, high priority: ship the Safety board right after this story). The mockup says "duty doctor alerted" on a high score: alerts need the notifications service (not in phase 1), so no message is sent and the toast does not claim one. The Medicines (MAR) tab of the Nursing sheet is SAH-32. The mockup opens the Nursing sheet from the Nursing button of the patient card; the card is not built (Admitted is still the table of SAH-23), so this story adds a **Nursing** button to the admission sheet instead (OQ-30-1). The colour bands of single vitals on the card (`vStat`), the "Vitals due" and "EWS" flags of the card, and the ward-wise nurse assignment ("Assigned nurse") also belong to the card story. The API still returns `vitalsDue` and the latest score so that the card can use them.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given IPD is Trial or On and an Admitted patient, then the admission sheet (SAH-23/25) footer gets a **Nursing** button (nurse icon) for OWNER, NURSE and DOCTOR (FRONT_DESK and BILLING get no button and the API answers 403). In Read-only state the button still opens the sheet to read, without the save button. It opens the sheet "Nursing · <patient name>" with the subtitle "Bed <code> · last vitals 35 min ago" (when no vitals yet: "Bed <code>"); "ago" reads "N min ago" under an hour and "H h M min ago" or "H h ago" from an hour.
2. Given the sheet, then it shows in this order: when there are vitals, a notice with the score — **"Early warning score N · Low|Medium|High"** followed by ". Routine vitals every 4 hours." (Low, green), ". Repeat vitals within 1 hour." (Medium, amber) or ". Inform the doctor now and repeat vitals every 30 minutes." (High, red); then five fields, each with its unit — **BP** mmHg (placeholder 120/80), **Pulse** /min (80), **SpO₂** % (98), **Temp** °C (37.0), **RR** /min (16) — prefilled with the last recorded values; **Nursing note** (3 rows, placeholder "What you observed and did"); and **Recent notes**: the last three notes, newest first, each as "35 min ago · <nurse name>" and the text, or "No notes yet". Footer: **Close** and, for roles that may record, **Save vitals and note** (check icon).
3. Given a role that may view but not record vitals (DOCTOR), then the fields and the note are disabled, the notice "Your role can view vitals but not record them." shows, and there is no save button. Recording is allowed to NURSE and OWNER (as in the mockup's default access; OQ-30-3).
4. Given **Save vitals and note**, then all five vitals are required: BP as two whole numbers "systolic/diastolic" of 2 or 3 digits each (for example 120/80), pulse, SpO₂ and RR whole numbers above 0, temperature a number above 0 with at most one decimal; otherwise 400 and the message "Enter all five vitals. BP as 120/80." Plausible-range limits to catch typing mistakes are not defined in the mockup (OQ-30-4). The note is optional (if empty, no note is stored) and up to 1,000 characters (proposed, OQ-30-4).
5. Given a valid save, then one vitals record is stored (the five values, the recording time on the server clock, the user) with the score and risk **calculated by the server** (the client never sends a score), and the note, if any, is stored with the same user and time. Nothing is edited or deleted afterwards: a wrong entry is followed by a new one (OQ-30-5). The sheet reloads showing the new score, "last vitals 0 min ago" and the new note.
6. Given the score, then it is the sum of five parameter scores: **SpO₂** 91 or less = 3, 92 to 93 = 2, 94 to 95 = 1, above 95 = 0. **RR** 8 or less, or 25 or more = 3; 21 to 24 = 2; 9 to 11 = 1; 12 to 20 = 0. **Pulse** 40 or less, or 131 or more = 3; 111 to 130 = 2; 91 to 110, or 41 to 50 = 1; 51 to 90 = 0. **Temp** 35.0 or less = 3; 39.1 or more = 2; 38.1 to 39.0, or 35.1 to 36.0 = 1; 36.1 to 38.0 = 0. **Systolic BP** 90 or less, or 220 or more = 3; 91 to 100 = 2; 101 to 110 = 1; 111 to 219 = 0 (the diastolic value is stored but not scored). **Risk**: High when the total is 7 or more; Medium when the total is 5 or more, or any one parameter scores 3; otherwise Low. The thresholds are exactly those of the mockup's `pcEws`; they have not been clinically validated for Sahaj (OQ-30-6, blocking for go-live).
7. Given a Low score, then the toast is "Vitals saved for <patient name> · early warning N (low)" and no escalation is raised.
8. Given a Medium or High score, then an escalation is raised or updated in the same transaction: with no open escalation (Open, or Acknowledged and not yet closed) for the admission, a new one is created with the score, the risk, the raising user and time, status Open and the response target **15 minutes for High, 1 hour for Medium**; with an open one, a higher new score replaces its score, risk and target (the original raise time stays) and a score equal or lower changes nothing. Toast: "Escalation raised for <patient name> · EWS N · doctor review within 15 min" (or "within 1 hour"); no message is sent to anyone (OQ-30-2).
9. Given the clinical event log (new, used by SAH-31/32 and the Safety board), then every save writes "Recorded vitals · EWS N (medium)" (kind info) and, when an escalation is raised, "Raised early warning escalation · EWS N (medium) · doctor review within 1 hour" (kind esc) or, when updated, "Escalation updated · EWS now N (high)" (kind esc), with the user and time. These entries hold no patient name and no note text, cannot be edited or deleted, and have no screen yet.
10. Given vitals due, then an admission has `vitalsDue = true` when it has no vitals or the last record is more than 4 hours old; the Nursing sheet itself shows no flag for it.
11. Given a Read-only IPD, a discharged admission or a patient who is not Admitted, then saving answers 409 (`ipd-readonly`, `not-admitted`); IPD Off answers 403 `module-off`; an admission of another clinic answers 404 (RLS). Reading the sheet of a discharged patient stays possible for OWNER, NURSE and DOCTOR.
12. Given a slow or failed network, then Save is disabled while the request runs, a failed save keeps the values typed and shows the error, and the sheet is never shown as saved until the server has answered; there is no offline queue in this phase.
13. Given privacy, then vitals and notes are clinical data: they are shown on screen only and never logged. The audit event of a save (IPD_VITALS_RECORDED) holds the admission id, the score, the risk and whether a note was added, never the values or the note text. Opening the Nursing sheet is not written to the patient record-access log in this story (OQ-30-7).

## 3. Technical design
- New migration (db-engineer), all tables with `tenant_id` and FORCE RLS, composite FK to `ipd.admission`:
  - `ipd.vitals`: `id`, `admission_id`, `bp_systolic smallint`, `bp_diastolic smallint`, `pulse smallint`, `spo2 smallint`, `temp_c numeric(3,1)`, `rr smallint`, `ews_score smallint`, `ews_risk` check in LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH, `recorded_at` (db default), `recorded_by`. Append-only (select + column insert; trigger denies update/delete/truncate, as `ipd.deposit`). Index `(tenant_id, admission_id, recorded_at desc)`. This table grows fastest in IPD: design it so it can be partitioned by `recorded_at` later (rule: plan partitioning before 50 million rows).
  - `ipd.nursing_note`: `id`, `admission_id`, `note text` (<= 1000), `recorded_at`, `recorded_by`. Append-only.
  - `ipd.ews_escalation`: `id`, `admission_id`, `ews_score`, `ews_risk` (MEDIUM or HIGH), `target_minutes smallint` (15 or 60), `status` check in OPEN/ACK/DONE (default OPEN), `raised_at`, `raised_by`, `acknowledged_by/at`, `response_action`, `response_note`, `senior_calls smallint default 0`, `closed_at`, `version`. At most one not-DONE row per admission (partial unique index). This story only inserts rows and updates score, risk and target; the later columns are used by the Safety board story. Never deleted.
  - `ipd.clinical_event`: `id`, `admission_id` (null allowed), `kind` check in give/order/verify/esc/view/bg/deny/stop/info, `text` (<= 300), `actor` (membership id), `at`. Append-only, never edited. Index `(tenant_id, at desc)` and `(tenant_id, admission_id, at desc)`. Text may name a medicine but never a patient name or a clinical note.
- The score is computed in one small domain class (`EarlyWarningScore`) used by the save and, later, by the card; the parameter bands are in code with the mockup values, not in the database.
- Roles are checked on the server; the card/ACL configuration ("Roles and access") is not built, so access is fixed as in AC 1 and AC 3.
- Contract changes: `AdmissionDetail` gains `vitalsDue` and `latestEws`; new `NursingSheet` and `VitalsInput`.

## 4. API (`/api/v1/ipd`, contract `docs/api/ipd.yaml`)
| Method | Path | Role | Request | Response | Errors |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GET | `/admissions/{id}/nursing` | OWNER, NURSE, DOCTOR | none | `NursingSheet` (latest vitals and score, vitalsDue, last 3 notes, canRecord) | 403, 404 |
| POST | `/admissions/{id}/vitals` | OWNER, NURSE | `{bp, pulse, spo2, temp, rr, note?}` | `NursingSheet` + `escalation` (RAISED, UPDATED or NONE) | 400, 403, 404, 409 (`not-admitted`, `ipd-readonly`) |

## 5. Tasks
### Tasks — Backend
Migration (db-engineer); `EarlyWarningScore` with a unit test for every band edge (91/92, 93/94, 95/96; RR 8/9, 11/12, 20/21, 24/25; pulse 40/41, 50/51, 90/91, 110/111, 130/131; temp 35.0/35.1, 36.0/36.1, 38.0/38.1, 39.0/39.1; systolic 90/91, 100/101, 110/111, 219/220; the "any 3 = Medium" rule; 7 = High); vitals service with escalation raise and update; clinical event writes; audit; unit tests. Real-DB tests by qa-engineer.
### Tasks — Frontend
Nursing button and Nursing sheet (score notice, five fields, note, recent notes, view-only state, save, toasts); unit tests.

## 6. Out of scope, Open questions
Out of scope: Acknowledge / Record response / Call senior and the Safety tab, "Vitals due" and score flags on a patient card, colour bands of single vitals, vitals history or trend chart, assigned nurse per ward, the MAR tab (SAH-32), alert messages to doctors, correcting a wrong entry, vitals in OPD, device integration, consciousness and oxygen scoring (not in the mockup score), paediatric or maternity score variants.
Open questions (also in `docs/stories/IPD-open-questions.md`):
- OQ-30-1: entry point. Until the patient card exists, Nursing is a button on the admission sheet. Confirm, or build the card first.
- OQ-30-2 (patient safety, high priority): a raised escalation has no way to be seen or answered until the Safety board story. Proposed: schedule the Safety board directly after this story, and do not use SAH-30 in a live ward without it.
- OQ-30-3 (roles): vitals can be recorded by NURSE and OWNER, viewed by DOCTOR; VISITING_DOCTOR, FRONT_DESK and BILLING have no access. Confirm, and whether a nurse sees all patients or only the nurse's ward (ward assignment does not exist).
- OQ-30-4 (clinical): allowed ranges for each vital (to refuse typing mistakes such as 370 for 37.0) and the note length. Proposed: refuse only impossible values (SpO₂ 1 to 100, temperature 25.0 to 45.0, pulse and RR 1 to 300, systolic 40 to 300, diastolic 20 to 200), for the doctor to confirm.
- OQ-30-5: how is a wrong vitals entry corrected (mark "entered in error" with a reason)? Not in the mockup. Proposed: enter a new set; no correction feature.
- OQ-30-6 (clinical, blocking for go-live): sign-off of the scoring bands and risk rule of AC 6, of the response times (High 15 minutes, Medium 1 hour) and of the monitoring advice (every 30 minutes, within 1 hour, every 4 hours) by a doctor. They are taken from the mockup, not from a published scale.
- OQ-30-7 (privacy): should opening the Nursing sheet be written to the patient record-access log (the mockup logs EMR opening, not nursing)?
- OQ-30-8: the fields are prefilled with the last values (as the mockup). Copying forward old vitals can be saved by mistake as new readings; should the fields start empty, with the last values shown beside them?
