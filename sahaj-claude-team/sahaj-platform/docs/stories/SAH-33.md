# SAH-33 · Safety board: early warning escalations (Acknowledge, Record response, Call senior) and the clinical event log (IPD, part 12)
Mockup screen: IPD → Safety (the "Safety" tab of Beds and admissions) — the red notice, the tiles, the panel "Early warning escalations" and its three actions, the sheet "Response", the panel "Clinical audit trail" (`sfTabOn`, `sfCount`, `ipSafety`, `escRow`, `sfack`, `sfresp`, `sfrespok`, `sfup`, `sfLateEsc`, `sfMins`, `sfLog`, `kic`), and the red banner with the button "Open safety board" on the Admitted patients tab (the `ipList` wrap, `sfban`)
Module: ipd

## 1. Story
As a doctor, I want to see every early warning escalation of the ward at once, acknowledge it when I take it on and record what I did when I close it; as a nurse, I want to see which of my patients' escalations a doctor has not yet seen and to call the senior doctor when the target time has passed; and as the owner, I want an unalterable log of what was done on the ward, so that a patient who is getting worse is never left unseen and every response can be shown afterwards.

Scope note: this is the Safety board of the mockup, cut to what exists in phase 1. SAH-30 raises and updates the escalation and writes the event log; until this story is built no screen lets a doctor act on an escalation (OQ-30-2). **SAH-30 must not be used on a live ward before this story is built** (and before the clinical sign-off of the score and response times, OQ-30-6); the human records this go-live condition (OQ-33-12).

The Safety tab of the mockup holds six things. This story builds three: the escalation panel with its tile, the red notice and the banner, and the clinical audit trail. The Pharmacist check panel and its tile are SAH-31. The Medicine round panel, the tile "Doses due or overdue" and the dose part of the tab count need the MAR (SAH-32); they are added to this tab when SAH-32 is built (OQ-33-15). **Emergency (break-glass) access is not in this story: it is split out as SAH-34**, for these reasons: (1) emergency access only means something when a nurse can see only her own ward, and ward assignment and the card's "Roles and access" scope do not exist in phase 1 (OQ-30-3: a nurse sees every admitted patient), so there is nothing to break through yet; (2) it adds a time-limited access grant that changes what every patient list and read returns for one user, a platform-level security behaviour that needs its own design, tests and security review; (3) it brings its own button, sheet, tile, review panel and card flag. This story only shows `bg` entries in the log if they ever exist. Alerts to the doctor or the senior doctor need the notifications service (not in phase 1): nothing is sent, and no text says it was (see AC 11 and OQ-33-3). The patient card flags "Not seen · N min" and "Doctor reviewing" belong to the card story; the API returns what the card needs.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given IPD is Trial, On or Read-only and at least one patient is Admitted (as the mockup), then **Safety** is the last tab of Beds and admissions (after Admitted patients, and after Emergency when that tab is shown) for OWNER, DOCTOR and NURSE, with the number of escalations not yet closed next to the name (hidden at 0), and the tab opens the Safety board. For FRONT_DESK, BILLING, VISITING_DOCTOR and PHARMACIST there is no tab and every endpoint of this story answers 403 (PHARMACIST sees the tab for the Pharmacist check panel only, SAH-31; OQ-33-1, OQ-33-15). IPD Off answers 403 `module-off`. The data of another clinic is never shown (RLS); an escalation id of another clinic answers 404.
2. Given the Safety board, then from top to bottom it shows: the red notice (AC 3) when something is past its target; the tiles (AC 4); the panel **Early warning escalations** (AC 5 to 8); the panels of SAH-31/SAH-32 when those exist, in the mockup order (Pharmacist check, Medicine round); the panel **Clinical audit trail** (AC 16). Emergency access to review (SAH-34) is not shown.
3. Given one or more escalations are past the response target (AC 7), then the red notice shows **"N escalation(s) past the response target."** followed by "<patient name> (EWS N) raised <ago>" for each, separated by semicolons, and ends with " Acknowledge, then record what you did." for OWNER and DOCTOR or " Call the senior doctor now." for NURSE. "ago" is written as in SAH-30 AC1. With none past target the notice is not shown.
4. Given the tiles, then **Escalations open** shows the number of escalations not yet closed (Open or Acknowledged) with the small text "N past target" in red when some are past target (and the number in red) or "All within target"; for NURSE a second tile **Your patients** shows the number of admitted patients the nurse can see with the small text "In your wards" (OQ-33-2: until wards are assigned this is every admitted patient, so the small text cannot be true and is proposed as "All admitted patients"). The tab number, the tile and the number of rows in the panel that are not closed are always the same figure, all counted by the server. The tiles of SAH-31 (Pharmacist checks), SAH-32 (Doses due or overdue) and SAH-34 (Emergency access) are added by those stories.
5. Given the panel **Early warning escalations** (subtitle: "High score: doctor within 15 min. Medium: within 1 hour. A nurse can call the senior doctor if the target passes."), then each escalation is one row with: the score and the word High or Medium in a red (High) or amber (Medium) badge; "<patient name> · bed <code>"; the small line "Raised by <nurse name> <ago> · target 15 min" (or "1 hour") followed by " · escalated to senior N×" when the senior doctor was called; a status pill; and the action buttons of AC 8 to 11. Rows not closed come first, then the higher score first, and (proposed tie-break, not in the mockup) the older first; closed rows follow. Empty: "No escalations. Scores of 5 and above raise one automatically when vitals are saved."
6. Given the status pill, then it reads **Not seen** (red) for an Open escalation, **Overdue · not seen** (red, the row is highlighted) for an Open escalation past its target, **Doctor reviewing** (amber) for an Acknowledged one, and **Closed** (green) for a closed one. An Acknowledged escalation shows the small line "Acknowledged by <doctor name> · response not recorded yet"; a closed one shows "<what was done> · <plan and next vitals> · <doctor name>" with a check icon. The response plan is clinical text: it is shown to OWNER, DOCTOR and NURSE only, never in a notice, a toast or a log line.
7. Given the response target, then an escalation is **past target** when it is Open (not Acknowledged) and the time since it was raised, on the server clock, is more than its target in minutes (15 for High, 60 for Medium, as stored by SAH-30). The minutes shown ("<ago>") and the overdue state are computed by the server from the database clock and sent with the answer (`asOf`); the browser clock is never used. Acknowledging stops the overdue state (as the mockup). Closing removes the escalation from the counts, the notice and the banner.
8. Given an Open escalation and the role OWNER or DOCTOR, then the row has the primary button **Acknowledge**; on tap the server sets the status to Acknowledged with the doctor and the time, writes the event "Acknowledged escalation after N min" (kind esc; N = whole minutes from raised to acknowledged) and the toast is "Acknowledged. Record what you did for <patient name> after you see the patient." Given NURSE, FRONT_DESK and every other role, then there is no button and the API answers 403 `forbidden` (the mockup: "Only a doctor can do this."). An Acknowledged or closed escalation cannot be acknowledged again (AC 15).
9. Given a not closed escalation and OWNER or DOCTOR, then the row has **Record response** (secondary for Open, primary for Acknowledged), which opens the sheet **"Response · <patient name>"** with the subtitle "EWS N (high|medium) · raised <ago> by <nurse name>", a select **What was done** — "Reviewed at the bedside", "Treatment changed", "Moved to ICU or HDU", "Rapid response team called", "Reviewed, no change needed" (first one preselected) — a text area **Plan and next vitals** (3 rows, placeholder "For example: oxygen increased to 6 L, repeat vitals in 30 minutes"), and the footer **Cancel** and **Close escalation** (check icon).
10. Given **Close escalation**, then an empty plan (after trimming) is refused with the message "Write the plan and when vitals are next due." (server 400, nothing saved; the sheet stays open with the typed text); an action not in the list is refused (400); the plan is up to 1,000 characters with the same text rules as the nursing note of SAH-30 (OQ-33-14). A valid answer sets the status to Closed with the action, the plan, the closing doctor and time; when the escalation was still Open, the closing doctor is also stored as the acknowledging doctor at that time (as the mockup, without an "Acknowledged" event); the event "Escalation closed: <what was done>" (kind esc) is written; the toast is "Escalation for <patient name> closed and recorded"; the sheet closes and the board reloads. The event holds the action only, not the plan (deviation from the mockup, which puts the plan in the log; OQ-33-7). A closed escalation is never edited, re-opened by hand or deleted; a later problem creates a new escalation through SAH-30.
11. Given an Open escalation and the role NURSE (OQ-33-1), then the row has **Call senior** (siren icon; primary when the escalation is past target, secondary otherwise); on tap the server adds one to the number of senior calls, writes the event "Called the senior doctor: escalation not seen after N min" (kind esc) and the row shows " · escalated to senior N×". The mockup toast says "Senior doctor and rapid response called for <patient name>. This call is logged." Phase 1 sends no call, so that sentence would be false: the toast is proposed as **"Call recorded for <patient name>. No alert is sent: phone the senior doctor now."** (deviation from the mockup, same reason as SAH-30 toast; OQ-33-3). Each tap is one call (as the mockup); the button is disabled while the request runs. The button is not shown for an Acknowledged or closed escalation, nor for OWNER and DOCTOR; the API answers 403 for the other roles and 409 `escalation-state` for a state that is not Open.
12. Given an Acknowledged escalation and a new vitals save (SAH-30) whose risk is **higher** than the escalation's (Medium to High; OQ-33-4, clinical), then in the same transaction the escalation goes back to **Open**: score, risk and target (15 min) are replaced, the acknowledgement is cleared, the response clock restarts at that moment (the original raise time stays on the record), and the event "Escalation re-opened · EWS now N (high) · doctor review within 15 min" (kind esc) is written; the earlier acknowledgement stays in the event log. The board shows it as Not seen again with the Acknowledge button, and the SAH-30 toast is unchanged. When the score rises inside the same risk (for example 5 to 6) the score is updated as in SAH-30 AC8, the status stays Acknowledged and the event "Escalation updated · EWS now N (medium)" is written. A score equal or lower, and any Low score, changes nothing: only a doctor closes an escalation (a later Low score never closes it, as the mockup).
13. Given a doctor who opened the Response sheet or the board before such a change, then Acknowledge, Record response and Call senior carry the version of the escalation they looked at; if the escalation changed meanwhile (re-opened, score raised, acknowledged or closed by someone else) the server answers 409 `escalation-changed` (or `escalation-state`) with the current state, nothing is written, the board reloads, and a notice says what happened, for example "The score is now EWS 8 (high). Review it and try again." or "<doctor name> already acknowledged this escalation." or "This escalation is already closed." A response is therefore never recorded against a score the doctor did not see.
14. Given an escalation that is not closed when the patient's discharge is completed (L4 of the SAH-30 security review; OQ-33-5, patient safety), then, proposed: the discharge is refused with 409 `escalation-open` and "This patient has an early warning escalation that is not closed. A doctor must record the response first.", and the discharge sheet (SAH-27) shows this as a warning before the button is pressed; the check is made in the discharge transaction under the admission row lock, so it cannot be bypassed by a race with a new vitals save. The alternative (the discharge closes it automatically as "Closed at discharge") is in OQ-33-5. A discharged patient leaves the board with his escalations (the board lists admitted patients only, as the mockup).
15. Given concurrency, then Acknowledge, Record response, Call senior, the re-opening of AC 12, the discharge of AC 14 and the SAH-30 save all take the admission row lock first (`for no key update`, as SAH-30 / ADR-006 D4) and read the escalation after it, in READ COMMITTED; two doctors acknowledging at the same time give one success and one 409 `escalation-state` ("<doctor name> already acknowledged this escalation."); one open escalation per admission stays guaranteed by the partial unique index; a failed action leaves nothing half written (rollback). The role of the caller is read again from the membership inside the transaction (SAH-30 security review M1), so a role change applies at once.
16. Given the panel **Clinical audit trail** (subtitle for OWNER and DOCTOR: "Every order, check, dose, escalation and record opened. Entries cannot be edited or deleted."; for NURSE: "Your actions and your patients. Entries cannot be edited or deleted."), then it lists the latest 14 entries of the clinical event log, newest first (OQ-33-8), each with a kind icon (give: pill, order: EMR, verify: shield, esc: siren, view: eye, bg: key, deny: alert, stop: cross, info: history), the text in bold, the small line "<user name>" followed by " · <patient name> · bed <code>" when the entry belongs to an admission, and the time ("11:05 AM" today, "24 Oct, 11:05 AM" earlier). OWNER and DOCTOR see all entries; NURSE sees her own entries and the entries of admitted patients (every admitted patient until wards are assigned; OQ-33-2). Empty: "Nothing recorded yet." The entries written so far are those of SAH-30 (vitals, escalation raised/updated) and of this story; the entries of SAH-31/32/34 appear when those stories write them. There is no way to edit or delete an entry, in the screen or in the API.
17. Given privacy of the log, then the text of an entry is built only from fixed wording, numbers and, for later stories, medicine names; it never holds a patient name, mobile number, ABHA number or note text (the plan of AC 10 included). The patient name and bed on the screen are read from the admission when the list is built, shown only to OWNER, DOCTOR and NURSE, and never written to the entry or to an application log (OQ-33-8). The user's name is read from the user at display time ("Team member" if unknown).
18. Given IPD Read-only (subscription lapsed), then the board, the banner and the log can be read; Acknowledge, Record response and Call senior stay allowed, proposed, because they record care that the ward owes a patient already admitted and must not be blocked by a billing state (OQ-33-9). Given a discharged patient or an unknown escalation, then the answer is 404.
19. Given the banner of the mockup on the Admitted patients tab, then for OWNER, DOCTOR and NURSE (roles with vitals access; not FRONT_DESK or BILLING) the tab shows at the top a red notice "<patient name> · EWS N, <patient name> · EWS N escalated and not seen by a doctor within target." with the button **Open safety board**, which switches to the Safety tab; with nothing past target there is no banner.
20. Given a slow or failed network, then each action button is disabled while its request runs; a failed Acknowledge, Close escalation or Call senior shows the error, keeps the Response sheet and the typed plan, and the row is never shown as changed until the server has answered; there is no offline queue in this phase. The board is reloaded every 60 seconds and after every action so that "<ago>", the overdue state, the counts and the notice follow the server clock (the interval is the same as the Emergency queue; OQ-33-10); while it is loading it shows "Loading" and a failed refresh keeps the last data with a notice "Could not refresh. Showing data from <time>."
21. Given privacy and audit, then every Acknowledge, Close escalation, Call senior and re-opening writes an audit event (IPD_ESCALATION_ACKNOWLEDGED, IPD_ESCALATION_CLOSED, IPD_ESCALATION_SENIOR_CALLED, IPD_ESCALATION_REOPENED) with the escalation id, the admission id, the score, the risk and, for the close, the action code; never the plan, a name or a vital value. The board holds names and clinical text: it is never logged. Whether reading the board writes the patient record-access log is OQ-33-8 (proposed: no row per patient per refresh; the actions above are the trail).
22. Given SAH-30 after this story, then its escalation toast "Escalation raised for <patient name> · EWS N · doctor review within 15 min. Tell the doctor now: no alert is sent." is unchanged, because the board only helps when someone looks at it and phase 1 sends no alert (OQ-33-11). Its API outcome gains REOPENED for AC 12.

## 3. Technical design
- Data (db-engineer; the tables exist from SAH-30, V2026_11_23 and V2026_11_25): `ipd.ews_escalation` and `ipd.clinical_event` are used as built. Needed in a new forward-only migration: widen the column grant of `ipd.ews_escalation` to `status`, `acknowledged_by`, `acknowledged_at`, `response_action`, `response_note`, `senior_calls`, `closed_at`; add `closed_by` and a time for the response clock restart (for example `reopened_at`; the overdue time is measured from the later of `raised_at` and `reopened_at`); checks for `response_action` (the five values) and `response_note` (1 to 1,000 characters, present exactly when DONE); and extend the update guard into a state machine: OPEN to ACK, OPEN to DONE, ACK to OPEN (only with a higher risk), ACK to DONE; DONE final; the acknowledgement fields set once per Open period; `senior_calls` and `version` only increase; the audit rows stay append-only. Nothing is deleted.
- One lock rule for every escalation write (ADR-006 D4 "Rule for the Safety board story", code review M2): lock the bare `ipd.admission` row `for no key update`, then read the joined data; the escalation is read after the lock. `escalation-changed` uses `version` as an optimistic check on top of the lock (AC 13).
- Overdue, "<ago>" and the counts come from the database clock (`clock_timestamp()`), one `asOf` per answer; the rule "past target" lives in one small domain class used by the board, the summary and later the card.
- The tab count and the banner come from a small summary read in the owning module (ADR-005 D3), not from loading the whole board. The summary carries no clinical text; the banner needs only names and scores of the escalations past target.
- Discharge change (SAH-27 `DischargeService`): refuse completion while an escalation is not closed (AC 14), under the admission lock it already takes.
- The clinical event log is only read here (kinds and icons as the mockup); its texts for this story are fixed templates in one place, as in SAH-30 (D7).
- Roles are checked twice (token, then membership inside the transaction) with exact role sets; the card/ACL configuration is not built, so access is fixed as in AC 1, 8, 11 and 16.
- Contract changes: new `SafetyBoard`, `SafetySummary`, `EscalationRow`, `EscalationAction`, `ClinicalEventPage`; `VitalsSaved.escalation.outcome` gains REOPENED; `DischargeResult` error `escalation-open`; `AdmissionDetail` may carry the open escalation status and the overdue flag for the card story.

## 4. API (`/api/v1/ipd`, contract `docs/api/ipd.yaml`; paths are proposals for the architect)
| Method | Path | Role | Request | Response | Errors |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GET | `/safety` | OWNER, DOCTOR, NURSE | none | `SafetyBoard` (tiles, past-target list, escalation rows with status, overdue, ago, senior calls, what each role can do, `asOf`) | 403 |
| GET | `/safety/summary` | OWNER, DOCTOR, NURSE | none | `SafetySummary` (open count, past-target count, past-target names and scores for the banner, `asOf`) | 403 |

| GET | `/clinical-events` | OWNER, DOCTOR, NURSE | `limit` (default 14), cursor | `ClinicalEventPage` (kind, text, user name, patient name and bed, time) | 403 |

## 5. Tasks
### Tasks — Backend
Migration (db-engineer); escalation state machine in one domain class with unit tests for every transition and refusal (OPEN to ACK, OPEN to DONE, ACK to DONE, ACK to OPEN only on a higher risk, DONE final, double acknowledge, stale version); "past target" rule with tests at 15:00 / 15:01 and 60:00 / 60:01 and for Acknowledged (never past); board, summary, acknowledge, respond, call-senior, event list; one admission lock for every write and for the SAH-30 re-open and the discharge check; clinical event writes with fixed templates; audit; role re-read; Read-only rule; unit tests. Real-DB and concurrency tests (two acknowledges, acknowledge against re-open, close against discharge, call senior against close) by qa-engineer.
### Tasks — Frontend
Safety tab with count; red notice; tiles; escalation panel with rows, pills, the three buttons by role, the Response sheet and its validation; audit trail panel; banner with "Open safety board" on the Admitted patients tab; 60-second refresh and stale-data notice; toasts; handling of 409 `escalation-changed` and `escalation-state`; discharge sheet warning (AC 14); unit tests.

## 6. Out of scope, Open questions
Out of scope: **emergency (break-glass) access: the "Emergency access" button and sheet, the 30-minute grant, the tile and the panel "Emergency access to review", "Mark reviewed", and the card flag (SAH-34, to be written after the patient card and Roles and access stories)**; the Pharmacist check panel and tile (SAH-31); the Medicine round panel, the tile "Doses due or overdue" and missed-dose handling (SAH-32 / OQ-32-2, OQ-33-15); card flags and the red card ("Not seen · N min", "Doctor reviewing"); any message, SMS, WhatsApp, sound or call to the doctor or the senior doctor (notifications are Later); a senior doctor roster or phone numbers; response times or score bands set by the clinic; a response-time report or analytics; search, filters, date range, export or printing of the log; a wrong response corrected or a closed escalation re-opened by hand; ward-wise or own-patient scoping; locked safety fields in card setup.
Open questions (to be added to `docs/decisions/IPD-open-questions.md` by the tech lead):
- OQ-33-1 (roles, high priority): the mockup lets only the Owner, who is the doctor of the demo, acknowledge and respond ("Only a doctor can do this. Switch to Dr. Meera (Owner)"), and shows the Safety tab to the owner and the nurse. Proposed: Acknowledge and Record response for OWNER and DOCTOR; Call senior for NURSE only; the tab for OWNER, DOCTOR, NURSE (and PHARMACIST for its own panel). Confirm, and decide (a) whether an OWNER who is not a doctor may acknowledge and close, (b) VISITING_DOCTOR (the card matrix gives the visiting doctor vitals view and own patients only; no own-patient scoping exists), (c) whether DOCTOR or OWNER may also call the senior.
- OQ-33-2 (scope): the mockup shows a nurse only the escalations, tiles and log entries of her own wards, and the tile "Your patients — In your wards". Ward assignment does not exist (OQ-30-3), so the nurse sees every admitted patient. Confirm, and the wording of the tile until wards exist.
- OQ-33-3 (patient safety, high priority): "Call senior" has no senior doctor in the mockup (no name, no roster, no phone) and phase 1 cannot call anyone. Proposed: it records that the nurse escalated, the toast says no alert is sent and to phone the senior doctor, and the red notice already says "Call the senior doctor now". Who is "the senior doctor" and how is he reached on a ward (a number shown on the board)? Confirm the toast wording (deviation from the mockup).
- OQ-33-4 (clinical, patient safety): re-opening an Acknowledged escalation when the risk rises (security review L3). The mockup keeps it Acknowledged. Proposed: re-open on a rise from Medium to High, restart the response clock from that moment, keep the original raise time; a rise inside the same risk only updates the score. A doctor to decide: any rise in score instead of a risk rise? Which clock (restart, or the original raise time, which would make it overdue at once)?
- OQ-33-5 (patient safety, high priority): an escalation not closed at discharge (security review L4, ADR-006 Q9). The mockup says nothing. Proposed: completing the discharge is refused until a doctor has recorded a response. Alternative: the discharge closes it automatically as "Closed at discharge" with a warning. Also decide for Death, LAMA, Referred and Transfer outcomes, and who closes it when the discharging user is FRONT_DESK. The discharge sheet warning is new text.
- OQ-33-6 (clinical): an Acknowledged escalation has no target for the response in the mockup and no overdue state; it can stay "Doctor reviewing" for hours with no one noticing. Proposed: show how long ago it was acknowledged; no new rule until a doctor sets a target.
- OQ-33-7 (privacy, deviation): the mockup writes the plan into the log ("Escalation closed: Reviewed at the bedside · Chest X-ray repeat ordered"). The log must hold no note text (ADR-006 D7, CLAUDE.md). Proposed: the log holds the action only; the plan is stored with the escalation and shown on the board row to clinical roles. Confirm. Also: the closed row shows who acknowledged (mockup); when another doctor closes it, should it show who closed it? Proposed: the closer, with the acknowledger in the log.
- OQ-33-8 (log view, privacy): the mockup shows patient name and bed in each log row and only the latest 14 entries, with no search, date or paging; a ward writes hundreds of entries a day, so 14 is not an audit trail. Proposed: build 14 as the mockup, add "Show earlier entries" loading 50 more (not in the mockup), no filters yet; names read at display time, never stored in the entry. Confirm or ask for a log without names (bed and UHID only). Should reading the board and the log write the patient record-access log (one row per patient per refresh would flood it; proposed: no, the actions are the trail)? Who may see the whole log (OWNER and DOCTOR proposed)?
- OQ-33-9: in Read-only IPD the mockup has no rule. Proposed: the board can be read and Acknowledge, Record response and Call senior still work (SAH-30 saving vitals does not). Confirm.
- OQ-33-10: refresh. The mockup has a demo clock and no refresh. Proposed: reload every 60 seconds (as the Emergency queue) while the tab is open; the tab count and banner come from the summary read the page already makes. Is 60 seconds acceptable for a High escalation with a 15-minute target?
- OQ-33-11: wording of the SAH-30 toast once the board exists. Proposed: keep "Tell the doctor now: no alert is sent" until notifications exist.
- OQ-33-12 (go-live): record as a condition that SAH-30 is not switched on for a live ward before SAH-33 is built and before OQ-30-6 is signed off; who records it and in which document (DEC-013 item 13 says so; there is no technical gate).
- OQ-33-13: closed escalations stay on the board for as long as the patient is admitted (the mockup); a long stay fills the panel. Proposed: all not closed ones, and the closed ones of the last 24 hours; older ones are in the log. Confirm.
- OQ-33-14: limits of the plan text: 1,000 characters and the SAH-30 text rules (line breaks allowed) are proposed; the mockup has none.
- OQ-33-15 (sequence): the Safety tab is also the home of SAH-31 (Pharmacist check, shown to OWNER and PHARMACIST) and SAH-32 (Medicine round). Proposed: the story built first creates the tab, the tab is shown to every role that can see at least one panel, and each story adds its own panel, tile and count; should the Medicine round panel and the dose tile be part of SAH-32 or of a small follow-up to this story?
- OQ-33-16 (retention): escalations and the clinical event log are part of the in-patient record and are never deleted in phase 1; the period is ADR-006 Q10.
