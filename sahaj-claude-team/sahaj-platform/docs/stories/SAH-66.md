# SAH-66 · Stories: Medical Oncology chemotherapy order sheet, part 1 (protocol, dose levels and dose maths, day-care sequence, safety checks, second check and send)
Requirement: `docs/requirements/SAH-45.md` (rules BR45-4, BR45-5, BR45-6, BR45-20, BR45-23; clinical rule C-8; legal rule L-3 consent; billing rule B-3; decided sign-offs SO-45-1, SO-45-32; answered questions OQ-45-23; answered, default accepted (DEC-039 addendum): OQ-45-27, OQ-45-28, OQ-45-30; **OQ-45-33 answered by the human, a decided difference from the mockup (SO-45-42): the second checker confirms with their own sign-in or the colleague PIN of SAH-38 before the order can be sent**; carried over from SAH-43: OQ-43-24; the PIN mechanism is SAH-38 / SAH-32 / ADR-010 / ADR-014). Module: opd (pharmacy hand-off later; scheme through billing). Phase 4 (BRD out of scope, in scope by DEC-039 answer 1). Depends on SAH-65 (decision, labs, BSA), SAH-48 (Prescription, allergy), SAH-58 (Chemotherapy course), SAH-51 (finish). Department: Medical Oncology (starter department, 27 sections). **Oversized story split (12 Nov 2026): take-home medicines, next cycle and patient calendar, referrals, cycle note and Complete moved to SAH-70. SAH-66 keeps the order sheet A to D and the second check.**
Mockup screen: Queue (sidebar → Queue) (EMR mockup #OncologyTreatment, "Medical Oncology · 2 Treatment (order sheet)").
Roles in every story: owner, doctor, visiting doctor, nurse, front desk, billing, pharmacist (the platform also has counsellor). Only doctor, visiting doctor and owner with doctor details (`clinic.consultation.write`) write the sheet, on their own patients (owner: all). Nurse, front desk, billing, pharmacist and counsellor get 403 on a direct call to write the sheet (nurse and pharmacist do not open the EMR). The one exception is the **second-check confirmation** (SAH-66-2): a nurse, pharmacist or doctor named as the checker may confirm that one order, and only that action, with their own sign-in or their colleague PIN (OQ-45-33, decided). "Not signed in" gets 401. The clinic always comes from the logged-in user, never from the request body.
**Patient-safety critical.** Dose maths, limits and the second check are built as drawn and every number carries the rule tag "value from the EMR mockup, accepted by the human in DEC-039, not clinically reviewed" (BR45-4). The tech lead advised against building these unreviewed; the tag stays on each number in the screen help text, the code documents and the tests. The screen prepares the order; it does not dispense or give anything.
**Human-decided difference from the mockup (SO-45-42, DEC-039 addendum):** the mockup lets the prescriber pick the second checker by name only. The human decided that the named checker must confirm with their own sign-in or the colleague PIN mechanism of SAH-38 before the order can be sent to pharmacy. Everything else about the second check is as drawn.

### SAH-66-1 · As an oncologist, I want an order sheet that fills itself from today's decision, with the protocol, dose levels and doses worked out per square metre, so that the doses are right and visible
Priority: Must
Screen: EMR mockup #OncologyTreatment, "No decision yet…", "A · Protocol", "B · Doses" (dose level, Calculated, Rounded, Final dose, Lifetime dose).
## Acceptance criteria
- Given no decision is recorded (SAH-65-4), then the sheet shows "No decision yet. Finish the assessment and confirm today's decision." with the button "Go to decision →" that opens the Assessment part at the decision card. (BR45-20)
- Given a decision, then "A · Protocol" shows the protocol from "the hospital's approved protocol library · doses are per m²": the library of the mockup holds AC (Doxorubicin + cyclophosphamide · every 21 days), Paclitaxel 175 mg/m² · every 21 days, Paclitaxel weekly 80 mg/m² · every 7 days and TC (Docetaxel + cyclophosphamide · every 21 days); each shows a tag "In plan", "Suggested" (when the decision is Change regimen and it is the suggested next protocol) or "New consent" (when the patient has no consent for it). Rule tag: value from the EMR mockup, accepted by the human in DEC-039, not clinically reviewed (C-8). (BR45-4)
- Given the library is the clinic's own approved list, then the owner maintains it as data (the architect decides the maintenance screen, flagged for the human); the four protocols are the seed of a demonstration clinic; the doses per m² are: AC doxorubicin 60 mg/m² and cyclophosphamide 600 mg/m²; paclitaxel 3-weekly 175 mg/m²; paclitaxel weekly 80 mg/m²; TC docetaxel 75 mg/m² and cyclophosphamide 600 mg/m². Rule tag as above (C-8). (BR45-4)
- Given "B · Doses", then the dose level offers 100%, 90%, 80% and 75% (100% at start); a level below 100% requires "Reason for reduction (required)" chosen or typed before the sheet can be sent; the decision "Give, reduce dose" sets the next level down. Rule tag as above (C-8). (BR45-4)
- Given each drug, then the table shows Drug, Dose per m², Calculated, Rounded, Final dose and Lifetime dose; Calculated = dose per m² × body surface area used (SAH-65-2) × level ÷ 100; Rounded = Calculated rounded to the drug's vial step (the mockup: doxorubicin 5 mg, cyclophosphamide 20 mg, paclitaxel 10 mg, docetaxel 10 mg); Final dose = the Rounded value (the doctor can edit it, and an edited value outside 5% of Calculated is shown in red with the words "More than 5% from calculated"; the red is an analyst addition because the mockup shows only the note). Rule tag as above (C-8). (BR45-4)
- Given the note under the table, then it reads "Rounded to the nearest vial size (within 5% of calculated). Doses recalculate if weight changes by 10% or more." and, when the weight differs from cycle 1 by 10% or more, the sheet shows "Weight changed by x%. Doses recalculated" and recomputes the doses from today's weight. Rule tag as above (C-8). (BR45-4)
- Given the lifetime dose for doxorubicin, then it reads "<n> of 450 mg/m² lifetime" with a bar, where n = the earlier cumulative dose (from earlier signed cycles of this patient, plus doses entered by the doctor from outside the clinic as "Earlier doses before this clinic") plus this cycle's dose per m² × level ÷ 100; the number is red above 400, and the check "Cumulative dose within limit" passes only when the total is 450 mg/m² or less. Other drugs show "No lifetime limit". Rule tag as above (C-8). (BR45-4)
- Given the BSA used, then the sheet shows it with its source ("BSA from today's weight") and the cap if applied (SAH-65-2); a change of weight or height after the sheet was sent shows "Facts changed after sending" and the earlier sent doses stay in the record. (BR45-4)
- Given the sheet has been sent (SAH-66-2), then doses and level are read-only; a correction is an addendum with a reason (SAH-51-5) and a new send. (BR45-18)
- Given a nurse, front desk, billing, pharmacist or counsellor, 403; a doctor who is not the visit's doctor (not owner), 403; another clinic's visit, 404; no login, 401. (BR45-16, BR45-5)
- Given any call, then no dose, drug or protocol is written to a log line or analytics event together with a patient; the audit holds ids and keys. (BR45-5)
## Notes for design
Dose maths runs on the server so that the same figures show on every client and in tests; each constant carries the tag in the code documents and test names. Hospital protocol library maintenance is a data question for the architect (a global seed plus clinic rows, or clinic rows only).

### SAH-66-2 · As an oncologist, I want the day-care sequence and a list of safety checks that must pass, then an independent second check, confirmed by the checker themselves, before I send the order, so that a wrong order cannot be sent
Priority: Must
Screen: EMR mockup #OncologyTreatment, "C · Day-care sequence", "D · Safety checks before sending", "Independent second check by (not the prescriber)", "Sign and send to pharmacy", stages.
## Acceptance criteria
- Given "C · Day-care sequence", then the pre-medicines of the protocol show first (AC: Ondansetron 8 mg IV, Dexamethasone 12 mg IV, Aprepitant 125 mg oral; paclitaxel: Dexamethasone 20 mg IV, Pheniramine 45.5 mg IV, Famotidine 20 mg IV; weekly paclitaxel: Dexamethasone 8 mg IV, Pheniramine 45.5 mg IV, Famotidine 20 mg IV; TC: Dexamethasone 8 mg oral (day before), Ondansetron 8 mg IV), then each chemotherapy drug with its infusion minutes (doxorubicin 15, cyclophosphamide 30, paclitaxel 180 or 60, docetaxel 60) and its note (doxorubicin: "Vesicant: give by port, check blood return"; paclitaxel: "Watch first 15 min for reaction · in-line filter"; docetaxel: "Watch for reaction · needs G-CSF"); the emetic risk of the protocol shows (High, Moderate, Low). Rule tag: value from the EMR mockup, accepted by the human in DEC-039, not clinically reviewed (C-8). (BR45-4)
- Given a pre-medicine, then the doctor can untick it for this cycle; the sequence prints on the cycle record (SAH-70-2). (BR45-6)
- Given "D · Safety checks before sending", then each check shows a tick or a cross, a short text and, where it can be fixed, a button: "Decision recorded" (a Give decision exists; button "Go"), "Fit to treat today" (no count or organ lab fails and no fever: "Counts, organ tests OK · no fever" or "Check labs / temperature"), "Labs within 72 hours" (the labs' drawn time is within 72 hours of now), "BSA from today's weight" (shows the m², and "(capped at 2.0)" when capped), "Cumulative dose within limit" ("Doxorubicin under 450 mg/m²" or "Over lifetime limit"), "Dose-reduction reason" ("Full dose · not needed", the reason, or "Pick a reason above"), "Allergies reviewed" ("No known drug allergy · confirmed" after the doctor confirms with the patient, buttons "Confirm" and "Undo"), "Consent for this regimen" (signed earlier for this regimen, or "New consent signed today" after "Mark signed", or "New regimen: take fresh consent") and "Scheme / TPA approval" ("MJPJAY package approved", "Fresh pre-auth raised" after "Raise pre-auth", or "Not in approved package"). Rule tag as above (C-8, L-3, B-3). (BR45-4)
- Given the line is not ready for a vesicant (SAH-65-2: "Port: no blood return yet"), then the sheet shows the reason and the check "Line ready for vesicant" is a cross for a drug marked as a vesicant. Rule tag as above (C-8). (BR45-4)
- Given the allergy confirmation, then "No known drug allergy" is written to the record only because the doctor explicitly confirmed it (OQ-45-23); the drug-allergy alert of SAH-48-2 also applies to take-home medicines. (BR45-23)
- Given the header badge "n of m passed", then it counts the checks that pass; when all pass it is green and reads "m of m passed"; with the second check confirmed by the checker (SAH-66-3) it reads "· second check done". (BR45-20)
- Given the stages Decided, Doses checked, Second check, Pharmacy and Chair, then the first three are marked by the system as they happen ("Second check" when the checker has confirmed, SAH-66-3, not when the name is picked); Pharmacy is marked "Sent" when the doctor sends and Chair is shown disabled with "Later" (OQ-45-28: no chemo pharmacy queue or chair schedule). (BR45-6)
- Given "Independent second check by (not the prescriber)", then the buttons offer the clinic's Sr. nurse, Pharmacist and another doctor by name (the mockup: "Sr. nurse [NAME]", "Pharmacist [NAME]", "Dr. [RESIDENT NAME]"); the prescriber is never offered; the buttons are disabled with "Clear the red checks before the second check." while any check fails. Rule tag as above (C-8). (BR45-4)
- Given the prescriber picks a checker, then the checker is only **named** at this point: the sheet shows "Waiting for <name> to confirm the second check", the stage "Second check" is not marked, and the checker confirms in SAH-66-3 with their own sign-in or colleague PIN. Human-decided difference from the mockup, where picking the name completed the second check (SO-45-42, OQ-45-33). (BR45-4)
- Given the checker's name, role, way of confirming (own sign-in or colleague PIN) and time of confirmation, then they are recorded with the order once the checker has confirmed (SAH-66-3), and the prescriber's own sign-in or PIN can never stand in for them. (BR45-4)
- Given the send area, then the note reads "<n> check(s) still red", "Waiting for the second check" (a checker is named but has not confirmed, or none is named) or "All checks passed. Ready to send."; the button "Sign and send to pharmacy" is enabled only when all checks pass **and the checker has confirmed**; naming a checker is not enough. (BR45-20)
- Given a send is attempted (tapped, or called directly) without a confirmed second check, then the order is not sent and the answer is "The second check is not confirmed yet." (409); the server decides this at send time, not the screen. (BR45-20)
- Given the doctor taps "Sign and send to pharmacy", then the order (protocol, level, final doses, BSA used, labs drawn time, verifier, decision) is signed, stored as the cycle's order and the button reads "✓ Sent to pharmacy" with the note "Sent <drug dose list> · pharmacy preparing"; no message is sent to a pharmacy module or system (OQ-45-28) and "pharmacy informed" is only a recorded state. Rule tag as above (C-8). (BR45-4)
- Given the decision is Delay 1 week or Stop chemo, then no order is sent; the sheet shows "Record the hold" and the button records the hold with the decision, reason and date; the footer then does not ask for "send chemo order" but for "record the hold". (BR45-20)
- Given a sent order, when it is tapped again or retried after a lost network, then one order results; changes after sending follow SAH-66-1 (addendum). (BR45-18)
- Given a nurse, front desk, billing, pharmacist or counsellor, 403 on send; a doctor who is not the visit's doctor (not owner), 403; another clinic's visit, 404; no login, 401. (BR45-16, BR45-5)
- Given the audit, then one entry records who, when, visit id, protocol key, level, number of checks passed, verifier user id, role and the way the verifier confirmed (ids and keys, no drug text, PIN or patient data). (BR45-5)
## Notes for design
The tick list and the second check are the control that makes the order safe, so each check is computed on the server at send time, not only on the screen. The consent for chemotherapy (L-3) is the tick "Consent for this regimen"; its wording is not drawn.

### SAH-66-3 · As the named second checker (nurse, pharmacist or resident doctor), I want to confirm the chemotherapy order myself with my own sign-in or my colleague PIN, so that the check is really independent of the prescriber
Priority: Must
Screen: EMR mockup #OncologyTreatment, "Independent second check by (not the prescriber)" and "Sign and send to pharmacy". **The confirmation step itself is not drawn in the mockup**: it is added by the human's decision (DEC-039 addendum, OQ-45-33, SO-45-42). This id was added on 12 Nov 2026 at the end of the block to keep SAH-66-2 within one sprint.
## Acceptance criteria
- Given a checker is named (SAH-66-2) and all safety checks pass, then the order is open for confirmation in two ways, and the checker uses either: (a) **own sign-in**: the checker signs in to Sahaj on their own device or session, finds the order waiting for them and confirms it; (b) **colleague PIN**: the checker is beside the prescriber's device, is named on the sheet and types their own PIN, exactly as a second nurse does for a high-alert dose (SAH-32, SAH-38). (BR45-4)
- Given way (a), then the checker sees a read-only summary of the order: protocol, dose level, reason for reduction if any, each drug with calculated, rounded and final dose, the body surface area used and its source, labs drawn time, the decision and the safety check results; the checker can read it fully before confirming; the only action is "Confirm second check" (or leaving without confirming). Doses cannot be edited here. (BR45-4)
- Given way (b), then the PIN rules of SAH-38 apply unchanged: a wrong PIN answers "That PIN is not right." with no count of tries left; the 7-day cap of wrong tries by one person at one colleague's PIN, the per-caller cap and the PIN lock apply; a locked, not set or switched off PIN answers as today; if the PIN check cannot be done the confirmation fails closed with "We could not check the PIN. Try again."; the PIN is never written to a log, audit value or the order. (BR45-5)
- Given no PIN works for the named checker (locked, not set, caps reached), then the sheet says so and offers the other way (own sign-in) or another checker; there is no bypass, no "send anyway" and no paper option in the product. (BR45-20)
- Given the checker confirms, then the order records the checker's user id, role, way of confirming (sign-in or PIN) and the time, the stage "Second check" is marked, the header badge reads "· second check done", and the prescriber sees "Second check confirmed by <name>, <role>, 11:05 AM IST". (BR45-20)
- Given the named checker is the prescriber (own sign-in or the prescriber's own PIN), then the confirmation is refused with "The second check must be done by someone other than the prescriber." and nothing is recorded. (BR45-4)
- Given the person confirming is not the person named on the sheet, then the confirmation is refused (403); a user whose role is front desk, billing or counsellor cannot be named or confirm. (BR45-16)
- Given the order is changed after the checker confirmed (protocol, level, any final dose, body surface area, labs, decision, a safety check turning red, or a different checker named), then the confirmation is cleared with the message "Order changed after the second check. A new confirmation is needed." and the order cannot be sent until the checker confirms again. (BR45-20)
- Given the order was already sent, then the confirmation cannot be given or changed; a correction follows SAH-66-1 (addendum, new send, new second check). (BR45-18)
- Given a confirmation tapped twice or retried after a lost network, then one confirmation results; given a slow network, then a busy state shows and no confirmation is recorded silently or assumed. (BR45-20)
- Given the signed-in user is a nurse, pharmacist, doctor, visiting doctor, or an owner whose membership has doctor details, then the sidebar shows a menu entry "Second checks" (the analyst's default list page "Waiting for my second check" with the confirm sheet, an accepted difference from the mockup, H7, DEC-044); it opens the list of orders that name this user, placed right after Queue where the role has Queue, otherwise near the end of the menu before Staff (for a pharmacist: Today, IPD, Second checks). It shows in both menu modes and needs no module switch. Rule tag as above (C-8). (BR45-16, BR45-20)
- Given a front desk, billing or counsellor user, or an owner without doctor details, then the entry "Second checks" is not shown, and a direct call to the list answers 403; the menu is only a convenience, the server checks the role on every call (BR45-16). (BR45-16)
- Given the list shows no order waiting for the user, then it says "No second checks waiting for you."; a badge with the count of waiting orders may show on the entry, read from the order list and not from the sign-in answer. (BR45-20)
- Given the checker belongs to another clinic, then they cannot be named or confirm (404); given the order belongs to another clinic, 404; no login, 401. (BR45-5)
- Given the audit, then one entry records the checker's user id and role, the order's visit id, the way of confirming (sign-in or PIN) and the time; no PIN, name, dose or drug text is logged. (BR45-5)
## Notes for design
Nurse and pharmacist do not open the EMR, so way (a) needs a small "second check" page or list for them that shows only orders waiting on them and the read-only summary; it is reached from the menu entry "Second checks" (key `secondchecks`, route `/app/emr/second-checks`, ADR-023 D13 item 5). Its layout is not in the mockup and is for the angular developer to draw to the smallest size (analyst addition, default: a list "Waiting for my second check" with a confirm sheet). The colleague PIN of SAH-32 and SAH-38 was built for a second nurse on the Give sheet; every role may set a PIN (ADR-010), so the architect decides how it is reused for a pharmacist or a resident doctor, and whether the "No second PIN works?" line (SAH-38 T6, worded for medicines) needs a chemotherapy wording. Chemotherapy is OPD day care: this is the same control principle as high-alert doses in IPD but a separate order type (OQ-43-24).

## Out of scope for SAH-66
