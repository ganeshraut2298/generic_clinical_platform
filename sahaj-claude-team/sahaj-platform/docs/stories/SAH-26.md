# SAH-26 · Bed transfer and bed transfer rules (IPD, part 5)
Mockup screen: Transfer, Discharge, Case paper (card actions) — the patient sheet row "Move to another bed" (`openIp`, `iptrans`), the card Transfer sheet (`pcTransfer`), `ipLines` (bill segments), Clinic setup → Admission and deposits, panel "Bed transfer rules" (`cfgIpAdm`, `upgradeOk`, `rateFrom`)
Module: ipd

## 1. Story
As the nurse or front desk of a hospital, I want to move an admitted patient to another free bed, with the clinic's rules for moving to a better room, so that the patient is billed for each bed for the right days and the old bed goes to cleaning.

Scope note: this story moves the patient and splits the bill by bed category. The transfer rules panel is added to Clinic setup → Admission and deposits (the page of SAH-23); the Packages panel under it is SAH-29. The mockup has two transfer entry points: the inline row in the patient sheet (bed list and a Move button, no reason) and the Transfer sheet of the patient card (bed list with ward, a Reason list, an explanatory notice). The patient card is not built (Admitted is still the table of SAH-23), so the inline row is built, as in `openIp`; the optional reason is accepted by the API and stored, and the card Transfer sheet comes with the card. "Owner approves" in the mockup means that only the owner can perform the move (the owner switches role and does it); there is no approval request or queue, so none is built. The nurse handover line of the card sheet ("the nurse handover moves to ...'s colleague in the new ward") needs nurse-to-ward assignment, which does not exist, and is not built. Moving to a Reserved bed is not offered (the mockup lists only Available beds).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given IPD is Trial or On (SAH-19) and an admission in the Admitted state, then the admission sheet (SAH-23/25) shows, for OWNER, FRONT_DESK and NURSE, the row **Move to another bed**: a select of the Available beds, each option "<code> · <category> · ₹<daily bed charge>" (for example "101-B · General · ₹800"), ordered by category (General, Semi-private, Private, ICU), then by room and code, and a button **Move** with the swap icon. With no Available bed the select shows the single option "No free bed" and **Move** is disabled. In Read-only state, for other roles, and for admissions that are not Admitted, the row is not shown.
2. Given the rule **Moving to a higher category** (AC 7) and the category order General < Semi-private < Private < ICU: when the target bed's category is higher than the current bed's and the rule is "Owner approves", then only the OWNER can move the patient; any other role gets 403 problem type `owner-approval-needed` with the message "Moving to <category> needs the owner's approval. Ask the clinic owner." (the mockup text "Switch to Owner at the top, or ask Dr. Meera." is the demo role switch and is not used). When the rule is "Front desk can do it", OWNER, FRONT_DESK and NURSE can move to a higher category. Moving to the same or a lower category is always allowed to the three roles.
3. Given **Move**, then the server checks in this order, each with the message shown: the admission exists in the clinic (404) and is Admitted (409 problem type `not-admitted`); the target bed exists in the clinic (404) and is not the patient's current bed (400); the target bed is Available (409 `bed-not-free`, "That bed is no longer free. Pick another."); the owner rule of AC 2 (403). Then, in one transaction: the admission points to the new bed, the new bed becomes Occupied, the old bed becomes Cleaning (SAH-24 then offers "Cleaned · ready" / "Block bed" on it), a transfer row is written (AC 4) and the action is audited.
4. Given a transfer, then the bill days of the old bed are decided and stored once, at the moment of the transfer: bill days now = n (SAH-25 rule for "Bed day counted"); days already moved to earlier segments = p (the sum of the segment days of earlier transfers of this admission); days on the current bed so far c = max(0, n − p); segment days = max(0, c − 1) when **New bed rate starts** is "Same day", and c when it is "Next day". The segment (old category, segment days) is stored with the transfer; when segment days is 0 nothing is added to the bill for the old bed. Examples with "Bed day counted: At midnight": admitted on day 1, moved General → Private on day 3: "Same day" gives General 2 days and Private from day 3; "Next day" gives General 3 days and Private from day 4. Moved on day 1 with "Same day": the whole first day is billed at the new bed's category.
5. Given the running bill (SAH-25) of a patient with transfers, then the lines are, in this order: for each transfer with segment days > 0, "Bed · <old category> (before transfer)" = that category's daily bed charge × segment days, and "Doctor visits · <old category> rate" = the visit charge × that category's multiplier (rounded half-up to whole paise) × segment days; then "Bed · <current category>" and "Doctor visits · <doctor short name>" for the days on the current bed (bill days − all segment days, at least 0); "Nursing care" for all bill days (not split by category); extra charges (SAH-25) as before. Lines with zero quantity are dropped. Rates are the ones currently set in Clinic setup, as for every other line. The list, the board, the Deposit low pill and the Deposit alerts tile use the same bill, so they all follow the transfer.
6. Given a successful move, then the sheet reloads with the new bed in the summary strip (Bed, "<category> · <ward>") and the toast "<patient name> moved from <old code> to <new code> · <category> rate from today · <old code> sent for cleaning" ("from tomorrow" when the rule is "Next day"). The bed board shows the old bed as Cleaning and the new bed as Occupied with the patient.
7. Given Clinic setup → **Admission and deposits** (visible when IPD is Trial/On/Read-only), then a panel **Bed transfer rules** under Minimum deposit has: **Moving to a higher category** (hint "For example General to Private") with the two-way choice "Owner approves" (default) and "Front desk can do it"; **New bed rate starts** (hint "On the day of the transfer") with "Same day" (default) and "Next day". Each choice is saved at once. Only the OWNER can change them; other roles see them read-only. They are read by all four IPD roles. 409 `ipd-readonly` on change in Read-only state.
8. Given two people moving two patients to the same bed, or a double click on **Move**, then only the first succeeds and the second gets 409 `bed-not-free` ("That bed is no longer free. Pick another.") when it is a different patient, or 400 on `toBedId` ("The patient is already in this bed. Pick another.") when it is the same patient double-clicking (decided 5 Nov after QA finding F1: the state is correct, one transfer and one audit row). A patient can be moved more than once; each move is a new transfer.
9. Given the roles and tenants, then writes are OWNER, FRONT_DESK, NURSE (403 otherwise; DOCTOR can read the admission but not move it — OQ-26-2); every move and every change of the rules is audited (IPD_BED_TRANSFER with admission id, from and to bed id and code, from and to category, segment days, rule values; IPD_TRANSFER_RULES_UPDATED with the values) and never contains the patient's name or the reason for admission. Module Off answers 403 `module-off`. Another clinic's admission or bed answers 404 (RLS). Money stays in integer paise.
10. Given a slow or failed network on **Move**, then the button is disabled while the request runs, a failure shows the error and leaves the sheet unchanged, and the user can read the sheet again to see whether the move happened before trying again.

## 3. Technical design
- New migration (db-engineer): `ipd.config` gets `transfer_upgrade_owner_only boolean not null default true` and `new_rate_from text not null default 'same' check in ('same','next')`. New table `ipd.bed_transfer`: `id`, `tenant_id`, `admission_id` (composite FK), `from_bed_id`, `to_bed_id` (composite FKs to `ipd.bed`), `from_category_key`, `to_category_key` (check in gen/semi/pvt/icu), `segment_days integer not null check (>= 0)`, `new_rate_from text` (rule value used), `reason text null` (check in the four mockup reasons, see Notes), `transferred_at` (db default), `transferred_by`. Append-only (select + column insert; trigger denies update/delete/truncate, like `ipd.deposit`). Index `(tenant_id, admission_id, transferred_at)`. FORCE RLS.
- `ipd.admission` already has the bed link; the grant `update (bed_id)` is added (SAH-23 granted only status, discharged_at, version).
- Concurrency: IPD tenant advisory lock, then `select ... for update` on the two beds in id order, then on the admission (no deadlock between two transfers).
- `RunningBill` (SAH-25) takes the admission's transfers as input; list, board and tiles load transfers for all listed admissions in one batched query (no N+1).
- Reason values for the optional `reason` (from `pcTransfer`): "Needs closer monitoring", "Moving to a step-down bed", "Patient or family request", "Isolation". Stored as given; not shown anywhere in this story.
- Contract changes: `AdmissionDetail.bill` lines gain a marker for segment lines; `AdmissionOptions` is reused for the free beds; new `TransferRules`.

## 4. API (`/api/v1/ipd`, contract `docs/api/ipd.yaml`)
| Method | Path | Role | Request | Response | Errors |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GET | `/transfer-rules` | OWNER, FRONT_DESK, NURSE, DOCTOR | none | `TransferRules` | 403 |
| PUT | `/transfer-rules` | OWNER | `{upgradeNeedsOwner, newRateFrom}` | `TransferRules` | 400, 403, 409 |
| GET | `/admission-options` (existing) | OWNER, FRONT_DESK, NURSE | none | `AdmissionOptions` (free beds with category and rate) | 403 |
| POST | `/admissions/{id}/transfer` | OWNER, FRONT_DESK, NURSE | `{toBedId, reason?}` | `AdmissionDetail` | 400, 403 (`module-off`, `owner-approval-needed`), 404, 409 (`not-admitted`, `bed-not-free`, `ipd-readonly`) |

## 6. Out of scope, Open questions
Out of scope: the card Transfer sheet and its reason list in the UI, nurse handover to the new ward's nurse, an approval request queue for the owner, transfer history screen, transfer of a package admission (SAH-29 decides), moving to a Reserved bed, transfer between branches, printing, WhatsApp.
Open questions (also in `docs/stories/IPD-open-questions.md`):
- OQ-26-1 (billing rule, confirm): with "Same day", a transfer on the admission day bills the whole first day at the new category, and extra charges are re-priced at the current category's multiplier even if added before the move (the mockup computes them that way). Is this the intended rule for upgrades and downgrades? Proposed: as the mockup.
- OQ-26-2 (roles): the mockup's default permission matrix lets DOCTOR transfer and not VISITING_DOCTOR; the Roles and access page is not built. Proposed: OWNER, FRONT_DESK, NURSE only.
- OQ-26-3 (rule meaning): "Owner approves" is built as "only the owner can do it". Does the clinic want a request the owner approves later from their own screen? Proposed: no (mockup has none).
- OQ-26-4 (package): when the admission has a package (SAH-29) the mockup adds no segment, so a move to a dearer bed is not billed. Confirm.
- OQ-26-5 (UI): add the Reason list to the inline row, or wait for the card Transfer sheet? Proposed: wait.
- OQ-26-6 (handover): nurse handover on transfer needs a ward nurse assignment; ask whether it is wanted in phase 1.
