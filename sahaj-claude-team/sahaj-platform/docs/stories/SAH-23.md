# SAH-23 · Admission and deposits (IPD, part 2)
Mockup screens: IPD → Beds and admissions (`vIpd`, Admitted tab, KPI strip, "Admit patient"), the Admit-a-patient sheet (`openAdmit`/`ipfRender`/`ipfgo`), the Collect deposit action of the patient sheet (`openIp`, `ipdep`), and Clinic setup → Admission and deposits (`cfgIpAdm`, key `ipadm`, Minimum deposit panel only)
Module: ipd (reads patients and the team through `patient.api` and `platform.api`)

## 1. Story
As the front desk of a hospital, I want to admit a patient to a free bed, take the deposit and see who is admitted, so that every in-patient has an IP number, a bed and a deposit on record from the first hour.

Scope note: this story creates the admission and the deposit. The running bill (bed days, nursing, charges), deposit alerts, bed board, transfer, discharge, emergency and the patient sheet's other actions are later stories, so the Admitted list here has no Running bill column and the "Deposit alerts" and "Going home today" tiles are not shown yet. The mockup's WhatsApp notice is replaced by "The IP number, bed and deposit receipt are not sent yet (WhatsApp is not in this phase)." The mockup types the patient's name, age, sex and mobile into the sheet; here the patient is picked from Patients (or registered there) so that no duplicate patient records are created. Sex, age and mobile are shown from the patient file.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given IPD is Trial or On (SAH-19), then the sidebar shows **Beds and admissions** (menu key `ipd`) for the roles OWNER, FRONT_DESK, NURSE and DOCTOR, opening `/app/ipd`. When IPD is Off the item is absent and `/ipd/*` answers 403 `module-off`. In Read-only state everything can be read and every write answers 409 `ipd-readonly`.
2. Given the page, then the title "Beds and admissions", the lead (from the mockup) and the button **Admit patient** (not for read-only state or other roles), two tiles — **Beds occupied** ("N / M" with "X% occupancy · Y available") and **Beds available** — and the **Admitted** list: "Patient" (name, "IP/26-27/0042 · 56 M · Emergency · day 3"), "Bed" (code, category), "Doctor" (short name), "Deposit" (₹ total) and "Status" (pill "Deposit OK" when the deposit meets the minimum or the payer is not Self and a deposit was taken, pill "Insurance" / "Corporate" when the payer is not Self, pill "Deposit due" when the payer is Self and the deposit is below the minimum for that admission), and an **Open** button. Empty list: "No one is admitted right now." With no beds at all: the panel "Add your wards and beds to start" with "IPD is on. Add wards, rooms and beds with their categories and daily charges." and, for the owner, a button "Add wards and beds" linking to Clinic setup → Wards, rooms and beds.
3. Given **Admit patient**, then a sheet "Admit a patient" (subtitle "From the front desk") has: **Patient** (search by name or mobile among the clinic's patients, with the existing patient search; a link "Register a new patient" opens the existing patient registration dialog and then selects the new patient), **Admitting doctor** (active DOCTOR and OWNER-doctor members), **Admission type** (Planned, Emergency, Day-care, Maternity; default Planned), **Bed** (select of free beds grouped by category with "General · ₹800 a day" headings, each option "101-A · General ward · Male"), **Reason for admission** (text up to 200 characters, placeholder "Provisional diagnosis", default saved as "Under evaluation"), **Payer** (Self, Insurance, Corporate; default Self), **Deposit now** (₹, prefilled with the minimum) with the hint "Minimum ₹X · planned, General" plus " · emergency can be admitted first" when that rule applies and " · not needed for insurance" / "not needed for corporate" when payer is not Self, **Paid by** (Cash, UPI, Card; default UPI) and the checkbox "Admission and treatment consent signed by the patient or a relative". Primary button: "Admit to bed <code>".
4. Given a submit, then the server checks in this order, each with the message shown: the patient exists in the clinic (404); the patient is not already admitted (409 problem type `already-admitted`, "This patient is already admitted."); the doctor is an active doctor of the clinic (400); the bed exists and is free (409 `bed-not-free`, "That bed is no longer free. Pick another."); consent is signed (400, "Record the admission consent first"); when the payer is Self and the deposit is below the minimum and not (type Emergency and the clinic allows emergency without deposit) (400, "The deposit is below the minimum of ₹X. Collect at least that, or choose Insurance or Corporate."). Minimum deposit = base deposit for the admission type + category add-on of the bed's category.
5. Given a valid admission, then in one transaction: an IP number is issued as "IP/" + the financial year (April to March, IST, as "26-27") + "/" + a 4-digit running number per clinic and financial year (starting 0001, no gaps lost to failed requests beyond what the database sequence allows, never duplicated); the admission is stored as Admitted with admission time (IST), who admitted, source (Direct); the bed becomes occupied (status `occ`); the deposit (if more than ₹0) is stored with mode, time and who collected. Toast: "<name> admitted to bed <code> · <IP number> · deposit ₹X received" (or " · deposit due today" when none).
6. Given **Open** on a row, then a sheet shows the patient (name, age, sex), IP number, bed, ward, doctor, admission type, payer, reason, the deposits received (mode, date) and, for OWNER and FRONT_DESK in Trial or On state, **Collect deposit** (amount ₹, paid by, default UPI, button Collect; amount must be at least ₹1, whole rupees, at most ₹10,00,000). Toast: "₹X deposit received from <name>'s family". Other actions of the mockup's sheet (add a charge, move to another bed, start discharge, print interim bill, running bill) are not shown in this story.
7. Given Clinic setup → **Admission and deposits** (visible when IPD is Trial/On/Read-only), then the panel **Minimum deposit**: for each type (Planned, Emergency, Day-care, Maternity) a row "<Type> admission · Base deposit" with a rupee input (defaults ₹10,000, ₹5,000, ₹3,000, ₹15,000); the line "Added for the bed category: General ₹0 · Semi-private ₹2,000 · Private ₹5,000 · ICU ₹10,000" made editable (four rupee inputs, same defaults); a switch **Emergency without deposit** ("Admit first, take the deposit the same day", default on); **Deposit alert** ("Warn when the running bill reaches this share of the deposit", a stepper 50% to 100% in steps of 5, default 80%; stored now, used when the running bill exists); and the pill **Confirmed** or the button **Confirm these rules** (owner), which marks the rules confirmed (toast "Deposit rules saved"). Changing a value after confirming keeps it confirmed. Values are whole rupees between ₹0 and ₹10,00,000. The mockup's Bed transfer rules and Packages panels belong to later stories and are not shown. Non-owners see the page read-only.
8. Given a bed that has ever had an admission, then removing it (SAH-22) is refused with 409 problem type `bed-has-history` ("This bed has admission history and cannot be removed."), replacing the earlier delete without a guard.
9. Roles for writes: admit — OWNER, FRONT_DESK, NURSE; collect deposit — OWNER, FRONT_DESK; rules — OWNER. Reads of admissions and options — OWNER, FRONT_DESK, NURSE, DOCTOR (403 otherwise). Every write is audited with ids, IP number, type, payer, bed code, amounts and modes — never the patient's name, mobile, or the reason text. The reason for admission is clinical text: never logged, never in audit. Opening an admission's detail is not written to the patient record-access log in this story (noted for the security review). Tenant isolation (RLS) applies to every new table. Money is integer paise.

## 3. Technical design
- New migration (db-engineer) adds to `ipd.config`: `dep_planned_paise`, `dep_emergency_paise`, `dep_daycare_paise`, `dep_maternity_paise`, `cat_add_gen_paise`, `cat_add_semi_paise`, `cat_add_pvt_paise`, `cat_add_icu_paise`, `emergency_without_deposit` (default true), `deposit_alert_percent` (default 80, 50 to 100), `deposit_rules_confirmed` (default false) with the defaults above (paise 1000000, 500000, 300000, 1500000; 0, 200000, 500000, 1000000), and creates:
  - `ipd.admission`: `id`, `tenant_id`, `patient_id` (FK `patient.patient`, the one allowed cross-schema FK besides tenant), `ip_number text` (unique per tenant), `bed_id` (composite FK to `ipd.bed`), `doctor_membership_id uuid` (no FK), `admission_type` check in ('planned','emergency','daycare','maternity'), `source` check in ('DIRECT','OPD','EMERGENCY') default 'DIRECT', `payer` check in ('Self','Insurance','Corporate'), `reason text` (<= 200), `consent_signed boolean` (must be true), `status` check in ('ADMITTED','DISCHARGED') default 'ADMITTED', `admitted_at`, `admitted_by`, `discharged_at` null, `version`. Partial unique index on `(tenant_id, patient_id)` where status = 'ADMITTED'. Index `(tenant_id, status, admitted_at)`. FORCE RLS; select/insert/update, no delete.
  - `ipd.deposit`: `id`, `tenant_id`, `admission_id` (composite FK), `amount_paise` > 0, `mode` check in ('Cash','UPI','Card'), `collected_at`, `collected_by`. Append-only (select, insert; update/delete denied by trigger using `platform.deny_audit_mutation()` as in patient_consent).
  - `ipd.ip_sequence`: PK `(tenant_id, fy text)`, `last_no integer`; grants select/insert/update; the number is taken with `insert ... on conflict do update set last_no = last_no + 1 returning last_no` in the admission transaction.
- Concurrency: the admit transaction takes the IPD tenant advisory lock already used by SAH-22 writes, then `select ... for update` on the bed row.
- Patient data from `patient.api` (existing lookup port: name, age, sex; mobile only in detail), doctor names from `platform.api`. ipd must not call patient or platform internals.
- Menu key `ipd` is added by `NavigationPolicy` when IPD state is not Off, for OWNER, FRONT_DESK, NURSE, DOCTOR (via `ModuleEntitlementPort`, as `staff` was for HRA).
