# SAH-70 · Stories: Medical Oncology chemotherapy order sheet, part 2 (take-home medicines, next cycle and patient calendar, referrals, cycle note, Complete)
Requirement: `docs/requirements/SAH-45.md` (rules BR45-4, BR45-5, BR45-6, BR45-20, BR45-23, BR45-27, BR45-29; clinical rule C-8; billing rule B-3; DEC-039 answer 4 for Marathi and Hindi; answered, default accepted (DEC-039 addendum): OQ-45-27, OQ-45-28, OQ-45-30, OQ-45-32). Module: opd (cycle charge through the Chemotherapy course and the billing `api`; appointments through Appointments). Phase 4. Depends on SAH-66 (order sheet), SAH-65 (decision), SAH-48 (Prescription), SAH-55 (language headings), SAH-58 (course), SAH-51 (finish). **This id was added on 12 Nov 2026 to split the oversized SAH-66 (the "after the order" part of the sheet: E, F, referrals, cycle note, Complete).**
Mockup screen: Queue (sidebar → Queue) (EMR mockup #OncologyTreatment, "E · Take-home medicines", "F · Next cycle and patient calendar", referrals, "Cycle note (written for you)", footer).
Roles in every story: owner, doctor, visiting doctor, nurse, front desk, billing, pharmacist (the platform also has counsellor). Only doctor, visiting doctor and owner with doctor details (`clinic.consultation.write`) write the sheet, on their own patients (owner: all). Front desk and billing see the booked appointment and the cycle charge on their own screens. Nurse, pharmacist and counsellor get 403 on a direct call. "Not signed in" gets 401. The clinic always comes from the logged-in user, never from the request body.
Rule tag: "value from the EMR mockup, accepted by the human in DEC-039, not clinically reviewed" (BR45-4). It is repeated on each clinical rule below.

### SAH-70-1 · As an oncologist, I want take-home medicines and the next cycle with its tests, so that the patient leaves with a plan
Priority: Must
Screen: EMR mockup #OncologyTreatment, "E · Take-home medicines", "F · Next cycle and patient calendar", fact cells, calendar language and WhatsApp.
## Acceptance criteria
- Given "E · Take-home medicines", then six lines are offered: Ondansetron 8 mg (1-0-1 × 3 days, Nausea), Dexamethasone 4 mg (1-0-1 × 2 days, Delayed nausea), Pantoprazole 40 mg (1-0-0 × 5 days, Stomach protection), Pegfilgrastim 6 mg SC (Once, day 2, Prevents low counts / fever), Benzydamine mouthwash (3 times a day × 7 days, mouth sores) and Loperamide 2 mg ("After each loose motion (max 8)", note "Loose motions"); Ondansetron, Dexamethasone, Pantoprazole and Benzydamine are ticked at the start of a cycle, Loperamide is not ticked at the start, Pegfilgrastim is ticked only when suggested (next line), and the doctor can tick or untick any. Rule tag: value from the EMR mockup, accepted by the human in DEC-039, not clinically reviewed (C-8, C-10). (BR45-4)
- Given the protocol is TC (docetaxel and cyclophosphamide) or a fever or admission event is recorded (SAH-65-1), then Pegfilgrastim is pre-ticked and its note reads "Prevents low counts / fever · suggested: fever / regimen"; otherwise it is not pre-ticked and has no "suggested" text; the doctor's own tick or untick always wins over the suggestion. Rule tag as above (C-8, H22 default: Loperamide takes the usual new-row defaults of the Prescription, SAH-48). (BR45-4)
- Given a ticked take-home medicine, then it is added to the visit's Prescription (SAH-48-1) with its frequency, days and note, so that the printed prescription and the case paper show it; the drug-allergy alert of SAH-48-2 applies to each. (BR45-23)
- Given the decision is Delay 1 week or Stop chemo, then the take-home list still shows and the doctor decides what to give; Stop chemo adds the note "Palliative / supportive care" as in the decision option. (BR45-6)
- Given "F · Next cycle and patient calendar", then four fact cells show: "Next chemo" (or "Re-booked chemo" when the decision is Delay 1 week) with the date and the cycle number; "Blood test" the day before with "CBC, kidney, liver · day before"; "Echo" with "Before next cycle" when LVEF last was below 55% (highlighted) or "Before paclitaxel" otherwise, with "LVEF last x%"; "Imaging" with free text (the mockup's example "After cycle 4: Mammogram + USG breast · response check"). Rule tag as above (C-8). (BR45-4)
- Given the interval, then the next chemo date is today plus the protocol interval (21 days for AC, paclitaxel 3-weekly and TC; 7 days for weekly paclitaxel) or today plus 7 days when delayed; the doctor can change the date; the date is shown with the day name. Rule tag as above (C-8). (BR45-4)
- Given the doctor confirms, then a planned sitting for the next cycle is added to the Chemotherapy course (SAH-58-2) at the cycle charge and, with the doctor's tick "Book appointment", an appointment is booked through Appointments (SAH-11) on that day with the same doctor; the charge is the clinic tariff price or the package price the doctor sets (B-2: no sample rate). (BR45-27)
- Given the "patient calendar", then it lists the next chemo date, the blood test date, the echo and imaging items, the fever warning "call if 38 °C or above", blood test reminders and a daily symptom check; it can be printed now in English, Marathi or Hindi (language choice as in SAH-55-2; Marathi and Hindi wording for the calendar words is OQ-45-32, default English headings until supplied). Rule tag as above (C-8). (BR45-25)
- Given the "WhatsApp calendar" switch (with the language buttons) and the line "patient gets the new date on WhatsApp", then they are shown as drawn, switched off with the note "Needs the WhatsApp service". **Stays as built, switched off. Sending the calendar and the new date is Later (DEC-049).** (BR45-29)
- Given the SMS reminder of the mockup, then it is not shown as active (OQ-45-27). (BR45-29)
- Given a signed visit, then the sheet is read-only; a change of the next date after signing is a change of the sitting in the course by the doctor (SAH-58-2) with an audit entry. (BR45-18)
- Given a nurse, front desk, billing, pharmacist or counsellor, 403; a doctor who is not the visit's doctor (not owner), 403; another clinic's visit, 404; no login, 401. (BR45-16, BR45-5)
- Given any call, then no drug, date or imaging text is written to a log line or analytics event together with a patient. (BR45-5)
## Notes for design
The chemo day-care chair is not scheduled (no module, OQ-45-28): "Next chemo" is a date, not a chair. The take-home list is starter content (C-10).

### SAH-70-2 · As an oncologist, I want referrals, an automatic cycle note and a Complete that prints the cycle record, so that the visit is documented and the patient is handed on
Priority: Should
Screen: EMR mockup #OncologyTreatment, referral chips, "Cycle note (written for you)" with "Copy", "Save draft", "Complete visit", footer.
## Acceptance criteria
- Given the referrals, then eight chips show as drawn: Radiotherapy, Surgical oncology, Palliative care, Dietitian, Psycho-oncology counselling, Fertility counselling, Social worker (scheme papers) and Cardiology (echo); they toggle on tap (Dietitian is ticked in the mockup as example data; none is ticked at the start of a new visit). Rule tag: value from the EMR mockup, accepted by the human in DEC-039, not clinically reviewed (C-10). Each ticked referral becomes a referral line on the case paper (SAH-52-4 default) with the doctor's note; no referral is sent to any service (OQ-45-28). (BR45-6)
- Given "Cycle note (written for you)", then a paragraph is composed from the facts of the visit: the cycle number and day, the protocol and intent, the stage and receptors, the interval since the last cycle, the side-effect grades, the labs, ECOG, response, the decision and the reason if different, the dose level and doses, and the plan for the next cycle (the mockup's example begins "Cycle 4 day 1 of AC (neoadjuvant, Ca left breast IIIA, ER+ PR+ HER2−)"). Every figure comes from the recorded fields; nothing is written that is not recorded. Rule tag: value from the EMR mockup, accepted by the human in DEC-039, not clinically reviewed (C-8). (BR45-6)
- Given the note, then the doctor can edit it before signing; "Copy" copies it to the visit record's note and the message reads "Cycle note copied to the visit record". (BR45-6)
- Given the footer, then it reads "Still needed: <items>." in red, where the items are "n side effect grade(s)" (grades not confirmed, SAH-65-1), "systemic exam" (until all five systems of SAH-65-2 are marked), "today's decision", "diagnosis", and "send chemo order" or, when the decision is Delay 1 week or Stop chemo, "record the hold" (SAH-66-2); and "Ready to complete. Cycle record, take-home medicines and calendar will print." in green. Rule tag as above (C-8). (BR45-20)
- Given "Save draft", then everything is stored without checks (SAH-47-5). (BR45-20)
- Given "Complete visit", then the checks of SAH-51-1 run (including the drug-allergy alert); on confirm the visit is signed (SAH-51-2), the cycle record, the take-home prescription and the calendar print, the cycle charge goes to the Chemotherapy course (SAH-58-2) and, where the clinic bills it, to the visit bill through the billing `api` once, the visit moves to its next workflow step (SAH-9, SAH-12), and the follow-up for the next cycle is created from the next chemo date (SAH-51-6). (BR45-23)
- Given scheme or insurance (PM-JAY, MJPJAY) on the patient, then the scheme balance tag in the header (the mockup's example "MJPJAY · ₹ 38,000 left") is shown from the Scheme / insurance section's typed balance; no balance is fetched from a scheme. Rule tag as above (B-3). (BR45-4)
- Given the same Complete is tapped twice or retried after a lost network, then one signature, one set of charges and one follow-up result. (BR45-23)
- Given a nurse, front desk, billing, pharmacist or counsellor, 403; a doctor who is not the visit's doctor (not owner), 403; another clinic's visit, 404; no login, 401. (BR45-16, BR45-5)
- Given the printed cycle record, then it holds patient data; it is never logged and no copy is stored by the print action. (BR45-5)
## Notes for design
The note is generated text, not an AI service: it is built from the fields by fixed templates. The Treatment part's footer is the one of the Oncology screen as a whole (SAH-65 and SAH-66 share it).
