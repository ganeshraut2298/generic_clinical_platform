# SAH-28 · Emergency queue, triage and Emergency setup (IPD, part 7)
Mockup screen: IPD → Emergency — the Emergency tab and page (`ipEr`, `erreg`, `erseen`, `eradmit`, `errel`, `erref`, `erlvl`), the tile "Emergency" (`vIpd`), the sidebar item "Emergency", and Clinic setup → Emergency and triage (`cfgIpEr`, key `iper`, `iptri`, `iptg`, `ipbays`)
Module: ipd (the admission link changes `POST /admissions` of SAH-23; patients through `patient.api`)

## 1. Story
As the nurse or front desk of a hospital with an emergency room, I want to register an arriving patient in seconds, give a triage level, see who has waited past the target time, and then admit, treat and release or refer the patient, so that the most urgent patient is seen first and nobody is forgotten in the queue.

Scope note: the clinic setup (triage scale, target times, bays, unknown-patient registration) and the queue are in this story. The mockup alerts the duty doctor when a red patient arrives and when a target time is breached, and the "Treat and release" action sends a bill to the OPD billing counter; alerts need the notifications service (not in phase 1) and the release bill needs a patient identity and an emergency consultation price that the mockup does not define. So here: a breached target and a most-urgent arrival are shown on screen (card colour, tile, menu badge) and no message is sent; "Treat and release" closes the case without creating a bill (OQ-28-3, high priority); "Refer out" does not print a referral letter. The "Emergency" panel (dock) of the 26-ward hospital bed board and the In-patients strip of the Today page belong to other stories.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given IPD is Trial or On (SAH-19), then OWNER, FRONT_DESK and NURSE (the roles of the mockup's default menu; DOCTOR in OQ-28-4) get the menu item **Emergency** (key `emergency`, siren icon, opening `/app/emergency`) with a badge of the number of cases in the queue, placed right after Follow-ups and before Beds and admissions; in the Simple menu there is no separate item and the Beds and admissions page has the tab **Emergency** with the same count, plus a secondary button **Emergency** in the page header (hidden on the Emergency tab). In Read-only state the item, tab and button are not shown and `/ipd/emergency*` answers 409 `ipd-readonly` for writes and still reads. When IPD is Off: 403 `module-off`.
2. Given the Emergency page, then the title is "Emergency" with the lead "Triage first. Admit, treat and release, or refer out.", and it has two panels: **Quick registration** (left, only for users who may register, when IPD is not Read-only) and **Emergency queue** (right) with the note "Most urgent first, then longest waiting · bays free X of Y".
3. Given **Quick registration** with the small text "Name, age, sex and the complaint are enough. Full details can wait.", then the form has: the checkbox "Name not known yet" (only when Unknown patient registration is on); **Name** (placeholder "Full name", hidden while the box is ticked); **Age (approx.)**; **Sex** (Male, Female, Other; default Male); **Mobile of patient or relative** "(optional)"; **Main complaint** (placeholder "For example: chest pain for 1 hour"); **Triage level** as one button per level, each with the level name in bold and the text "<description> · within N min" ("<description> · now" when the target is 0), default the second level; and the button **Register and add to queue** (siren icon).
4. Given **Register and add to queue**, then: without a name and without the box ticked the message "Enter a name, or tick "Name not known yet""; without a complaint "Enter the main complaint". Name up to 80 characters, age a whole number 0 to 120 or empty (shown "?"), mobile empty or 10 digits, complaint up to 200 characters. An unknown patient is named "Unknown male, about 40" (with "female", or "person" for Other, and "?" when the age is empty). The case gets the arrival time (now), status Waiting, the chosen level, and, only when the level is the most urgent one and a bay is free, the first free bay ("ER-1", "ER-2", …). Toast: "<name> added to the emergency queue · <level name>" with " · bay ER-1" when a bay was given. The form is cleared (level back to the second level).
5. Given the queue, then cases are ordered by level (most urgent first), then by longest wait. Each card shows: the level name and description on the left in the level colour (Red or Level 1 and 2 critical, Yellow or Level 3 warning, Green or Level 4 and 5 good); the name (bold), "<age> <sex letter>" with " · ER-1" when it has a bay, the complaint; and pills: **Seen by doctor** (good) once seen, otherwise "Waiting 38 min · target 30 min" (neutral) or, past the target, the critical pill with the alert icon (card highlighted); "target now" when the target is 0 (any wait over 0 minutes is past it); **Identity pending** (warning) on an unknown patient. Waiting minutes are computed from the arrival time on the server clock; the page reloads after each action and every 60 seconds while open. Empty queue: "No one in emergency right now."
6. Given the actions on a card (for users who may act, IPD not read-only): **Doctor has seen** (only while Waiting; primary) marks the case Seen with the time and the user, toast "<name> seen by the duty doctor"; the level list (accessible name "Triage level for <name>") changes the level, keeps the arrival time and its wait, records who and when, toast "<name> re-triaged to <level name> · logged"; **Admit** (bed icon) opens the Admit sheet (AC 7); **Treat and release** closes the case as Released, toast "<name> treated and released"; **Refer out** closes it as Referred, toast "<name> referred out". Closed cases leave the queue and the bay is freed. A case already closed answers 409 `case-closed` ("This patient is no longer in the queue.").
7. Given **Admit** on a case, then the Admit-a-patient sheet of SAH-23 opens with subtitle "From Emergency", Admission type Emergency, Reason for admission = the complaint, and the bed preselected as the first Available ICU bed for the most urgent level, otherwise the first Available General bed (when there is no free bed, the toast "No free bed. Mark a cleaned bed ready, or transfer a patient."). The Patient field is filled by the existing patient search/registration (OQ-28-1): for a known name the search opens with the name and mobile; for an unknown patient the "Register a new patient" dialog opens with the placeholder name, age and sex. When the admission is saved with the case id, the admission gets source EMERGENCY, the case becomes Admitted with the admission linked, the bay is freed, in one transaction (409 `case-closed` if someone closed the case meanwhile). Cancelling the sheet leaves the case in the queue.
8. Given the tiles on Beds and admissions, then the second tile **Emergency** shows the number of cases in the queue and the text "All within target time" or, in the critical colour, "N over target time"; in Read-only state it shows "—". The Beds occupied tile stays first, Deposit alerts and Going home today follow (SAH-25/27).
9. Given Clinic setup → **Emergency and triage** (key `iper`, visible when IPD is Trial/On/Read-only), then the panel **Triage scale** has: **Levels** (hint "3 colours suit a small nursing home; 5 levels suit a hospital emergency") with the choice "3 colours" (default) and "5 levels"; one row per level, "Red · Immediate", "Yellow · Urgent", "Green · Can wait" (3 colours) or "Level 1 · Resuscitation", "Level 2 · Emergent", "Level 3 · Urgent", "Level 4 · Less urgent", "Level 5 · Non-urgent" (5 levels), each with the hint "Target time to see a doctor" and a stepper in steps of 5 minutes from "Now" (0) to 240 min (accessible name "<level> target"); **Emergency bays** (hint "Shown on the emergency queue") a stepper 0 to 10, default 2; **Unknown patient registration** (hint "Register as "Unknown male, about 40" and add details later") a switch, default on. Defaults: 3 colours 5 / 30 / 120 min; 5 levels Now / 10 / 30 / 60 / 120 min (the two sets are kept separately). Each change is saved at once and audited; only the OWNER can change them, other roles see them read-only. If the setup overview shows a summary line for this page, it reads "3 colours · 2 bays" or "5 levels · 2 bays".
10. Given the owner changes the scale with active cases in the queue, then their levels are mapped and logged: 3 → 5 colours: Red → Level 2, Yellow → Level 3, Green → Level 5; 5 → 3: Level 1 and 2 → Red, Level 3 → Yellow, Level 4 and 5 → Green (as the mockup; OQ-28-2: clinical sign-off of this mapping). Target times of the new scale apply at once.
11. Given roles: reading the queue and settings — OWNER, FRONT_DESK, NURSE, DOCTOR; register, re-triage — OWNER, FRONT_DESK, NURSE, DOCTOR; Doctor has seen, Treat and release, Refer out — OWNER, NURSE, DOCTOR; Admit — OWNER, FRONT_DESK, NURSE (the admit roles of SAH-23); setup — OWNER (OQ-28-4). Others get 403. Another clinic's case answers 404 (RLS).
12. Given patient safety and privacy, then the name, mobile, age and complaint are shown on screen only: never logged, never in the audit text. Audit records (IPD_ER_REGISTERED, IPD_ER_SEEN, IPD_ER_RETRIAGED, IPD_ER_RELEASED, IPD_ER_REFERRED, IPD_ER_ADMITTED, IPD_ER_SETTINGS_UPDATED) hold the case id, level, bay and times only. The level history of a case (registered level, each re-triage with who and when) cannot be edited or deleted.
13. Given a slow or failed network, then the registration button is disabled while the request runs and a failed request keeps what was typed; because a double registration would put the same patient twice in the queue, the request carries a client-generated key and a repeat with the same key returns the first case.

## 3. Technical design
- New migration (db-engineer): `ipd.config` gets `triage_scale smallint not null default 3 check in (3,5)`, `triage_targets_3 smallint[]` default `{5,30,120}`, `triage_targets_5 smallint[]` default `{0,10,30,60,120}` (each value 0 to 240, step 5), `er_bays smallint not null default 2 check (between 0 and 10)`, `unknown_patient_registration boolean not null default true`.
- New table `ipd.emergency_case` (tenant_id, FORCE RLS): `id`, `patient_id` (FK `patient.patient`, null until linked), `display_name text` (<= 80), `age smallint null`, `sex` check in M/F/O, `mobile text null` (10 digits), `complaint text` (<= 200), `identity_pending boolean`, `level smallint`, `bay text null`, `status` check in WAITING/SEEN/ADMITTED/RELEASED/REFERRED, `arrived_at` (db default), `registered_by`, `seen_at/seen_by`, `closed_at/closed_by`, `admission_id` (composite FK, null), `client_key uuid` (unique per tenant), `version`. Index `(tenant_id, status, arrived_at)`. Never deleted. Grants: select, insert, update on the status/level/bay/closure columns only.
- New table `ipd.emergency_triage_event` (append-only like `ipd.deposit`): `id`, `case_id`, `level`, `scale`, `reason` (REGISTERED / RETRIAGED / SCALE_CHANGED), `at`, `by`.
- Waiting minutes and "over target" are computed on read from `arrived_at` and the clinic targets; nothing is stored or scheduled. "Over target" applies to status WAITING only.
- Bay choice and admission linking run under the IPD tenant advisory lock (two simultaneous red arrivals never get the same bay).
- Admission: `POST /admissions` gets the optional `emergencyCaseId`; the admission service calls the emergency service inside the same transaction.
- Menu key `emergency` is added by `NavigationPolicy` through `ModuleEntitlementPort` like `ipd`.
- Contract changes: `AdmissionInput.emergencyCaseId`; new `EmergencyQueue`, `EmergencyCaseInput`, `EmergencySettings`.

## 4. API (`/api/v1/ipd`, contract `docs/api/ipd.yaml`)
| Method | Path | Role | Request | Response | Errors |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GET | `/emergency` | OWNER, FRONT_DESK, NURSE, DOCTOR | none | `EmergencyQueue` (levels with targets, bays, cases with wait minutes and over-target flag, counts) | 403 |
| POST | `/emergency/cases` | OWNER, FRONT_DESK, NURSE, DOCTOR | `EmergencyCaseInput` (+ client key) | `EmergencyQueue` (201) | 400, 403, 409 |
| POST | `/emergency/cases/{id}/seen` | OWNER, NURSE, DOCTOR | none | `EmergencyQueue` | 403, 404, 409 |
| PUT | `/emergency/cases/{id}/level` | OWNER, FRONT_DESK, NURSE, DOCTOR | `{level}` | `EmergencyQueue` | 400, 403, 404, 409 |
| POST | `/emergency/cases/{id}/release` | OWNER, NURSE, DOCTOR | none | `EmergencyQueue` | 403, 404, 409 |
| POST | `/emergency/cases/{id}/refer` | OWNER, NURSE, DOCTOR | none | `EmergencyQueue` | 403, 404, 409 |
| GET | `/emergency-settings` | OWNER, FRONT_DESK, NURSE, DOCTOR | none | `EmergencySettings` | 403 |
| PUT | `/emergency-settings` | OWNER | `EmergencySettings` | `EmergencySettings` | 400, 403, 409 |
| POST | `/admissions` (existing) | OWNER, FRONT_DESK, NURSE | `AdmissionInput` + `emergencyCaseId?` | `AdmissionDetail` (201) | 400, 403, 404, 409 (`case-closed`) |

(409 `ipd-readonly` applies to every write; `case-closed` to every action on a closed case.)

## 6. Out of scope, Open questions
Out of scope: alert messages to the duty doctor (notifications service), the bill created by "Treat and release", referral letter printing, hospital bay map, editing a case after registration (full details, merging an unknown patient later is done in Patients), the Emergency dock on the 26-ward board, the In-patients strip on Today, ambulance or police intimation, MLC marking, live (websocket) refresh.
Open questions (also in `docs/stories/IPD-open-questions.md`):
- OQ-28-1 (patient identity): the Admit sheet picks a patient from Patients. Should an emergency patient be registered in Patients at arrival (including "Unknown male, about 40", later merged by SAH-10), or only when admitted? What does Patients require (age or date of birth, mobile)? Proposed: register only when admitting, through the existing registration dialog, with the placeholder name for an unknown patient.
- OQ-28-2 (clinical): confirm the triage level names, descriptions, default target times (3 colours 5/30/120; 5 levels Now/10/30/60/120) and the 3-to-5 and 5-to-3 level mapping.
- OQ-28-3 (billing, high priority): "Treat and release" in the mockup creates an OPD visit and a ₹600 "Emergency consultation" bill in the billing counter. Where does that price come from (Tariff and services?) and which patient record does the bill belong to (the release case may have no registered patient)? Until answered, no bill is created and an ER treatment can go unbilled.
- OQ-28-4 (roles): the mockup's menu default shows Emergency to front desk, nurse and owner; should DOCTOR see and use it (the doctor marks "seen")? Proposed: DOCTOR can read and act, but the menu item for DOCTOR is decided with the roles.
- OQ-28-5: how does the duty doctor get alerted (push, WhatsApp, SMS, call) when a most urgent patient arrives or a target is breached? Not possible in phase 1; proposed: screen only.
- OQ-28-6: bays are assigned automatically to the most urgent level only and cannot be changed or freed by hand in the mockup; confirm.
