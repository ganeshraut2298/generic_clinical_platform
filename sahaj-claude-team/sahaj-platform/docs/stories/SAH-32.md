# SAH-32 · Medicines (MAR) and giving a dose with the bedside checks (IPD, part 11)
Mockup screen: Nursing sheet (card → Nursing) — the "Medicines (MAR)" tab of the Nursing sheet (`sfNsTabs`, `sfMar`, `sfSlot`, `sfSlotChip`, `sfMedHead`, `SF_FREQ`) and the Give sheet (`sfGive`, `sfgiveok`, `sfnogive`, `sfscan`, `sfIdBar`)
Module: ipd (reads the team through `platform.api`)

## 1. Story
As a nurse on a ward, I want to see today's doses for each patient, and to record a dose as given only after the wristband and the patient's own name and date of birth have been checked (and a second nurse for high-alert medicines), or record why it was not given, so that the right patient gets the right medicine at the right time and every dose is on record.

Scope note: this story is the MAR (medicine administration record) of one patient inside the Nursing sheet of SAH-30, using the verified orders of SAH-31. The cross-patient list "Medicine round" and the counters on the Safety tab belong to the Safety board story; this story only provides the per-patient count on the MAR tab. The mockup's **Scan wristband** button does not scan anything: it copies the patient's own UHID into the box and so would make the identity check pass by itself. That must not be built. Here the box accepts what a keyboard-type barcode scanner sends or what the nurse types; camera scanning and wristband printing are not in phase 1 (OQ-32-1). The second-nurse PIN has no source in the mockup (OQ-31-3 holds the question for both stories). Dose times are fixed by the frequency (SAH-31 AC 12); when a dose is "due" or "late" uses the server clock in Asia/Kolkata.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given the Nursing sheet (SAH-30), then it has two tabs at the top, using the accessible tab pattern: **Vitals and notes** (heart icon, default) and **Medicines (MAR)** (pill icon) with a small count of the patient's doses that are due or late, shown only when above 0. The tab is shown to OWNER, NURSE and DOCTOR (DOCTOR reads only). The sheet title stays "Nursing · <patient name>"; on the MAR tab the subtitle is "Bed <code> · medicine round, today 11:05 AM" (the current time in IST).
2. Given the MAR tab, then it shows the identity bar of SAH-31 (name, "<UHID> · <age> · <sex> · bed <code>", allergy chip) and one block per order that is Pharmacist check or Active (Rejected and Stopped orders are not listed): the medicine name; for a high-alert medicine the red pill "High-alert · <Anticoagulant|Insulin|Concentrated electrolyte>" with the hint "High-alert medicine: a second nurse checks every dose"; the small line "<route> · <frequency> · <ordering doctor>"; the status pill "Pharmacist check" or "Active"; and a row of dose slots, one per time of the frequency. With no such order: "No medicines on the chart. The doctor orders them from EMR." A closing line says "Every dose needs the wristband scan and the patient's name and date of birth. High-alert medicines also need a second nurse." and the button **Done**.
3. Given a slot of today (IST), then its state is: **Given** when recorded as given — a check icon, the slot time, and the small text "<time recorded> · <nurse's last name>" (hint "Given by <nurse>" and ", checked by <second nurse>"); **Not given / Refused** when recorded so — the slot time and the small text "Not given · <reason>" or "Refused · <reason>"; **Waiting for pharmacist** when the order is still Pharmacist check; otherwise by the clock: more than 60 minutes after the slot time = **late** (red button, small text "1 h late · Give", the hours rounded down; the mockup's "N min late" form can never occur because late starts after 60 minutes), within 60 minutes before to 60 minutes after the slot time = **due** (amber button, "Due now · Give"), more than 60 minutes before the slot time = **Later today** (grey, not clickable). Times read "8 AM", "2 PM", "8:30 AM". The buttons are disabled for users who may not give (AC 4). A pending order shows "Cannot be given until the pharmacist checks the order." (lock icon).
4. Given a slot button, then only NURSE and OWNER can open the Give sheet (OQ-32-3: DOCTOR); others see the button disabled and, if they call the API, get 403 "Only nurses and doctors can give medicines."; an order that is not Active answers 409 `order-not-verified` ("This order is not checked by the pharmacist yet."); a slot that is more than 60 minutes ahead answers 409 `dose-not-due`; a slot that already has a record answers 409 `dose-recorded` ("This dose is already recorded."), so the same dose can never be given twice by two nurses or a double tap.
5. Given the Give sheet, then the title is "Give · <medicine>", the subtitle "<time> dose · <route> · <frequency>" plus " · 1 h late" (hours rounded down) when late, and it holds: the identity bar; the step **1 · Confirm the patient with two identifiers**, with the box (accessible name "Wristband MRN", placeholder "Scan the wristband or type the MRN") and the checkbox "The patient said their name and date of birth, or a relative confirmed them"; for a high-alert medicine the step **2 · Second nurse check · high-alert <class in lower case>** ("A second nurse checks the drug, dose, patient and route, then enters their PIN.") with **Checked by** (select "Choose a nurse", the other active nurses of the clinic, never the signed-in nurse) and **Their PIN** (4 digits); and the last step ("2" or "3") **If you are not giving it** with **Reason** ("Choose a reason", "Patient refused", "Held on doctor's advice", "Nil by mouth", "Medicine not available", "Patient away from the ward"). Footer: **Record not given** and the primary **Given** (check icon). There is no "Scan wristband" button (see the scope note).
6. Given **Given**, then the server checks in this order and answers with the message shown: the box is empty (400, "Scan the wristband first."); the box does not equal the patient's UHID, compared without case and spaces (409 `wristband-mismatch`; the screen shows "Wristband does not match <patient name>. Stop. Check the patient before giving anything." and under the box "Wrong patient. This band is not <patient name>'s."; the entry "Stopped at the wristband check: scanned <text sent, first 40 characters>, expected <UHID> (<medicine>)" is written to the event log as kind deny); the checkbox is not ticked (400, "Confirm the second identifier: name and date of birth."); for a high-alert medicine, no second nurse or a PIN that is not exactly 4 digits (400, "High-alert medicine: a second nurse must check it and enter a 4-digit PIN."); the second nurse is the same person (400, "The second check must be a different nurse."); the second nurse's PIN is not theirs (403 `wrong-pin`, mechanism OQ-31-3; a format-only check is not acceptable for release). All the checks are made on the server; the client's ticks are only a record of the nurse's statement.
7. Given a valid **Given**, then one dose record is saved (order, slot time, outcome Given, the nurse, the second nurse if any, the scanned text, the time on the server clock) in one transaction with the entry "Gave <medicine> (<time> dose) · wristband scanned" plus " · second check by <second nurse>" (kind give), the toast is "<medicine> recorded as given to <patient name>", the Give sheet closes and the MAR tab shows the slot as Given.
8. Given **Record not given**, then a reason is required (400, "Choose why the dose is not given."); the dose record is saved with the outcome Refused when the reason is "Patient refused" and Held for any other reason, with the entry "Did not give <medicine> (<time> dose) · <reason>" (kind give), and the toast is "Recorded: <medicine> not given · <reason>. The doctor sees this on the chart." The wristband and second-nurse steps are not required to record a dose as not given. (Where the doctor sees it: OQ-32-4.)
9. Given dose records, then they are append-only: a recorded dose can never be changed or deleted, there is no "undo" and no late edit; a mistaken record is handled by a clinical note and the doctor (OQ-32-5). Opening the MAR tab is not written to the record-access log in this story (same open question as OQ-30-7).
10. Given a medicine order that is Stopped (SAH-31), then it disappears from the MAR and its remaining slots can no longer be recorded; recorded doses stay in history. Given an order verified in the middle of the day, then only slots after the verification time, minus the 60-minute due window, can be late; earlier slots of that day do not appear as late (OQ-32-2).
11. Given a Read-only IPD, a discharged or not Admitted patient, then dose records answer 409 (`ipd-readonly`, `not-admitted`); IPD Off 403 `module-off`; another clinic's admission or order 404 (RLS). Reading the MAR stays possible for OWNER, NURSE, DOCTOR.
12. Given audit and privacy, then the audit events IPD_DOSE_GIVEN and IPD_DOSE_NOT_GIVEN hold ids, medicine key, slot time, outcome and reason key only, never the patient's name; the scanned text and clinical notes are never written to application logs. Roles and tenants as in the rows above.
13. Given a slow or failed network, then **Given** and **Record not given** are disabled while the request runs, a dose is never shown as given until the server has answered, a failed request keeps the sheet open with what was entered (the PIN is cleared), and a repeated request for the same slot is refused by the rule of AC 4 and not recorded twice. There is no offline queue in this phase.

## 3. Technical design
- New migration (db-engineer): `ipd.dose_administration` (tenant_id, FORCE RLS), `id`, `admission_id`, `order_id` (composite FK to `ipd.medicine_order`), `slot_at timestamptz` (the dose time of the day), `outcome` check in GIVEN/HELD/REFUSED, `reason_key text null` (check in the five reasons; required for HELD and REFUSED), `given_by`, `second_check_by null` (required when the medicine is high-alert and the outcome is GIVEN: enforced by the service, since the flag is on the medicine), `identity_scan text null` (<= 40), `recorded_at` (db default). Unique `(tenant_id, order_id, slot_at)`. Append-only (select + column insert; trigger denies update/delete/truncate). This is a high-volume table: design so it can be partitioned by `slot_at` later (rule: plan partitioning before 50 million rows).
- Slots are computed, not stored: for an order and a day, the times of its frequency in Asia/Kolkata; the state is derived from the dose records, the order status and the server clock (one small class, `DoseSlots`, used by the MAR, the count on the tab and later the Safety board).
- The identity check and the high-alert rule run in the service (`MedicineAdministration`); the second nurse's PIN is verified by the same component as the pharmacist's PIN (SAH-31 OQ-31-3).
- Team members for "Checked by": active NURSE members from `platform.api` (names only), excluding the caller.
- Contract changes: `NursingSheet` (SAH-30) gains no field; new `Mar`, `GiveInput`.

## 4. API (`/api/v1/ipd`, contract `docs/api/ipd.yaml`)
| Method | Path | Role | Request | Response | Errors |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GET | `/admissions/{id}/mar` | OWNER, NURSE, DOCTOR | none | `Mar` (identity, orders with today's slots and states, due/late count, nurses for the second check) | 403, 404 |
| POST | `/medicine-orders/{id}/doses` | OWNER, NURSE | `{slotAt, outcome: GIVEN, wristband, identityConfirmed, secondNurseId?, secondNursePin?}` | `Mar` | 400, 403 (`wrong-pin`), 404, 409 (`wristband-mismatch`, `order-not-verified`, `dose-not-due`, `dose-recorded`, `not-admitted`, `ipd-readonly`) |
| POST | `/medicine-orders/{id}/doses` | OWNER, NURSE | `{slotAt, outcome: NOT_GIVEN, reasonKey}` | `Mar` | 400, 403, 404, 409 (as above) |

## 5. Tasks
### Tasks — Backend
Migration (db-engineer); `DoseSlots` (slot times, windows, late text) with unit tests at the edges (60 minutes before and after: due at exactly 60 minutes late, late at 61, later at 61 minutes ahead, hour text, order verified mid-day, stopped order); `MedicineAdministration` with unit tests for each check in AC 6 and their order, high-alert and not, same nurse, double record; event and audit writes. Real-DB tests by qa-engineer (clock hook for the time of day).
### Tasks — Frontend
Tabs in the Nursing sheet, MAR tab with slot chips, Give sheet with its steps and error texts, count badge; unit tests.

## 6. Out of scope, Open questions
Out of scope: the "Medicine round" list across patients and the Safety tab counters; camera or Bluetooth scanning and wristband printing; barcode on the medicine pack; dose, route or site entry on giving; PRN and stat doses; an undo or correction of a dose; missed-dose follow-up at midnight; medicine stock deduction (Pharmacy is Later); notifications to the doctor when a dose is not given or refused.
Open questions (also in `docs/stories/IPD-open-questions.md`):
- OQ-32-1 (safety): confirm that the simulated "Scan wristband" button is not built, and decide how the wristband is made and read in phase 1 (UHID printed on a wristband by hand or by the label printer; keyboard-type scanner). The UHID is the MRN of the mockup.
- OQ-32-2 (clinical): when is a dose "late" for an order placed or verified during the day, and what happens to a dose nobody recorded by midnight (silent loss vs a "missed" record)? Proposed: no late status before verification; missed doses become part of the Safety board story.
- OQ-32-3 (roles): the mockup allows "nurses and doctors" to give (in the demo the owner is the doctor). Can DOCTOR give? Proposed: NURSE and OWNER only.
- OQ-32-4: where does the doctor see "Not given" and "Refused" (the mockup says "The doctor sees this on the chart")? The EMR sheet of SAH-31 does not show doses. Proposed: add the last outcome of each order to the EMR list.
- OQ-31-3 (shared, blocking): the PIN of the second nurse.
- OQ-32-5: how is a wrongly recorded dose corrected? Proposed: no correction in the product; a nursing note and the doctor.
