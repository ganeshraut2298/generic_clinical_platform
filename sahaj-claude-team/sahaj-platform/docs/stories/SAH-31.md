# SAH-31 · Medicine orders with safety checks, and the pharmacist check (IPD, part 10)
Mockup screen: EMR sheet (card → EMR) — the "Medicines on chart" block and the order form (`pcEmr`, `sfOrdBlock`, `sfCheck`, `sfordchk`, `sfstop`), and IPD → Safety, the "Pharmacist check" panel and its sheets (`ipSafety`, `pendRow`, `sfver`, `sfverok`, `sfrej`, `sfrejok`)
Module: ipd (reads the allergy text through `patient.api`)

## 1. Story
As a doctor, I want to order a medicine for an admitted patient with the system checking allergies, duplicates and interactions before the order is placed, and as the pharmacist I want to check every new order before the first dose, so that a wrong or dangerous medicine is stopped before a nurse can give it.

Scope note: this story covers ordering and checking. Giving a dose (the MAR with the wristband check and the second-nurse check) is SAH-32; until it exists a verified order is Active and waits for the MAR. The mockup was split in two because one story would hold the order form, the rule engine, the pharmacist panel and the dose giving. The Safety tab of the mockup also holds escalations, the medicine round, emergency access (break-glass) and the clinical audit trail; those are the Safety board story, so this story adds the Safety tab with the **Pharmacist check** panel only. The EMR sheet in the mockup also shows "Results" made up from billed tests ("Reported"); there is no results module, so that block is not built. The mockup opens the EMR sheet from the EMR button of the patient card; the card is not built, so an **EMR** button is added to the admission sheet (OQ-31-7). The medicine list, the classes, the high-alert flags and the three interaction rules are the mockup's demo content and must be approved by a doctor and a pharmacist before real use (OQ-31-1). Notifying the ordering doctor of a rejection needs notifications (not in phase 1).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given IPD is Trial or On and an Admitted patient, then the admission sheet gets an **EMR** button (emr icon) for OWNER, DOCTOR and NURSE. It opens the sheet "EMR · <patient name>" with the subtitle "<UHID> · <age> · <Male|Female|Other> · <admitting doctor name>". FRONT_DESK and BILLING get no button and the API answers 403; the sheet text for a role without clinical access is "Clinical details are hidden for your role." Opening the sheet writes the entry "Opened the clinical record" (kind view) to the clinical event log (SAH-30) and to the patient record-access log, and the sheet ends with the line "Opening this record is logged in the clinical audit trail." and the button **Done**.
2. Given the sheet, then it shows: the identity bar with the patient's name, "<UHID> · <age> · <sex> · bed <code>" and the allergy chip — "No known allergies" (green) when the patient's allergy text says "none known", "Allergies not recorded" when it is empty or says "not recorded", otherwise "Allergy: <text>" (red); a strip with **Diagnosis** (the reason for admission) and **Day** (bill days); the block **Medicines on chart** ("Doctor's orders · pharmacist checked before the first dose") and, for doctors, the order form. The chip and the strip are always visible at the top.
3. Given the Medicines on chart block, then each order shows the medicine name and, for a high-alert medicine, the red pill "High-alert"; the small line "<route> · <frequency> · <ordering doctor>" with " · override: <reason>" when the doctor continued past a warning; a status pill — **Pharmacist check** (amber), **Active** (green), **Rejected** (red), **Stopped** (grey); and, for doctors, a **Stop** button on Pharmacist check and Active orders. With no orders: "No medicines ordered yet". A Rejected order also shows the pharmacist's reason in its small line (OQ-31-5).
4. Given the order form (only DOCTOR, and OWNER or VISITING_DOCTOR when they are doctors; for others the text "Only doctors can order medicines." with a lock icon), then **Medicine** is a select of the 14 medicines of the starting list (AC 12) written "<name>" or "<name> · high-alert", **How often** is a select of "Once daily", "Twice daily", "Three times daily", "8-hourly" (no dose entry: the strength is in the medicine name), and the button **Check and order** (shield icon) with the hint "Checks allergies, duplicates and interactions. A pharmacist checks every order before the first dose." and, once a check has run, **Clear**.
5. Given **Check and order**, then the server runs the checks on every request (the client is never trusted) and answers by the first rule that applies: **Hard stop** — the order is not placed, the red notice "Hard stop. This order cannot be placed." lists every blocking message, and the entry "Order blocked: <medicine> · <first message>" (kind deny) is written to the event log. Blocking messages: (a) a documented allergy: "Documented allergy: <allergy text>. <medicine> is a penicillin." or "… is a sulfa drug." (the allergy text containing "penicillin" or "amoxic" means penicillin; containing "sulfa" or "sulpha" means sulfa; a medicine of that class is blocked); (b) a duplicate: "<medicine> is already on the chart. Change the existing order instead of adding a second one." (a Pharmacist check or Active order of the same medicine). **Warnings** — the amber notice "Check before ordering" lists each as "<Moderate|Major>: <text>": a penicillin allergy with a cephalosporin medicine (Moderate) "Penicillin allergy: possible cross-reaction with cephalosporins. Confirm what reaction the patient had."; allergies not recorded (Moderate) "Allergies are not recorded. Ask the patient and record them before the first dose."; an interaction with a medicine on the chart (Pharmacist check or Active), each written "<text> (<other medicine> is on the chart).": heparin with aspirin (Major) "Bleeding risk: heparin with aspirin", heparin with an NSAID (Major) "Bleeding risk: heparin with an NSAID", aspirin with an NSAID (Moderate) "Stomach bleeding risk: aspirin with an NSAID". Warnings are shown with the select **Reason to continue (recorded in the audit trail)**, options "Choose a reason" then "Benefit outweighs the risk, monitoring in place", "Specialist advised this combination", "Allergy history checked with the patient: mild rash only, no anaphylaxis", "Recent coronary stent, cardiology advised", and the button now reads **Order with this reason**; pressing it with no reason shows "Choose a reason to continue, or change the medicine." (server: 409 `order-needs-reason` listing the warnings, then 400 for a reason not in the list). No finding: the order is placed.
6. Given an order is placed, then it is saved as Pharmacist check with the doctor, the time and the override reason (if any) and the warnings that applied; the entry "Ordered <medicine> <frequency in lower case>" plus " · override: <reason>" (kind order) is written; the toast is "<medicine> ordered. It goes to the pharmacist before the first dose."; the form clears.
7. Given **Stop** (doctor), then a Pharmacist check or Active order becomes Stopped, the entry "Stopped <medicine>" (kind stop) is written and the toast is "<medicine> stopped. Remaining doses are removed from the MAR." A Rejected or Stopped order cannot be stopped again (409). Orders are never deleted.
8. Given the Safety tab on Beds and admissions (a fourth tab after Admitted, and Emergency when present), then it is shown to OWNER and PHARMACIST (OQ-31-2) with the count of orders waiting for the pharmacist; it has the tile **Pharmacist checks** ("Orders waiting before the first dose", amber when above 0) and the panel **Pharmacist check** ("Every new order is checked before the first dose. The person who ordered cannot verify it."). Empty: "No orders waiting."
9. Given a waiting order, then its row shows "<medicine> · <frequency>", the small line "<patient name> · bed <code> · <allergy text> · ordered by <doctor> at <time>", every hard-stop message (red) and warning ("<Severity>: <text>", amber) found by running the checks again now, the line "Doctor's reason: <reason>" when there is an override, and the buttons **Reject** and **Verify**; Verify is disabled with the hint "Hard stop: reject this order" when the order is now blocked.
10. Given **Verify**, then the sheet "Pharmacist check · <medicine>" (subtitle "<patient name> · <UHID> · ordered by <doctor>") shows the identity bar, the same warnings with the doctor's reason or the green notice "No allergy, duplicate or interaction found.", **Verified by** (the signed-in verifier) and **PIN** (4 digits), and the buttons **Cancel** and **Verify order** (check icon). The server refuses: the orderer verifying their own order (403 `self-verification`, "The doctor who ordered this cannot verify it. Ask the pharmacist."); a PIN that is not exactly 4 digits (400, "Enter the 4-digit PIN."); a PIN that is not the verifier's (403 `wrong-pin`; mechanism in OQ-31-3, a format-only check is not acceptable for release); an order that is now blocked (409 `order-blocked`, "Hard stop on this order. Reject it."); an order that is not Pharmacist check (409 `order-state`). On success the order is Active with the verifier and time, the entry "Verified <medicine>" plus " · accepted doctor's reason" when there was an override (kind verify) is written, and the toast is "<medicine> verified by <verifier name>. It is now on the MAR."
11. Given **Reject**, then the sheet "Reject · <medicine>" (subtitle "<patient name> · ordered by <doctor>") has **Reason, sent to the doctor** with "Allergy conflict", "Interaction risk too high", "Wrong dose or frequency", "Duplicate therapy", and the buttons **Cancel** and **Reject order** (red). The order becomes Rejected with the reason, the entry "Rejected <medicine> · <reason>" (kind verify) is written, and the toast is "Order rejected · <reason>." (the mockup adds "<doctor> is notified"; no message is sent in this phase).
12. Given the starting medicine list, then it is: Tab. Paracetamol 650 mg (oral); Inj. Pantoprazole 40 mg (IV); Inj. Ondansetron 4 mg (IV); IV Normal saline 500 ml (IV); Inj. Ceftriaxone 1 g (IV, cephalosporin); Tab. Amoxicillin + clavulanate 625 mg (oral, penicillin); Inj. Piperacillin + tazobactam 4.5 g (IV, penicillin); Tab. Cotrimoxazole 960 mg (oral, sulfa); Tab. Aspirin 75 mg (oral, antiplatelet); Tab. Ibuprofen 400 mg (oral, NSAID); Tab. Metformin 500 mg (oral); Inj. Heparin 5,000 IU (SC, anticoagulant, high-alert "Anticoagulant"); Inj. Regular insulin, sliding scale (SC, insulin, high-alert "Insulin"); Inj. Potassium chloride 20 mmol in 500 ml (IV infusion, potassium, high-alert "Concentrated electrolyte"). The dose times of each frequency (used by SAH-32) are: Once daily 8:00 AM; Twice daily 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM; Three times daily 8:00 AM, 2:00 PM and 8:00 PM; 8-hourly 6:00 AM, 2:00 PM and 10:00 PM.
13. Given roles and tenants: the sheet — OWNER, DOCTOR, NURSE read (nurse: list only); ordering and stopping — doctors only; verifying and rejecting — PHARMACIST, and a DOCTOR or OWNER who did not place the order (OQ-31-2); anything else 403 with the message "Only a doctor can do this." for ordering and stopping. IPD Off 403 `module-off`; Read-only 409 `ipd-readonly` on writes; a discharged or not Admitted patient 409 `not-admitted` on orders. Another clinic's admission or order answers 404 (RLS).
14. Given privacy and audit, then clinical event entries hold medicine names and reasons from the fixed lists, never the patient's name, and cannot be edited or deleted. The audit events (IPD_MED_ORDERED, IPD_MED_ORDER_BLOCKED, IPD_MED_STOPPED, IPD_MED_VERIFIED, IPD_MED_REJECTED) hold ids, medicine key, frequency, status and override reason key only. The allergy text is clinical data: shown on screen only, never logged.
15. Given a slow or failed network on any button, then it is disabled while the request runs, a failed order is not shown as placed, and a retry never creates two orders (a second order of the same medicine is stopped by the duplicate rule).

## 3. Technical design
- New migration (db-engineer): reference tables without tenant (read-only for `sahaj_app`, like `opd.medicine`): `ipd.drug` (`key`, `name`, `route`, `classes text[]`, `high_alert text null`) and `ipd.drug_interaction` (`class_a`, `class_b`, `severity`, `text`), seeded with the content of AC 5 and AC 12 as a starter list. Tenant table `ipd.medicine_order` (FORCE RLS, composite FK to `ipd.admission`): `id`, `admission_id`, `drug_key`, `frequency_key` (check in od/bd/tds/q8), `status` check in PENDING/ACTIVE/REJECTED/STOPPED, `ordered_by`, `ordered_at`, `override_reason_key text null`, `warnings text[]`, `verified_by/at`, `rejected_reason_key`, `stopped_by/at`, `version`. Never deleted; update grant only on the status columns. Uses `ipd.clinical_event` (SAH-30) for the trail. Index `(tenant_id, admission_id)` and `(tenant_id, status)` for the pharmacist panel.
- The checks live in one domain class (`MedicineSafetyCheck`), used by the order, the verify and the pharmacist panel, so the three always agree. The allergy text comes from `patient.api` (the patient file field, SAH-9); ipd never reads patient tables.
- Whether the medicine list should be the shared `opd.medicine` list (no classes or high-alert flag) or an IPD list is OQ-31-1.
- The record-access log entry on opening the EMR uses the existing `patient.api` access-log port.
- Contract changes: new `EmrSheet`, `MedicineOrderInput`, `OrderCheckResult`, `PharmacistChecks`; menu: the Safety tab visibility rule.

## 4. API (`/api/v1/ipd`, contract `docs/api/ipd.yaml`)
| Method | Path | Role | Request | Response | Errors |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GET | `/admissions/{id}/emr` | OWNER, DOCTOR, NURSE | none | `EmrSheet` (identity, allergy, summary, orders, drug and frequency lists, canOrder) | 403, 404 |
| POST | `/admissions/{id}/medicine-orders` | doctors (OWNER, DOCTOR, VISITING_DOCTOR with a doctor profile) | `{drugKey, frequencyKey, overrideReasonKey?}` | `EmrSheet` (201) | 400, 403, 404, 409 (`order-blocked`, `order-needs-reason`, `not-admitted`, `ipd-readonly`) |
| POST | `/medicine-orders/{id}/stop` | doctors | none | `EmrSheet` | 403, 404, 409 |
| GET | `/pharmacist-checks` | OWNER, PHARMACIST | none | `PharmacistChecks` (waiting orders with the checks run now, count) | 403 |
| POST | `/medicine-orders/{id}/verify` | PHARMACIST; DOCTOR or OWNER who is not the orderer | `{pin}` | `PharmacistChecks` | 400, 403 (`self-verification`, `wrong-pin`), 404, 409 (`order-blocked`, `order-state`) |
| POST | `/medicine-orders/{id}/reject` | same as verify | `{reasonKey}` | `PharmacistChecks` | 400, 403, 404, 409 |

## 6. Out of scope, Open questions
Out of scope: giving doses and the MAR (SAH-32); the rest of the Safety tab (escalations, medicine round, emergency access, audit trail view); results; dose, route and duration entry; PRN and stat orders; medicine changes (stop and order again); order sets; pharmacy stock and dispensing (Pharmacy module is Later); drug-drug checks beyond the three rules; paediatric or renal dosing checks; notifications to the doctor.
Open questions (also in `docs/stories/IPD-open-questions.md`):
- OQ-31-1 (clinical, blocking for go-live): the 14 medicines, their classes, the high-alert flags, the three interactions and the four override reasons are demo content. Who approves the real list, and is it the shared medicine list (`opd.medicine`) extended with classes?
- OQ-31-2 (roles): the mockup shows the Pharmacist check panel only to the owner and lets the verifier be a pharmacist or a doctor. Proposed: OWNER and PHARMACIST see and use it; a doctor can verify only another doctor's order. The IPD menu does not yet include PHARMACIST; confirm.
- OQ-31-3 (security, blocking): what is the "PIN"? The mockup accepts any 4 digits. Proposed: a 4-digit PIN each verifier or nurse sets once in their profile (a screen not in the mockup), stored hashed, locked after repeated wrong tries; or re-entry of the sign-in password instead.
- OQ-31-4 (clinical): allergies are matched by keywords in free text ("penicillin", "amoxic", "sulfa", "sulpha"); other allergies and other spellings are not caught. Should allergies be structured in the patient file?
- OQ-31-5: where does the doctor see why the pharmacist rejected an order? Proposed: under the order in the EMR sheet.
- OQ-31-6: a patient whose allergy field is empty is "Allergies not recorded" and only warns. Should it be a hard stop until allergies are recorded?
- OQ-31-7: entry points EMR and Nursing are buttons on the admission sheet until the card exists.
- OQ-31-8: VISITING_DOCTOR can order in the mockup (EMR action) but has no access to the admission sheet in SAH-23; confirm whether they get IPD access.
