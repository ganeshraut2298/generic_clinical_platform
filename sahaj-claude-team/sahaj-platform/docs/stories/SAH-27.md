# SAH-27 · Discharge, final bill and Discharge setup (IPD, part 6)
Mockup screen: Transfer, Discharge, Case paper (card actions) — the patient sheet action "Start discharge" (`openIp`, `ipdis`), the Discharge sheet (`openDis`, `ipdisseg`, `ipdisback`, `ipdisok`), the tile "Going home today", and Clinic setup → Discharge (`cfgIpDis`, key `ipdis`)
Module: ipd (creates the follow-up through the `opd.api` port; reads patient and team through `patient.api` and `platform.api`)

## 1. Story
As the doctor, front desk or nurse of a hospital, I want to discharge a patient in steps (doctor signs the summary, the final bill is settled, the bed is released) and have the follow-up planned, so that no patient leaves with an unsigned summary or an unsettled bill, and the bed is ready for cleaning at once.

Scope note: this story discharges the patient and settles the money. Two things in the mockup depend on services that are not in phase 1: the discharge summary and final bill are sent on WhatsApp, and the death summary is printed. Here the summary is the on-screen preview plus the recorded type, template and signature (no document is generated), the "Print interim bill" button stays disabled, and the WhatsApp parts of the notices are replaced by "not sent yet" texts. The approval chain steps "Billing clearance" and "Nursing handover" are shown, but the mockup has one action that completes them together, so they are not separate sign-offs (OQ-27-2). The mockup has no screen listing discharged patients: only the counter "Going home today" shows them, so there is no discharged list screen (OQ-27-6). How a patient becomes "Going home today" before discharge is not in the mockup (OQ-27-5); an assumption is built and marked.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given IPD is Trial or On and an Admitted patient, then the admission sheet (SAH-23/25) footer gets the button **Start discharge** (door icon, primary) for OWNER, FRONT_DESK, NURSE and DOCTOR; in Read-only state it is not shown. It opens the sheet titled "Discharge <patient name>" with the subtitle "<IP number> · bed <code> · day N" and the buttons **Back** (returns to the admission sheet) and the primary discharge button of AC 6. Opening the sheet changes nothing except one row in the patient record-access log (the sheet shows the patient name and diagnosis; amended 5 Nov after security review M3).
2. Given the sheet, then it starts with a numbered step list. With **Approval chain** on (default): "Doctor signs the summary", "Billing clearance", "Nursing handover"; with it off: "Summary signed", "Final bill". A signed summary shows the first step as done (check icon) and the next step as current; the other steps show their number. Billing clearance, Nursing handover and Final bill are completed together by the discharge button (AC 6); this story does not add separate sign-offs for them.
3. Given the sheet, then **Discharge type** is a choice of "Normal" (default), "On request (LAMA)", "Referred", "Death" (the types listed on the Discharge setup page); **Summary template** is a select of "General medicine", "Surgical", "Maternity", "Day-care". The default template is the one chosen in Discharge setup; when none is chosen: "Maternity" for a Maternity admission, "Day-care" for a Day-care admission, "Surgical" for an admission with a package (SAH-29), otherwise "General medicine".
4. Given the sheet, then a summary preview shows the rows **Diagnosis** (the reason for admission), **Stay** ("N days · bed <code> · <doctor name> · <specialty>", "1 day" when N is 1; N = the bill days of SAH-25), **Treatment** ("As recorded in the case sheet and nursing notes", fixed text) and **Follow-up** ("OPD review on 31 Oct 2026 with <doctor short name>", or "—" when the type is Death). Under it the checkbox "Summary checked and signed by <admitting doctor's name>".
5. Given the checkbox, then only DOCTOR and OWNER can tick it (other roles see it disabled; OQ-27-1). Ticking records the signature (who, when, the chosen type and template) at once; it is a clinical sign-off and cannot be undone. Changing the type or the template afterwards makes the signature not apply: the box shows unticked and the doctor signs again for the new choice. Server: 403 for other roles.
6. Given the sheet, then the block **Final bill** shows "Total charges" (the running bill of SAH-25/26/29 as it is now), "Deposits received" (with a minus sign) and a last row "To collect now" (balance > 0), "To refund" (balance < 0) or "Settled" (0) with the amount without sign. When the balance is not 0, **Collect by** / **Refund by** offers UPI (default), Cash, Card. The primary button reads "Collect ₹X and discharge", "Refund ₹X and discharge" or "Discharge"; it is disabled with the hint "The doctor signs the summary first" until the summary is signed for the chosen type and template. Under the block a notice: "The discharge summary and final bill are not sent yet (WhatsApp is not in this phase). An OPD follow-up is added for 31 Oct 2026." and for Death: "The death summary cannot be printed in this phase. No WhatsApp messages are sent. No follow-up is added." (the mockup says "go on WhatsApp" and "is printed for the family").
7. Given the discharge button, then the server checks in this order: the admission exists (404) and is Admitted (409 `not-admitted`); IPD is not read-only (409 `ipd-readonly`); the summary is signed for the sent type and template (409 `summary-not-signed`, "The doctor signs the summary first"); the bill the user saw is still the bill: the sent expected total equals the server's total (409 `bill-changed`, "The bill changed. Review the final bill and try again."); a refund is only allowed to the OWNER (403 `refund-needs-owner`, "Only the owner can refund a deposit."; OQ-27-3). The amount to collect or refund is always computed by the server, never taken from the request.
8. Given a valid discharge, then in one transaction: the final bill is frozen (every line with name, quantity, unit and amount, the total, the deposits and the balance) so that later rate changes in Clinic setup never change it; the settlement is recorded (collected, refunded or settled, with the mode and who); the admission becomes DISCHARGED with the discharge time (IST); the bed becomes Cleaning; the discharge record keeps type, template, who signed and when, who discharged and when; and, unless the type is Death, an OPD follow-up is created for the patient with the admitting doctor, due on the discharge day + the follow-up days of Discharge setup (default 7), with the reason "Review after discharge · <reason for admission>" cut to 200 characters. The follow-up appears in the Follow-ups list (SAH-16) and its fee follows the clinic's follow-up fee rule. Toast: "<patient name> discharged (normal) · ₹X collected by UPI · bed 101-A sent for cleaning · follow-up on 31 Oct 2026", with "₹X refunded by Cash" or "bill settled" in place of the collection. The type is written in lower case as in the mockup (for example "discharged (on request (lama))", "discharged (death)"), and for Death the follow-up part is left out.
9. Given a repeated tap or two users discharging the same patient, then only the first succeeds; the second gets 409 `not-admitted` and nothing is collected twice.
10. Given the Beds and admissions page, then a fourth tile **Going home today** shows a number and the text "N discharged · M planned": N = patients discharged today (IST date), M = admitted patients flagged to go home today. Assumption to confirm (OQ-27-5): a patient is flagged when the doctor has signed the summary today and the patient is not yet discharged. A flagged patient shows the teal pill "Going home today" in the Status column INSTEAD of the Insurance / Corporate / Deposit pills, and the flag "Going home today" on the bed tile INSTEAD of "Deposit low" (the mockup is exclusive: amended 5 Nov after code review 4; the patient settles the bill at discharge). The discharged patient leaves the Admitted list and the count of the Admitted tab.
11. Given the Admitted list, the board and the tiles after a discharge, then the bed shows as Cleaning (SAH-24: "Cleaned · ready"), the beds counters update, and the deposit alert counts no longer include the patient.
12. Given Clinic setup → **Discharge** (key `ipdis`, visible when IPD is Trial/On/Read-only), then the panel **Discharge** has: **Summary template** (hint "The doctor fills and signs it before the final bill") a select "Choose a template" + the four templates; **Approval chain** (hint "Doctor signs → billing clearance → nursing handover") a switch, default on; **Follow-up after discharge** (hint "Adds an OPD follow-up. The fee follows your follow-up rule (free within 6 days)." or "(charged within 6 days)", from the OPD fee settings) a stepper "7 days", 1 to 30, step 1; and a read-only line **Discharge types** with the four types as chips. Each change is saved at once and audited; only the OWNER can change them, other roles see them read-only; 409 `ipd-readonly` on change in Read-only state.
13. Given a discharged admission, then its detail can still be read (OWNER, FRONT_DESK, NURSE, DOCTOR) with the frozen final bill, discharge type, signature and settlement; nothing on it can be changed; a deposit, charge, transfer or second discharge on it answers 409 `not-admitted`. In Read-only state discharged records stay readable.
14. Given roles and tenants: opening the sheet — OWNER, FRONT_DESK, NURSE, DOCTOR; signing — DOCTOR, OWNER; discharging with collection or no balance — OWNER, FRONT_DESK; refund — OWNER; setup — OWNER (OQ-27-1/3 hold the questions). Before inserting a sign-off the server re-reads the caller's membership (active DOCTOR, or OWNER; OQ-27-1 stays open about an OWNER without a doctor profile); the discharge button is disabled with the hint "Front desk or the owner completes the discharge" for roles that cannot complete (amended 5 Nov). All writes are audited (IPD_DISCHARGE_SIGNED with admission id, type, template; IPD_DISCHARGED with admission id, IP number, type, total, balance, mode, bed code; IPD_DISCHARGE_SETTINGS_UPDATED) and never contain the patient's name, mobile or the reason text. Another clinic's admission answers 404 (RLS). Module Off answers 403 `module-off`. Money is integer paise.
15. Given a slow or failed network on the discharge button, then it is disabled while the request runs; if the answer is lost the user reopens the admission: a discharged patient shows as discharged and nothing is collected again.

## 3. Technical design
- New migration (db-engineer): `ipd.config` gets `discharge_template_key text null` (check in the four template keys), `discharge_chain boolean not null default true`, `followup_days smallint not null default 7 check (between 1 and 30)`. New tables, all with `tenant_id` and FORCE RLS, composite FK to `ipd.admission`:
  - `ipd.discharge_signoff`: `id`, `admission_id`, `discharge_type` (check in normal/lama/referred/death), `template_key` (check in general/surgical/maternity/daycare), `signed_by`, `signed_at`. Append-only. The signature applies to a discharge when the latest sign-off of the admission has the same type and template.
  - `ipd.discharge`: `id`, `admission_id` (unique per tenant), `discharge_type`, `template_key`, `signed_by`, `signed_at`, `followup_days`, `followup_due date null` (null for Death), `completed_by`, `completed_at`. Insert only.
  - `ipd.final_bill`: `id`, `admission_id` (unique per tenant), `total_paise`, `deposits_paise`, `balance_paise` (positive collected, negative refunded, 0 settled), `settle_mode text null` (Cash, UPI, Card), `created_by`, `created_at`; and `ipd.final_bill_line`: `id`, `final_bill_id`, `line_no`, `label`, `qty`, `unit_paise`, `amount_paise`. Both append-only (select + insert; update/delete/truncate denied by trigger).
  - `ipd.admission` already has `status` and `discharged_at` with an update grant (SAH-23).
- The discharge transaction takes the IPD tenant advisory lock, locks the admission and the bed `for update`, recomputes the bill with `RunningBill`, compares it with the expected total, then writes everything. The bed goes to `clean`.
- Cross-module: the follow-up is created through a new port in `opd.api` (for example `FollowUpPort`), not by writing opd tables. `opd.follow_up` currently requires `source_visit_id` (unique, not null): the architect and db-engineer decide how a follow-up without a visit is stored (for example a nullable visit and a source admission id).
- Money records: the settlement is stored in `ipd` only. Whether it must also appear in the Billing counter, day close and revenue (SAH-19/20) is OQ-27-4; if yes, a port in `billing.api` is added by the architect.
- Contract changes: `AdmissionDetail` gains `discharge` and, once discharged, the frozen `finalBill`; `AdmissionList` gains `dischargedToday` and `goingHomeToday`, `AdmissionRow` gains `goingHomeToday`; `BoardBed` gains `goingHomeToday`; new `DischargeSheet`, `DischargeSettings`.

## 6. Out of scope, Open questions
Out of scope: generating, printing or sending the discharge summary or final bill (documents service, WhatsApp), the death certificate, a discharged patients list or search, editing the summary text (the mockup preview is fixed text), separate billing-clearance and nursing-handover sign-offs, discharge reversal, discharge against an unpaid bill with approval, insurance claim settlement, bed reservation after discharge, case paper printing, the card action "Update stage" ("Discharge initiated" stage).
Open questions (also in `docs/stories/IPD-open-questions.md`):
- OQ-27-1 (roles, legal): who may sign the discharge summary (any DOCTOR, the admitting doctor only, a visiting doctor) and who may complete a discharge? The mockup's default matrix lets doctors and visiting doctors start a discharge and nobody else, while its sheet lets anyone finish it. Proposed: sign DOCTOR/OWNER; complete OWNER/FRONT_DESK.
- OQ-27-2: are "Billing clearance" and "Nursing handover" real sign-offs by the billing and nursing staff, or only labels? Proposed: labels (as the mockup).
- OQ-27-3 (money): who may refund the unused deposit? The mockup tip says "the owner or the accounts person". Proposed: OWNER only (BILLING role has no IPD access yet).
- OQ-27-4 (billing, blocking for go-live): must the final bill, balance collected and refund appear in the Billing counter, day close and revenue lists? Today deposits do not. Proposed: ask billing owner; build in a later billing-link story.
- OQ-27-5: how does a patient become "Going home today" before discharge (the mockup only seeds it)? Assumption built: summary signed today.
- OQ-27-6: where do staff see discharged patients, their summaries and final bills (the mockup says they stay viewable 90 days in Read-only)? No screen exists in the mockup.
- OQ-27-7 (clinical, legal): "On request (LAMA)" and "Death" need extra records in practice (LAMA consent, cause of death, medico-legal case, certificate). None is in the mockup. Should a deceased patient be marked in the patient file so reminders and follow-ups stop?
- OQ-27-8: which doctor gets the follow-up (the mockup uses a fixed doctor), and what if the admitting doctor is inactive or a visiting doctor?
- OQ-27-9: Insurance and Corporate patients: the mockup collects "total − deposit" from the family like a Self patient. What should the final bill show for the insurer's share?
- OQ-27-10: should the follow-up reason include the diagnosis ("Review after discharge · <reason>"), since the Follow-ups list is seen by front desk, who cannot see clinical details?
